# Analysis-repository handoff

The main repository supplies per-recording, versioned measurements and their
provenance. The analysis repository owns cohort aggregation, state and
phenotype comparisons, retrospective audits, balancing, and biological or
clinical claims.

Carry forward the coordinate contract, capability identifiers, support counts,
validity status, and Q time semantics. Do not combine layers with different
coordinate anchoring or compare fractions with different denominators as
equivalent evidence.
