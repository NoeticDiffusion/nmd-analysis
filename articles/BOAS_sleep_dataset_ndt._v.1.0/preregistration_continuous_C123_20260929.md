## 1. Scope and claims

This protocol operationalizes finite-horizon, observed-chart prediction questions. It does not test the unobserved latent future law in its unrestricted form and cannot establish or refute NDT as a whole. The frozen stage-sampled v5 analysis remains untouched. The ordinary MNDM output remains the canonical descriptive surface; any prediction-safe surface is a separately named derivative and must not silently replace canonical MNPS `[m,d,e]` or 9D values.

The order is C1 and an observational stage-context C3 pilot first. C2 is explicitly **deferred**: no current continuous HDF5 contains a qualified accessibility/future-law predictor. A computed Jacobian, FTR field, stage contrast, or deterministic transformation of baseline predictors will not be relabelled as such a predictor.

## 2. Common measurement and eligibility rules

- Keep all repeated recordings linked to biological `pid`; no participant may occur in more than one outer fold. All fitting of feature transforms, chart anchors, nuisance transforms, model parameters and thresholds is confined to outer-training participants. Use the MNDM fold-safe path where its source-support contract passes.
- Freeze chart generation to the unchanged MNDM measurement definitions and versioned config: 3D `[m,d,e]` from the inherited fixed `mnps_projection.v1_mapping`, and 9D from `mnps_9d.definition_version=2.0`. Per-feature standardization is exactly: default `[robust_z, clip]`; `eeg_delta`, `eeg_theta`, `eeg_alpha`, `eeg_beta`, `eeg_gamma` use `[log10, robust_z, clip]`; `eeg_alpha_theta`, `eeg_beta_alpha`, `eeg_sample_entropy`, `eeg_permutation_entropy`, `eeg_spectral_entropy`, `eeg_hjorth_complexity`, and `eeg_hjorth_mobility` use `[robust_z, clip]`; all other mapped features use the declared default. Fit the MNDM fold-safe external feature anchor only on training PIDs (`scale_method=iqr`, `min_subjects=20`, `subject_anchored=false`, `cohort_anchored=true`). Do not fit or apply ComBat or any other whole-recording/cohort transform. Bind each fold's resolved config, MNDM code and anchor artifact by hash. A fold whose required feature anchors do not qualify is unavailable, not re-fit on apply PIDs. These settings must be checked against the resolved analysis config before any C1/C3 score is generated. The two-recording measurement-only pilot does not fit a chart anchor or test chart coordinates.
- Keep canonical 3D `[m,d,e]` as the primary chart and 9D as a separate sensitivity representation. Do not pool dimensions or treat 9D components as independent replications. For the primary C1/C3 population, require `support_signature/v1/coordinates/e_m/source=fallback` and `fallback_feature=ppg_rate_bpm` in every included recording. This is the largest single exact `e_m` fallback stratum in the pre-outcome support audit (100/128 recordings); it is a computationally homogeneous input stratum, not evidence that PPG is biologically equivalent to EEG or a validated measure of latent energy. Exclude the EOG-blink-rate and EEG-high-frequency fallback strata from the pooled primary analysis; report their availability separately and do not combine their outcomes. Use identical recording/PID eligibility for the 9D sensitivity.
- In the primary PPG-fallback stratum, define `ppg_rate_bpm` using MNDM's opt-in `window_local_present_only_v1` estimator only: require exactly one source channel whose name matches the fixed pulse/PPG name rule (`ppg|pleth|pulse|photopleth`), and record both its name and MNE channel type after dataset typing (the BOAS `PSG_PULSE` channel is `misc`, not MNE `bio`). For each current 30-s raw pulse-channel window, do not filter the PPG stream; subtract that window's median, detect peaks in its absolute centered signal with minimum distance 0.35 s and prominence `max(1e-6, 0.75 * (median(abs(centered_window)) + 1e-8))`, and calculate `60 / mean(interpeak_interval_seconds)` from peaks in that same window. Set the PPG QC flag only when the rate is finite and at least two peaks are detected; otherwise retain missingness. Record the selection rule, channel name/type, algorithm version, window bounds, feature QC and provenance. This is a present-window measurement, not a future predictor fitted over the recording. The legacy stream-wide zero-phase PPG filter/global threshold is prohibited on the prediction-safe surface.
- Forecast origin is the end of an eligible 30-second measurement window. Candidate horizons are fixed row lags `h1`, `h2`, `h4` (nominally 30, 60, 120 seconds). `h1` is primary; `h2` and `h4` are secondary. Report actual timestamp differences. A candidate is eligible only when the origin, every intervening row and target are finite and coordinate-QC valid, in the same recording and verified contiguous segment, with finite causal conservative raw-sample support and no rejected interval crossing the dependency bounds. Any feature whose dependency interval crosses a source-quality exclusion is unavailable, including neighboring rows affected by the causal filter support.
- Do not interpolate, bridge gaps, or substitute a failed/missing value with zero. Preserve unavailable rows and their reasons. Require at least 20 independent eligible PIDs for participant-level interval summaries; this is a reporting/feasibility floor, not a power guarantee. Below it, report `INCONCLUSIVE` for that cell.
- Use deterministic five-fold outer `GroupKFold` by `pid`. For model comparisons, fit and score both models on exactly the same eligible origin-target rows. At each row, squared error is averaged across output dimensions; average rows within recording, recordings equally within PID, then PIDs equally for the cohort summary. Use a participant-cluster bootstrap (10,000 draws, seed `20260929`) for descriptive 95% intervals. No epoch-level inferential sample size, confirmatory p-value, success threshold, or equivalence claim is defined.

## 3. C1 — finite-lag chart predictive sufficiency

**Operational question:** does adding recent, observed chart history improve held-out prediction of the future chart beyond the current chart alone?

- Primary endpoint: 3D target at `h1`; fixed secondary endpoints are 3D `h2`/`h4` and the separate 9D representation at all three horizons.
- Compare two fixed multi-output ridge conditional-mean predictors: `M0: x[t] -> x[t+h]`; `M1: (x[t], x[t-1], x[t-2]) -> x[t+h]`. History rows must be past-only and contiguous with `t`. Fit robust predictor scaling on outer-training PIDs only. Use scikit-learn `Ridge(alpha=1.0, fit_intercept=true)` after that scaling; do not tune alpha against the held-out fold.
- For each outer fold and representation, form participant-balanced training summaries: within each training PID, take each recording's coordinate-wise median over eligible chart rows, then the PID's coordinate-wise median over its recordings. For each chart coordinate, define center as the median and scale as the IQR of those finite training-PID summaries; a zero/non-finite scale makes that coordinate/fold unavailable. Apply the same training-only affine transform `(x-center)/scale` to every current/history predictor block and every future target coordinate in that fold. Fit one coordinate-wise transform per representation/fold and reuse that exact transform for each M0/M1 lag slice and the target; do not fit a second scaler per model, lag or matrix column. This gives both models identical coordinate units despite different matrix widths and makes held-out loss comparable across folds in outer-training-IQR units. The fixed ridge penalty is `alpha=1.0` on these scaled predictors; no model or feature selection is performed.
- The evaluation set is the intersection of valid origin-target rows for which both `M0` and the two-row-history `M1` have all required present/past inputs. Primary score is the participant-averaged reduction in held-out mean squared error, `MSE(M0) - MSE(M1)`; positive values mean the specified history-augmented predictor was more accurate. Publish every fixed horizon/representation cell and its participant support.
- This tests only conditional-mean predictability under the stated ridge model, not equality of full future distributions. No gain constrains only this chart, history length, model class and horizons; it does not refute existential or unmeasured-state C1.
- A within-segment time-shifted history negative control is reported where segments are long enough; its shift is at least five rows and is fixed before scoring. It is diagnostic, not a pass/fail gate.

## 4. C3 — observed sleep-stage context at matched current state

**Primary context for this version:** human-scored N2 versus Wake at the forecast origin. Stage is an observational context, not an intervention, a measure of experience, or evidence of causal control. Resolve stage from the prespecified human-scored source intervals at `t_end` using half-open intervals `[onset, onset+duration)`; if the source has conflicting overlapping labels or no unique label at the origin, exclude that origin. Also require the complete feature window `[t_start,t_end)` to be covered by that same source-stage label, so transition-straddling windows are not used. If common support fails, the cell is `INCONCLUSIVE`; do not extrapolate.

- Primary endpoint: within-participant difference in future 3D chart displacement, `x[t+h1] - x[t]`, between N2 and Wake origins matched on the current 3D chart and recording-relative time-of-night. Fixed secondary horizons are `h2`/`h4`; 9D is a separate sensitivity analysis.
- Match N2 and Wake origins one-to-one without replacement within the same held-out PID **and recording**; matches may not cross nights/recordings. The context cells used to construct the training caliper are exactly `PID × recording × stage context` (N2 or Wake); nearest-distinct-origin distances are computed only within each such cell. Distance is Euclidean after each chart coordinate and recording-relative time-of-night have been centered/scaled using outer-training PIDs only. Take the median within each training context cell, then give each PID equal weight by averaging its available recording/context-cell medians. The maximum accepted cross-context match distance is the 95th percentile of these participant-weighted medians in the outer-training partition. Future coordinates, future stages and outcome values cannot enter matching, scale fitting, or the distance threshold.
- The one-to-one matching algorithm is deterministic greedy closest-edge matching: within each PID-recording, enumerate all eligible N2-Wake edges at or below the frozen caliper; sort by `(distance, N2 t_end, Wake t_end, N2 epoch_id, Wake epoch_id)` ascending; accept an edge only if neither origin has already been used. This stable tie-break is applied identically in every fold and replay.
- For each PID-recording and horizon, calculate the mean paired displacement difference; average recordings equally within PID, then report the distribution across PIDs, all three chart-axis summaries, matched-pair counts, distance/balance diagnostics and support exclusions. Participant-cluster intervals use the common bootstrap rule above. The overlap gate requires at least 20 independent PIDs with at least 10 accepted pairs per PID at `h1`, and absolute standardized mean differences no greater than 0.10 for each matched current-chart coordinate and time-of-night, using the training-fold scaling. If not met, report `INCONCLUSIVE`, not a pooled estimate. No pooled epoch-level test is run.
- At each horizon and axis, the C3 point estimand is the arithmetic mean of the eligible PID-level paired displacement differences in outer-training-IQR units, with each PID weighted equally after equal-weight averaging over that PID's eligible recordings. Its descriptive 95% interval is the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of 10,000 participant-cluster bootstrap means, sampling PIDs with replacement using seed `20260929`. Matching-distance scaling uses the training-PID median as center and training-PID IQR as scale for each current-chart coordinate and recording-relative time; each training PID contributes one coordinate-wise median per variable, irrespective of its row count. The same per-coordinate training IQR transform used for C1 is applied to C3 chart predictors and displacements. If any required IQR is zero/non-finite, that fold's matching is unavailable. For each matching variable, compute the paired mean difference within each PID-recording, average recording-level differences equally within PID, then average PID values equally; define this as `SMD_IQR`, an IQR-standardized paired-mean diagnostic rather than a pooled-SD SMD. Its absolute value must be no greater than 0.10 for each current-chart coordinate and time-of-night. A zero/non-finite training-IQR scale fails the gate.
- A fixed time-shifted-stage negative control circularly shifts stage labels within each recording/contiguous segment by at least eight rows where feasible, preserving segment boundaries; report feasibility and results without interpreting the control as causal identification.
- This is a C3-motivated observational first-moment proxy only. It does not establish a full conditional-future-law difference or causal C3. Power/arousal and other available confounds must be shown in the audit and discussed; do not claim they are eliminated by matching on chart and time.

The default context is N2 versus Wake, consistent with the handover's initial state-matched example. A spindle-versus-non-spindle N2 test is not part of this version and would require a dated amendment and event-support audit.
