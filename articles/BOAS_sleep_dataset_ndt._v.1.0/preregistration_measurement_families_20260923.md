## 4. Primary and secondary analyses from this run

### A1. Local Jacobian abscissae by recorded stage

The two **primary descriptive endpoints** are 3D `spectral_abscissa` and `numerical_abscissa` in the fixed PPG-fallback stratum, for **N2 minus Wake**. For each recording, take the median of each endpoint among eligible center windows labelled N2 and Wake separately, then subtract Wake from N2 only if both stages meet the five-center rule in that recording. For a person with multiple eligible recordings in that stratum, take the median of their within-recording contrasts. The estimand is the median of these contrasts among **eligible people in the PPG stratum**, not all 100 people. Epochs, overlapping neighbourhoods and multiple nights are not independent population units.

Report the eligible-person count per endpoint, recording and center counts at each reduction, median participant contrast, participant Q25/Q75, and positive/negative/zero counts. If at least 20 people are eligible, add a 95% percentile interval for the eligible-person median from 10,000 person-cluster bootstrap resamples, seed `20260923`; resample people with all their eligible recordings and recompute the median. If fewer than 20 people are eligible, the interval is unavailable. Because chart scaling/reference fitting is recording-local, the across-person median describes recording-relative algorithmic contrasts, not a calibrated physical effect. No p-value, success threshold, equivalence margin or directional confirmatory decision is registered because the same cohort and direction have been inspected before.

Fixed secondary cells use REM minus Wake and N3 minus Wake for those two 3D endpoints; `reactivity_gap` is reported for all three stage pairs as a derived diagnostic. Repeat all these cells for 9D as a separate representation, with absent 9D recordings kept null. Produce the same tables separately for EOG and EEG-high-frequency fallback strata, without pooling source types or selecting a preferred stratum by outcome. In a distinct inventory table, retain finite/status counts and within-recording stage medians for the other fixed stored Jacobian diagnostics `trace`, `frobenius_norm`, `rotation_norm`, `henrici_departure` and `spectral_radius`, plus counts of recorded regime flags; these have no registered participant contrast or selected positive finding. N1 has no selected rows and receives no inferred contrast. Publish every prespecified cell even when it is unavailable.

As a boundary sensitivity, retain a center only when the immediately preceding and following stored epochs have the same known stage and touch its window bounds within `1e-6 s`; recording ends, gaps and unknown labels fail this rule. Recompute the fixed contrasts, reporting support loss including empty cells. This does **not** certify filter support, full-neighbourhood stage purity or a stage-specific Jacobian. Report the fraction of eligible alpha/omega rows with known 3D out-of-sample fit diagnostics as a diagnostic; do not substitute these diagnostics for the unexecuted producer fit-fidelity gate.
