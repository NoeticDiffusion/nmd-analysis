## 1. Question, scope, and prior knowledge

The feasible question for this *specific derived run* is whether the canonical subject-anchored MNPS coordinates show **descriptive associations** with human-scored sleep stage in the selected 30-second epochs. A second, explicit aim is to determine whether this run satisfies the measurement requirements for testing the three core commitments in [*Noetic Diffusion Theory - Neural State, Dynamical Accessibility, and the Geometry of Possible Futures*](<../NoeticDiffusionTheory_v2_cognitive_neurodynamics/Noetic Diffusion Theory - Neural State, Dynamical Accessibility, and the Geometry of Possible Futures.typ>), especially its “Three core commitments” and nested predictive and matched-state designs (lines 460–515 in the manuscript as inspected on 2026-09-23; source-file SHA-256 `CA880298C3CC720C2DF8ED05D311A5E5EA21956D3A1BA00B5EB1ED272034834B`).

The BOAS source is OpenNeuro ds005555 version 1.1.1 according to the run manifest (DOI `10.18112/openneuro.ds005555.v1.1.1`). The 2026-09-18 ingest uses stage-stratified blocks, a 30-second epoch length and step, target sampling of 20 minutes each for Wake, N2, N3 and REM, configured five-minute blocks, and seed 42. The HDF5 does not expose original sampling-block IDs; only observed contiguous runs can be reconstructed. It is **not** a continuous full-night representation. Its `run_manifest.json` reports 128 HDF5 files, subject-anchored 3D and 9D coordinates, no cohort-anchored coordinates, and no regional outputs. A read-only structural audit for this plan found 128 unique `sub-*` recording IDs but 100 distinct person-level `pid` values in `/participant/row_json`, 16,629 selected windows, 1,469 gaps and 15,032 exactly contiguous pairs between stored windows, and stage counts Wake 4,695, N2 5,118, N3 1,854, REM 4,962; N1 was not selected. For example, `sub-1` and `sub-2` share `pid=89`. These are inventory facts, not analysis outcomes. The manifest records ingest commit `58cf913bd817348f44d8713ea3f2aa9c1e98559f` and resolved-config digest `3816549ff89974c751919adcbc53d41bf773579c097549ea10a20633dba67d84`.

Prior work on the *same underlying cohort* includes a full-night descriptive sleep analysis in `articles/ndt_v3_sleep/sciencelead/13_sleep_full_cohort_results.md`, including a known Wake–N2 diffusion-summary association. That result informed the choice of N2–Wake as the leading stage contrast here, although the primary endpoints below are different coordinates. The present team has inspected that report, the current run's manifest and schema, and support/status fields before writing this plan. No partition of these same participants will be represented as an untouched external replication. Any numerical result from this run will be reported as a **registered exploratory reanalysis of existing data**, with the extent of prior exposure stated in the eventual report. A future independent recording set would be needed for a clean confirmatory replication.

## 2. Theory-to-measurement map and falsification boundary

| Commitment | Exact empirical issue | Status for this run | What an interpretable adverse result would require |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **C1: reduced-state insufficiency** | Current reduced chart position need not determine the latent future law. | **Not directly testable.** The latent law is unobserved. A finite-lag *chart sufficiency* test could use observed exact-dt contiguous sequences or a continuous recording after their full temporal support and prediction design are qualified in a separate protocol. | If predefined history adds no held-out predictive information beyond present chart state across adequately powered regimes and horizons, it would constrain insufficiency **for that chart and those horizons**, not refute the existential latent claim. |
| **C2: non-redundant accessibility** | A prespecified future-law functional must add reproducible held-out information beyond chart state plus conventional static, spectral, nuisance, and available-control covariates. | **Not testable from the stored family outputs.** The run has no qualified, stage-specific future-law functional, and its trajectories are sampled with gaps. Static stage separation or a computed drift summary is not a C2 test. | No gain, or a gain erased by capacity-matched baselines and null controls, in independently held-out participants under a separately qualified exact-dt or continuous-data protocol would count against the **specified** C2 operationalization. One null dataset cannot refute “in at least some distinct neural regimes.” |
| **C3: conditional control** | At matched current chart state, predefined context, history, or physiology must change a nominated feature of the future law beyond standard covariates. | **Not testable as control or causation in this analysis.** Sleep stage could serve as an observational context in a separately registered matched-state test using observed exact-dt contiguous pairs, but the stored family outputs lack qualified future laws and verified full signal support. Stage is neither an intervention nor a report of experience. | Adequately supported, state-matched stage-specific future laws showing no prespecified difference after power, arousal, timing and other confound controls would constrain that **sleep-stage C3 test**, not all possible controls. An observational difference alone would not establish causal control. |

This registration therefore makes **no C1, C2, or C3 success or falsification claim** from the primary analysis below. The theory's C2 design requires the comparison `M1: Y ~ f(x,s)` versus `M2: Y ~ f(x,s,F[K_h])` with training-fold estimation of `F[K_h]` and matched model capacity. Its C3 design requires future-law comparison at matched `x`, plus appropriate phase-shuffle, time-shift, wrong-event and power-matched nulls where relevant. Sleep stages cannot substitute for an experience/no-experience contrast.

## 3. Frozen measurements and audit gates

**Gate A — source identity and support, before contrasts.** Enumerate exactly the HDF5 files in the input run; capture SHA-256 for each file, `run_manifest.json`, the ingest config, analysis code, and the `sub-*` to person-level `pid` mapping. Verify unique recording keys, 30-second stored bounds, chronological order, no overlaps, the stage codebook, and equal per-epoch array lengths **within each file**; allow and report different row counts across recordings. Report all missing, duplicate, unknown, nonfinite and invalid rows by recording and stage. Define a stored sampling gap only between consecutive rows of one recording when `next window_start > current window_end + 1e-6 s`; its duration is `next window_start - current window_end`. Recording boundaries are not gaps. Report the count and duration distribution; this describes omitted sampled time, not raw-signal or filter-support quality. Define an **observed same-stage run** as consecutive stored rows of the same known stage for which each next start touches the prior end within `1e-6 s`. Such runs may merge originally distinct selected blocks; do not label them recovered five-minute sampling blocks. Use `labels/stage` (human-score-derived `stage_hum` according to run metadata), with 0=Wake, 1=N1, 2=N2, 3=N3, 4=REM. Unknown or other codes remain explicit unknowns. The raw event TSV is outside this delivered run; if source annotations are available, audit their mapping separately and record their own hashes. A failed identity or label audit stops contrasts until resolved in a dated amendment.

**Gate B — coordinate eligibility.** The representation is the existing canonical subject-anchored MNPS `[m,d,e]` vector at `/coords_3d_subject_anchored/values`, with no change to its projection, scaling, axes, missingness or producer semantics. A row is eligible only if its entire vector is finite and the masks `/qc/windows/coverage_ok`, `/qc/windows/retained_after_qc`, `/qc/windows/geometry_valid`, and `/qc/windows/mnps_3d_valid` are explicitly true. Missing or unrecorded masks fail the gate. For descriptive 9D sensitivity, require the complete canonical ordered vector at `/coords_9d_subject_anchored/values`, the explicitly true `/qc/windows/coords_9d_valid` mask, and the same coverage/retention conditions. No invalid or unavailable value becomes zero. Report each exclusion reason separately as well as the joint eligible count. Inspect `/support_signature/v1` and anchoring provenance; if the coordinate contract is inconsistent within an intended comparison, do not pool it. In this run `/support_signature/v1/coordinates/e_m` has `semantic_equivalence=false` in all 128 recordings and uses fallback feature `ppg_rate_bpm` in 99, `eog_blink_rate` in 26, and `eeg_highfreq_power_30_45` in three. The 3D `e` axis has source `mixed` in all files. Record fallback type per recording; neither `e` nor 9D `e_m` is treated as one pooled biological construct across these different sources.

**Gate C — dynamic qualification.** A computed field is not necessarily a valid or scientifically qualified endpoint. All 128 inspected files mark diffusion, one-step, amplification, history, turning, destination and resilience families `not_testable`; the first five fail on materially irregular increment timing, destination has no explicit A/B reaction-coordinate contract, and resilience has no perturbation protocol. Drift is marked `computed` but `measurement_validity=not_assessed`. All 128 files record `temporal_support_status=unknown`; the sample HDF5 explains that epoch bounds do not certify effective filter support. These facts close use of the **stored family outputs** as C1–C3 tests and close dynamic testing in this registered analysis. They do not prove that every observed exact-dt contiguous pair is unusable; qualifying those pairs and a new estimator would require a separate dated protocol and source/support audit. Do **not** reinterpret recording-level quantities as stage-level estimates, bridge sampled gaps, use `mnps_3d_dot` as an independently validated drift, or promote Q/W_Q and model-derived Jacobian/FTR quantities to biological future laws.

## 4. Registered descriptive analysis of the supplied run

The **primary descriptive contrast** is N2 minus Wake for the fixed 3D coordinates `m` and `d`. Here `d` is the canonical MNPS chart coordinate; its name does not make it an estimated future-law diffusion tensor. Secondary contrasts are REM minus Wake and N3 minus Wake for `m` and `d`. The canonical 3D `e` coordinate is a prespecified **method-limited sensitivity** for all three stage contrasts: report it separately by the recording's `e_m` fallback feature, without an all-source pooled `e` contrast or cross-stratum significance test. N1 will not be analysed because it is absent from the selected run. The 9D coordinates are a secondary component-by-component representation check in their canonical order. All nine components remain visible, but `e_m` is reported only within its fallback-feature strata, with no all-source pooled contrast or biological equivalence claim. The nine components will not be searched for a preferred subcoordinate or interpreted as independent replications.

For each recording and stage with at least five eligible selected epochs, calculate the median of each coordinate. Form a contrast only when **both stages occur in that same recording**. For a person with multiple recordings, take the median of that person's available within-recording contrasts. For `e` and `e_m`, make this reduction separately within each fallback-feature stratum; a person with recordings using different fallbacks may appear in more than one stratum, and that overlap must be reported. The person, not the epoch, observed run or recording, is the population unit within each reported cell. Report the number of eligible epochs, observed same-stage runs, recordings and persons at every reduction step. A stage with fewer than five eligible selected epochs contributes no contrast for that recording and retains an explicit insufficient-support reason. This threshold is a descriptive minimum; it does not establish stage-pure estimator support.

For each fixed contrast and coordinate/stratum cell, report its eligible-person count, the median across those eligible persons, the 25th/75th percentiles of their contrasts, and the numbers positive/negative/zero. The estimand is the **median among persons eligible for that cell**, not a median over all 100 persons; missing persons remain in the coverage table. For cells with at least 20 eligible persons, report a 95% percentile interval for that eligible-person median from 10,000 person-cluster bootstrap resamples (seed `20260923`); below 20, mark the interval unavailable. Resample eligible persons only, carrying their relevant recordings together and recomputing the median in each draw. These intervals describe sampling uncertainty under the observed, stage-stratified cohort and are **not** a confirmatory test of C1–C3. No sign of effect, significance threshold, or minimally important difference has been established in advance. Publish all six pooled `m`/`d` cells and the fallback-stratified `e` cells regardless of value. The 9D sensitivity reports all 27 fixed stage-by-component combinations, with `e_m` split by fallback and its pooled result unavailable. No pooled 3D+9D score is formed; no horizon, coordinate, stage pair or fallback is selected by outcome.

Present a recording-by-stage coverage table and the distribution of observed same-stage run lengths and gaps. Stage sampling equalizes selected time where available and changes the denominator; do not estimate natural stage prevalence, transition probabilities, night-wide dwell time or whole-night temporal order from these files. Coordinate normalization/reference fitting uses the selected epochs from each recording, including more than one stage, so the sampling policy affects the values and the estimand. The same EEG/PSG recording underlies feature-derived coordinates and manual stage annotations; their association is not independent biological validation. Report these dependencies with every stage contrast. No cross-person distance or matching in subject-anchored coordinates is interpreted as a common anatomical state.

## 5. Robustness and failure reporting

Repeat the descriptive contrasts after removing any selected epoch adjacent to a stored gap or a change of stage within the stored sequence (both immediate neighbours must be present, contiguous at 30 seconds and have the same known stage). This is a **boundary sensitivity**, not a certificate of filter support or diffusion-neighbourhood purity. Do not replace the primary set with this subset if a stage becomes sparse; report the resulting loss of support, including empty cells. Repeat after excluding each person in turn only to show influence on an eligible-person median; do not use that exercise to redefine the cohort. Report the use and quality of source signals and feature fallbacks, including the exact `e_m` fallback and any embodied-arousal proxy; do not describe a fallback as a direct physiology measurement.

For C1–C3, the report must include a separate feasibility ledger with the exact producer computation, validity, support and qualification statuses, the number of observed exact-dt contiguous pairs (and same-stage subset), the number of row boundaries crossing sampling gaps, stage-label support, and reasons for each closed gate. “Not testable” and “unknown” remain those statuses even if a descriptive contrast looks large. A null descriptive contrast does not refute NDT; a positive one does not validate its accessibility or control claims.

## 6. Conditions for a separately registered theory test

The present analysis ends after the descriptive outputs and feasibility ledger. A future protocol could test a finite-horizon **chart** version of C1 with verified observed exact-dt contiguous sequences or continuous gap-aware trajectories, full signal/filter support, predeclared horizon(s), and person-blocked held-out prediction of future state from current state versus current state plus history. C2 would additionally require a future-law functional estimated from training-fold trajectories; compare capacity-matched `M1` and `M2` on held-out persons with conventional EEG features, spectral power/complexity, sleep stage, timing and nuisance covariates fixed beforehand. A functional that is merely a deterministic re-expression of the baseline inputs does not satisfy the theory's incremental-information claim. C3 would require within-person state overlap between conditions, explicit matching/calipers in a comparable coordinate contract, a prespecified distributional future-law feature, confound controls, and appropriate negative controls. No person's trajectory may cross training and test folds. Failed overlap, failed prediction fidelity, unknown signal support or insufficient independent persons must yield **inconclusive**, not a zero effect.

Even an eligible observational sleep-stage C3 test would be about context-conditioned neural transitions, not causal control, thalamic mechanism or subjective experience. To test the manuscript's experience-facing prediction, an independent design with report/experience labels and appropriate within-state controls is required.
