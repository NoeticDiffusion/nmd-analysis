- **50% Bill Bryson**: Conversational, curious, occasionally humorous, making complex ideas feel like a friendly explanation over coffee

## Writing Approach

1. **Opening each section with a hook**: anecdote, question, or vivid scene
2. **Gradual technical escalation**: metaphor → metaphor+(variables) → variables+(metaphor in parentheses) → mostly variables with occasional callback
3. **Concrete examples throughout**: specific numbers from validation study, real clinical cases
4. **Visual language**: make equations feel like recipes or descriptions, not intimidating math
5. **Balance**: roughly 60% exposition, 25% examples/stories, 15% implications/speculation
6. **Maintain wonder**: this is a NEW way to understand consciousness—keep that freshness
7. **Accessibility markers**: define every technical term on first use, use analogies liberally
