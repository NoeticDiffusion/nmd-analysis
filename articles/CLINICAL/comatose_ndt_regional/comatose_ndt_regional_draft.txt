The present draft locks the manuscript structure and analytic contract before the regional export is fully populated, so effect sizes, tables, and predictive comparisons can be inserted directly once the data handoff is complete.IntroductionCardiac-arrest coma remains a strong test case for geometry-centered electroencephalog­raphy (EEG) analysis because the clinically relevant question is not only whether the EEG appears globally slow, suppressed, or discontinuous, but whether early neural dynamics retain enough short-horizon flexibility to support later recovery. Continuous EEG already contributes to multimodal neuroprognostication after cardiac arrest, and clinically interpretable signal exists in 2Regional Recovery-Linked Geometry3standardized visual patterns, continuity structure, reactivity, and quantitative EEG summaries.[1], [2], [3], [4], [5], [6], [7], [8]The previous global I-CARE reachability article asked whether later CPC 1 and later CPC 5 patients differ in local short-horizon geometry during the first 12 h after EEG-anchor and found the clearest signal in a correlated family spanning effective dimensionality, persistence, and capture-related summaries. The next question is spatial rather than merely global: is the recovery-linked signal broadly distributed across the scalp, or does it localize more strongly to particular channel groups?That question matters for both physiology and translational use. If the geometry signal is spatially diffuse, the global result may already capture most of what is clinically useful. If it is spatially structured, then regional reachability may reveal a more informative organization of preserved versus collapsed dynamics. A regional result would still not be a source-localization claim, but it could show whether the most article-relevant divergence is preferentially frontal, central, posterior, temporal, midline, or lateralized.NeuralManifoldDynamics provides a natural contract for this extension.[9] Rather than training outcome-specific embeddings separately for each endpoint, the pipeline exports standard­ized geometric summaries that can be recomputed within predefined channel bundles. The aim is still prognostic and dynamical rather than metaphysical. The regional extension asks where that structure is most visible when the same geometric summaries are recomputed within predefined EEG channel groups.Several outcomes would each be scientifically useful.The exported tables favor the first of those outcomes. Most regional groups recapitulate the same directional reachability pattern, which points to a broadly distributed scalp-level recovery signal rather than a single sharply dominant locus.
