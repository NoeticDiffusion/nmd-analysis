# COMA-BG-001 part1: spectral/complexity-conditioned L1

Part2 stays sealed. SL-004A is not modified. These covariates are
**spectral/complexity background** from `features_raw`, not ACNS
suppression and not raw EEG.

## Question

At comparable EEG spectral/complexity background, does CPC-linked L1
geometry still separate CPC1 vs CPC5?

Frozen background (recording-then-subject medians): `eeg_delta`,
`eeg_alpha_theta`, `eeg_hjorth_mobility`, `eeg_hjorth_complexity`.
L1 outcomes reused from existing part1 tables. Holm is a **new** family
of four adjusted CPC coefficients.

## Missingness (internal validated)

Subject 0–12 h: 71/71 CPC1 and 70/70 CPC5 finite for all four
covariates. Recording 805/805 vs 782/782. Fisher p=1. Contrasts are
allowed.

## Unadjusted background

Only `eeg_delta` differs by CPC (Cliff d=0.214, MW p=0.028; CPC1 median
higher). Cohen’s d is ~0.04 because the Combat/`features_raw` scale is
heavy-tailed (sample sd ~636). Alpha/theta, Hjorth mobility, and Hjorth
complexity are not CPC-separating at this grain.

Pooled Spearman of L1 vs these four covariates is essentially null
(e.g. \(\operatorname{Tr}(a)\) vs delta \(\rho=-0.07\), p=0.39). Subject
medians of spectral/complexity features are not a strong linear stand-in
for L1.

## Frozen adjusted family (HC3 OLS)

\[
y \sim z(\delta)+z(\alpha/\theta)+z(\mathrm{mob})+z(\mathrm{comp})+I(\mathrm{CPC1})+n_{\mathrm{runs}}
\]

z from the CPC1+CPC5 analysis sample. Primary \(y=\log\operatorname{Tr}(a)\)
(all finite traces positive). Holm across the four CPC coefficients only.

| Endpoint | beta_CPC1 | p | Holm | Direction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| \(\log\operatorname{Tr}(a)\) | −0.90 | 0.0090 | 0.027 | CPC1 lower |
| \(\widetilde H\) | +0.054 | 0.038 | 0.038 | CPC1 higher |
| G90 | −1.20 | 0.0059 | 0.024 | CPC1 lower |
| \(R_{b/a}\) | −0.031 | 0.010 | 0.027 | CPC1 lower |

**PASS** on this Holm family: all four survive \(\alpha=0.05\). Secondary
cohort \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}\) remains ns (p=0.26), as before.

Background z-coefficients on \(\log\operatorname{Tr}(a)\) are themselves
ns. CPC is doing the adjusted work; the four covariates are not absorbing
the phenotype at this grain.

## Tertile sensitivity (`eeg_delta`, pooled cuts)

| Tertile | n CPC1/CPC5 | Cliff d on \(\operatorname{Tr}(a)\) | MW p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| T1 low | 16 / 31 | −0.20 | 0.28 |
| T2 mid | 28 / 19 | −0.37 | 0.033 |
| T3 high | 27 / 20 | −0.51 | 0.0033 |

Direction is CPC1 < CPC5 in every tertile. The low-delta cell is
CPC5-heavy and not a pass. This is **not** epoch-matched suppression
control.

## What this licenses

**Internal validated:** complete background capture; delta Cliff
separation; four Holm-adjusted CPC coefficients remain finite and
significant; \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}\) still null.

**Plausible:** part1 L1 geometry is not only a re-expression of these
four subject-median spectral/complexity features.

**Not established:** independence from ACNS suppression, continuity,
burst-suppression, amplitude in physical units, or epoch-level
background matching. `features_raw` is ComBat post_features, not
microvolts.

**Rejected here:** calling this suppression-conditioned geometry;
folding the Holm family into SL-004A; opening part2; using duplicated
gamma/highfreq/arousal or both entropy columns as independent
covariates; Family B.

PASS here is the four-coefficient Holm family, not an unqualified
clinical claim.
