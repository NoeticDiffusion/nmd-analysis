| Model | OOF AUROC | LOSO |
|---|---:|---:|

| \(M_1\) \(M_0\)+L1 | 0.640 [0.544, 0.731] | 0.594 |

Primary:

\[
\Delta\mathrm{AUC}=0.138\ [0.056,\ 0.223].
\]

\(\Delta\)AUPRC = 0.075 [0.013, 0.143]. \(\Delta\)Brier = −0.019
[−0.045, 0.007].

So L1 does **not** merely recapitulate this restricted baseline: the
increment is materially positive.

Two caveats keep that from becoming a clinical-complementarity claim.

1. Manuscript AUROC 0.599 was pooled part1+part2 (n=281) with fold-mean
   AUROC. It is not \(M_0\) here (part1 n=141, subject-mean OOF).
2. The 26-feature \(M_0\) is a weak *multivariate* model on part1. Diagnostic
   only, not a new frozen model: univariate shockable rhythm on the same 141
   subjects has direct AUROC 0.72, while \(M_0\) OOF collapses near chance.
   ROSC is missing in ~52% and is median-imputed. Adding the 26-block to L1
   also *lowers* AUROC relative to L1 alone (0.640 vs 0.680).

**Call:** complementary to this particular restricted export block; not shown
to add value beyond the strongest ordinary clinical variable, and not a test
against expert EEG or full neuroprognostication.
