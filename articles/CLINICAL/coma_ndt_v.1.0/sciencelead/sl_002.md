This is a very strong result for the **measurement architecture itself**. It shows that the level system is not merely a naming convenience; it is separating genuinely different empirical support regimes.

The pattern is almost textbook:

$$
\boxed{
L0/L1 \approx \text{dense measurement surface}
}
$$

while

$$
\boxed{
L2/L3 \approx \text{progressively stronger identification claims with sharply lower coverage}.
}
$$

For part1, the empirical hierarchy is now roughly:

$$
\begin{aligned}
L0 &: 98.0\%-99.8\%\\
L1 &: 97.7\%-98.0\%\\
L2 &: 85\%-86\%\\
L3\ \alpha &: 1.2\%.
\end{aligned}
$$

That is exactly what we hoped `_level[n]` would reveal.

## What I think we can now say

On I-CARE part1, the overwhelming majority of recordings support:

* observed movement,
* local conditional covariance,
* finite-lag conditional drift,
* one-step neighbor amplification/separation,
* turning,
* history-dependent predictive gain.

They do **not** all support:

* a qualified discrete operator,
* a Markov interpretation,

That is an important methodological result.

# The key CPC hypothesis can now be made sharper

The existing coma result suggested something like:

$$
\text{broader reachable geometry in CPC1}
$$

but

$$
\text{lower local deformation in CPC1}.
$$

With L1 we can decompose that into direct questions.

### Hypothesis A — breadth

$$
d_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(a)}(CPC1)
>
d_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(a)}(CPC5).
$$

If true, then part of the old reachability-dimensionality signal is already present in the **innovation geometry**.

### Hypothesis B — finite-step expansion

$$
\gamma_{\mathrm{sep}}(CPC1)
<
\gamma_{\mathrm{sep}}(CPC5).
$$

If true, it would recover some of the scientific meaning that originally motivated spectral abscissa without claiming an infinitesimal generator.

### Hypothesis C — extreme amplification

$$
G_{90}(CPC1)
<
G_{90}(CPC5).
$$

That would indicate fewer strongly amplifying local transitions in recoverable patients.

### Hypothesis D — directional freedom

Potentially:

$$
f_\perp(CPC1)
>
f_\perp(CPC5),
$$

meaning a larger proportion of stochastic spread lies transverse to current drift.

That one I would keep secondary until we have crossfit robustness nailed down.

# I would not use all 98%-coverage metrics just because they exist

This is another place where the level architecture could otherwise become a metric factory.

The fact that amplification exports four Level1 metrics does not mean all four should enter the primary contrast family.

Freeze one or two representative quantities.

For example:

Likewise, for drift, use norm first rather than all coordinate components unless a directional question is preregistered.
