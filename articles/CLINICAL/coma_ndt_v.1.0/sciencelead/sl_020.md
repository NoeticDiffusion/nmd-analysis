`_load_clinical_qeeg`. Numeric/bool columns kept the filtered timeline
index while `out` was a RangeIndex, so most clinical/qEEG values became
NaN and were median-imputed. Dummy columns were `reset_index`'d and stayed
complete, which is why hospital/sex survived.

| Shockable column | n finite | missing | direct AUROC → CPC1 |

| index-aligned diagnostic (not a new M0) | 132 | 9 | **0.720** |

shockable coefficients mostly follow the *corrupted* polarity (mean −0.22,
94% sign match). Complete dummy trap is also present (sex and hospital

repaired clinical+qEEG block would be a separate future analysis. The
observed +0.138 is not reused as a headline.
