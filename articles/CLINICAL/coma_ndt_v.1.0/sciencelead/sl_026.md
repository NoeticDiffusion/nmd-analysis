## What was asked, and what was actually tested

**BG-001** asked: at comparable EEG background, does L1 still separate?
This dump has `features_raw` spectral/complexity columns, not ACNS
suppression, continuity, or burst-suppression. The test is therefore
**spectral/complexity-conditioned geometry**, not suppression-conditioned
geometry (`sl_023`, `sl_024`).

**RESID-001** asked: how much of \(\operatorname{Tr}(a)\) remains after
blocked-crossfit prediction of the conditional mean increment? The object
is **cross-fitted residual spread** \(C_r\), not innovation, not Itô, not
Family B, and not ingest `/transition_residuals` (generator expm,
`no_biological_claim`) (`sl_025`).

## How this moves the sl_022 ranking

Two leading “it is just \(X\)” stories fail at this grain:

1. **Not absorbed by these four spectral/complexity subject medians.**
   Pooled Spearman of \(\operatorname{Tr}(a)\) vs the background vector
   is essentially null; CPC does the adjusted work. The low-delta tertile
   is CPC5-heavy and ns — a real caveat, not a pass on every slice.
2. **Not extra variance from this predictable local mean.** Removing the
   opposite-fold kNN displacement leaves the CPC magnitude effect intact.

**Plausible reading:** part1 \(\operatorname{Tr}(a)\) is a local residual
spread difference that is not a linear re-expression of these four
background medians.

**Not established:** independence from ACNS suppression, continuity,
physical amplitude, epoch-level background matching, or a
history-conditioned \(\widehat\mu(x,h)\). A richer predictor or a
conventional coma pack could still change the picture.

**Rejected here:** folding either Holm family into SL-004A; opening
part2; calling \(C_r\) diffusion; calling BG-001 suppression control;
Family B \(\alpha\); using stored generator residuals as \(C_r\).
