## Question (sl_022)

How much of the strong \(\operatorname{Tr}(a)\) CPC effect remains after
predicting the conditional mean displacement as well as we reasonably can,
with blocked cross-fitting?

Current \(a\) is raw increment covariance / \(dt\). \(\operatorname{Tr}(a)\)
nearly saturates the L1 classifier (AUC \(\approx 0.68\)).

Subject-level 0–12 h: 71/71 vs 70/70 finite for \(\operatorname{Tr}(a_{\mathrm{raw}})\),
\(\operatorname{Tr}(C_r)\), \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}(C_r)\), and \(\widetilde H(C_r)\).
Recording-level 97.8% vs 97.7%, Fisher p=1. Contrasts are allowed.

## Secondary and diagnostic

Not Holm:

- \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}(C_r)\): d=0.27, p=0.13 (same order as cohort \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}\))
- \(\widetilde H(C_r)\): d=0.39, p=0.053 (directional, unconfirmed)
- ratio \(\operatorname{Tr}(C_r)/\operatorname{Tr}(a_{\mathrm{raw}})\): medians 1.010 vs 1.007, d=0.20, p=0.40

The ratio near 1 in both groups means the opposite-fold kNN mean did not
strip a large extra variance component beyond the in-sample neighborhood
centering already inside \(a_{\mathrm{raw}}\). On this chart and \(k=20\),
predictable local motion is not inflating \(\operatorname{Tr}(a)\).

## Synthetic gate (CPC-blind; run first)

Constant drift + isotropic noise: \(\operatorname{Tr}(C_r)\approx 2.90\)
against target 3. Neighborhood-varying mean, same isotropic residual:
\(\operatorname{Tr}(a_{\mathrm{raw}})=4.48 > \operatorname{Tr}(C_r)=3.48\).
The estimator can reduce mean-heterogeneity when it is actually present.

## What this does not license

is a different experiment.

## Claim ledger

**Internal validated:** synthetic gate; balanced missingness; both Holm
primaries CPC1 < CPC5 with nearly identical \(d\); ratio \(\approx 1\).

**Plausible:** the part1 \(\operatorname{Tr}(a)\) phenotype is residual
spread inside local neighborhoods, not heterogeneity of predictable
local mean displacement under this kNN mean.

**Speculative:** the same conclusion under a history-aware or
background-conditioned mean; transport to an untouched cohort.

**Rejected here:** calling \(C_r\) diffusion or innovation; piping ingest
generator residuals into this claim; folding RESID-001 into SL-004A;
opening part2.
