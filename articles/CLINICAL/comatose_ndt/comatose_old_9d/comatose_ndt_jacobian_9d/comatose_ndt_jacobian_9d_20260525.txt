The reason, however, is now more specific than in the older draft. The dedicated 20260525 export does not support a simple story in which one hand-picked five-metric Jacobian family cleanly carries the whole paper. Instead it supports a layered story: one dominant replicated expansion marker, one secondary replicated anisotropy marker, a small pooled-promoted rotational / mdr layer, and a legacy norm-based trio that still replicates directionally but no longer deserves top billing.That revised structure is scientifically more useful than the older shortlist because it makes the article less doctrinaire. The follow-up paper no longer has to argue that the repaired 9D family replaces the reachability story or even the older 3D Jacobian comparators. In the current rerun the 3D Jacobian-only model is still very competitive, and the revised 9D family is better interpreted as a sharper decomposition of local-linear structure than as a universal winner across every compar­199D Jacobian Geometry in Coma20ison. The joint-model results push in the same direction: revised 9D features remain active in the combined reachability+9D model, but the combined coefficients are still led by tube_d_eff_median and by a small subset of qEEG features rather than by a wholesale 9D takeover.The practical implication is that the strongest framing for this paper is complementarity. The main paper remains the cleanest source for a frozen reachability-first narrative. The present follow-up paper then asks what happens when one looks directly at repaired local-linear deformation in the stratified 9D space. The answer is that the 9D layer is not empty and not redundant, but also not uniformly stronger than every comparator. It contributes a dominant spectral-radius effect, a robust secondary anisotropy effect, and a smaller rotational / mdr block that is worth reporting precisely because the formal pooled export moved it up the hierarchy. The new exact hourly rerun sharpens that picture further: the dominant pair is already present in the first hour and then strengthens in the early-to-mid window, rather than appearing only after a delayed transition. The earlier best-available hourly proxy map points in the same direction, which matters because it shows that the temporal organization was already visible before the exact revised-family rerun was rebuilt.LimitationsThis article has eight main limitations.1.The revised article-facing hierarchy was frozen after inspecting the formal pooled 20260525 export, not from a prospectively preregistered metric plan. The durable center is spectral_radius_median, with anisotropy_abs_eig_median as the clearest secondary replicated signal. The pooled hierarchy 219D Jacobian Geometry in Coma22then promotes mdr and rotational-power summaries ahead of the older norm-based trio, but those promoted signals are not equally robust and should be reported with graded confidence.
