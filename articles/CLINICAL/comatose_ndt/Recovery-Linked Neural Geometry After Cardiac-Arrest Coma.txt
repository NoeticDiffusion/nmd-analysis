We address this gap directly: rather than pooling across the full 0-12 h window and reporting only an average effect, we test explicitly whether short-horizon neural geometry already separates later good (CPC 1) from poor (CPC 5) outcome in the earliest analyzable hour. What is new here relative to prior single-cohort work is a layered, two-cohort design: we pooled invalidity-fixed I-CARE exports from two independently processed, non-overlapping cohorts (301 analyzable patients, 281 in the extreme-outcome contrast) and jointly evaluated short-horizon reachability, local Jacobian deformation (MNJ 3D and invalidity-corrected MNJ 9D), and between-run temporal stability, so that any early signal must survive 1Recovery-Linked Neural Geometry2pooling and cross-cohort heterogeneity checks rather than resting on one metric in one sample. All five prespecified reachability metrics were BH-FDR significant in the pooled cohort, led by persistence_hysteresis_log_det_median (d = -0.909) and persistence_log_det_median (d = 0.896), with MNJ 3D/MNJ 9D directionally aligned but cohort-heterogeneous in magnitude. The lead reachability separator, tube_d_eff_median, was already significant in the very first analyzable hour (0-1 h: d = 0.786, p = 1.2e-04) and stayed positive across all 12 hourly bins, supporting early presence of the geometric signal; this should be read narrowly, however, because a subject-wise circular-shift null did not support a precise claim about which hour shows the strongest separation, so the safe reading is “detectable early,” not “sharply localized in time.” Between-run instability in the subset with ≥3 qualifying recordings (n = 130) added a further, complementary axis of separation. Adding the five-metric geometry layer to a feature-limited (limited_clinical_only) baseline raised internal AUROC from 0.599 to 0.662, a bounded increment that should not be read as outperforming full multimodal clinical prognostication. Because WLST, time-to-death, and sedation annotations were unavailable, the CPC 1 versus CPC 5 contrast should be interpreted as recovery-linked geometry rather than as a pure assay of neural viability.Author SummaryAfter cardiac arrest, doctors currently have very little to go on in the first hours of EEG monitoring beyond how severe the recording looks at a glance. This article asks a more specific question: is there already a measurable difference in the underlying short-horizon dynamics of the EEG — not just its severity level — between patients who go on to recover well and those who do not, and is that difference present early enough to matter? Using two independently processed cardiac-arrest EEG cohorts pooled together, we find that the answer is yes on both counts. We are careful, however, not to overstate the timing claim: while the signal is clearly present early, our data do not let us pin down one specific hour as uniquely decisive. Three metric families recur throughout the article and are illustrated schematically in Figure 2:•MNPS (state-space spread): plain dispersion of the exported 3D/9D coordinates, e.g. m_std or m_e_std. MNPS answers “how spread out is the trajectory,” independent of its short-horizon dynamics, and is treated as a restored descriptive anchor rather than a mechanistic claim.•MNJ (local Jacobian): the estimated local linear operator 𝐴𝑡 (the local Jacobian 𝐽𝑡) governing how nearby trajectory points are pulled together or pushed apart over one step. Higher MNJ spectral radius or Frobenius norm indicates stronger local expansion or deformation.•Reachability (tube_d_eff, persistence_*, tube_capture_gate): properties of the short-hori­zon forward-reachable set built by propagating local covariance under 𝐴𝑡 and 𝑄𝑡 (exact definitions in S1). tube_d_eff_median summarizes how many effectively independent directions remain reachable; the persistence_* metrics summarize how stable that reachable shape stays across neighboring time windows; tube_capture_gate_median summarizes whether nearby futures are funneled into a narrow captured region or remain relatively open.These three families are not interchangeable. MNPS is a static spread summary, MNJ is a one-step local-deformation summary, and reachability is a multi-step forward-propagated summary that depends jointly on 𝐴𝑡 and 𝑄𝑡. A chart can therefore show unremarkable MNPS while still differing 7Recovery-Linked Neural Geometry8sharply in MNJ or reachability, which is why the article reports all three layers jointly rather than collapsing them into a single index.Figure 2: Schematic intuition for two of the three recurring metric families. Horizon sensitivity and early-versus-late distribution summaries are moved to S3.DiscussionThe pooled analysis does not flatten the coma geometry program into one repaired metric. Instead it makes the layered structure more legible. Better outcome is associated with broader chart-level short-horizon reachability and with lower local linear expansion and lower total local deformation. That joint pattern is compatible with a conservative interpretation in which better-outcome patients retain broader measurable local reachability while occupying a less locally explosive linear regime.The merged analysis also clarifies the status of the invalidity-corrected 9D Jacobian layer. The layer clearly contains meaningful signal in both cohorts, but with asymmetric magnitudes 16Recovery-Linked Neural Geometry17(part1 stronger than part2 for the pooled anchor rows). In the pooled result, MNJ 9D no longer appears as a faint appendage; its standardized effects are comparable to or larger than the corre­sponding MNJ 3D rows for several anchors. The defensible reading is therefore not “supportive but tiny,” but “material and cohort-heterogeneous.”The reachability family now distributes its evidence more evenly. All five BH-FDR-corrected metrics show significant pooled separation: persistence_hysteresis_log_det_median and persistence_log_det_median provide the two strongest individual effect sizes (|d|≈0.91 and 0.90 respectively), tube_d_eff_median and persistence_log_eig_var_median anchor the effective-dimensionality dimension, and tube_capture_gate_median retains transport-dynamics complementarity. That all five metrics now clear BH-FDR correction is a materially different result than the previous two-strong-three-directional reading. At the same time, the subject-wise circular-shift null means that hourly peak localization should be treated as exploratory rather than as a validated timing biomarker.The between-run temporal variability analysis in the recording-complete subset adds an orthogonal dimension. For tube_capture_gate_median, the between-run instability (MAD) is a stronger separator than the central-tendency level: d = -1.033 versus d = -0.484 in the same n = 130 subset. This suggests that attractor stability — how consistently the capture gate is maintained across a patient’s recording sessions — carries distinct outcome-relevant information beyond the metric’s average level. The same pattern holds for persistence_hysteresis_log_det_median, where the MAD contributes independently alongside a strong central-tendency separation. Both MAD effects indicate that CPC5 subjects fluctuate more, rather than simply occupying a different mean position.17Recovery-Linked Neural Geometry18The incremental-validity result also needs a data-completeness caveat that is easy to miss when reading the AUROC numbers alone. The limited_clinical_only baseline is not a proxy for full clinical judgment: it is bounded by exactly what the locked export retains, and it omits SSEP, pupillometry, motor score, NSE, and neuroimaging, all of which are standard inputs to contempo­rary multimodal neuroprognostication and are associated with substantially higher discrimination in the published literature than the 0.596-0.599 range reported here.[5] The geometry layer is evaluated on the same restricted footing, so the +0.062 AUROC gain is internally comparable, but it licenses only the narrower claim that geometry adds information beyond this restricted feature set, not the broader claim that geometry adds information beyond current clinical practice.The lead reachability result cannot yet be fully separated from residual artifact burden. The current export preserves only coarse QC proxies such as channel coverage and channel count, while direct artifact indices are unavailable because the detector is off. The available evidence argues against simple coverage confounding, but it does not prove artifact independence, and the I-CARE release itself allows residual channel contamination inside otherwise selected windows.[3]Several competing interpretations therefore remain live. One is a genuine recovery-linked preservation of chart-level short-horizon reachability and lower local deformation. Another is that better-outcome patients contributed smoother or less artifact-loaded trajectories, which could lower Jacobian expansion and alter reachability summaries without proving a specific mechanism. A third is that site or treatment-pathway differences still contribute, although the leave-one-site-out stability checks argue against a single-site explanation. A fourth is that timing, sedation, temper­ature management, and unobserved care-pathway variables contribute to the pooled contrast. The present analyses support a robust association signal in the pooled corrected exports, but they do not fully distinguish among these mechanisms.18Recovery-Linked Neural Geometry19Claim hierarchyInternal empirical result: pooled harmonized I-CARE exports show CPC 1 versus CPC 5 separation across all five article-facing reachability metrics, including two persistence log-determinant metrics with pooled |d| ≈ 0.90, in addition to MNJ 3D expansion summaries with consistent partwise direction. All five article-facing reachability metrics clear BH-FDR correction — led by persistence log-determinant and persistence hysteresis log-determinant anchors (|d| ≈ 0.90) alongside effective dimensionality, log-eigenvalue spread, and capture gate — while Jacobian anchors (MNJ 3D and MNJ 9D) provide convergent support, and cohort heterogeneity is explicitly quantified rather than hidden. The geometry layer raises repeated-CV AUROC from 0.599 to 0.662 (delta +0.062), with leave-one-subject-out AUROC 0.668 reported in S3. In the recording-complete subset (n = 130), between-run temporal variability reveals an additional dimension: the MAD of tube_capture_gate_median separates outcomes with d = -1.033, exceeding its own central-tendency signal in the same subset, and persistence_hysteresis_log_det_median MAD likewise remains significant (d = -0.687). Together, the central-tendency and variability evidence suggest that better-outcome patients not only maintain broader average reachability but also sustain that geometry more stably across recordings. Relative to the exploratory 12_24h global-pool audit summarized in S2, this early-window (0_12h) hierarchy is more stable under its prespecified family-wise correction: the five prespecified rows are jointly corrected-positive in 0_12h, whereas 21Recovery-Linked Neural Geometry22late-window global correction reweights toward cone/tube log-eigenvalue and kappa branches with greater multiplicity sensitivity of the prespecified five-row set.
