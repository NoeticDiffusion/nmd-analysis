This section explains those rows in NDT/NMD terms.d_n_std: stratified deviation variabilityThe strongest stable standard disease rows for Control vs Parkinson OFF and part of the Control vs Parkinson ON cluster sit on d_n_std, especially in global and frontal 9D.In the compact summary notation, the d_n subcoordinate can be written operationally as d_n = 1 - norm(window-average of Tr(H(t_h,w)) / |V|).where H(t_h,w) denotes the local heat-kernel style propagator and the averaging is over valid windows. In practical article terms, d_n is the network-deviation subcomponent of the d family. The exported statistic d_n_std is the across-window standard deviation of that subcoor­dinate.Why this matters here:•lower Parkinson medians do not mean “less disease” in a clinical sense;•they mean that the cohort-anchor disease state occupies a differently distributed d_n surface than controls;•and the replication of this shift across global, frontal, and parietal-occipital 9D rows suggests that the disease-state effect is not carried by one isolated regional artifact.For this article, d_n_std is therefore interpreted as a stratified dispersion marker within the deviation family, not as a literal direct readout of one biophysical process.9Parkinson Supplementary Methods10speed_median: realized path velocityThe strongest event-layer row in the whole rerun is Control vs Parkinson OFF global 3D eyes_closed speed_median.The wrapper definition is the median of:𝑣𝑡=‖𝑧𝑡+1−𝑧𝑡‖Δ𝑡over valid steps within the segment. The shared-reference Jacobian surface remains measurably different.hysteresis_area_me: forward versus return asymmetry in the (m,e) planeThe strongest tone-centered results that survive q < 0.05 are Control vs Parkinson ON standard_tone and novel_tone hysteresis_area_me.The wrapper computes hysteresis as the polygonal area enclosed by the outbound and recovery trajectories after projecting into a family plane. For the (m,e) projection, this can be written operationally as H_me = (1 / 2) abs(sum_t m_t e_(t+1) - e_t m_(t+1)), up to the practical polygonal discretization implied by the sampled path.In article terms, this is a loop-asymmetry statistic. Larger values mean that entering and leaving the event do not trace the same family-level route. It is a sign-sensitive divergence-balance summary built from the local Jacobian trace surface, with the retained row emphasizing the negative side of that balance.Why the interpretation is intentionally narrow:•the metric is real enough to survive correction,•but it is the only paired row under q < 0.05,•and it does not come with parallel corrected event-layer support.The manuscript therefore treats it as evidence that the paired OFF vs ON comparison is not completely null, but also not broad enough to carry a large medication-geometry claim by itself.Main-text selection ruleThe main paper was intentionally restricted to seven focal rows. Wrapper contrasts use:•paired Wilcoxon tests for Parkinson OFF vs ON,•Mann-Whitney tests for control-versus-Parkinson contrasts,•Cohen’s d_z for paired effects,•Cohen’s d for unpaired effects,•BH-FDR within contrast and analysis-type families.The main-text threshold is BH-FDR q < 0.05.The broader q < 0.1 retained inventory is still preserved in S2 because it is useful for auditability and follow-up analysis, but it is no longer the threshold used to decide the headline rows in the manuscript.BH-FDR correction is applied separately within each (contrast × analysis-type) family. The primary_standard_contrasts.csv file contains 2916 rows spanning 16 analysis-type families per contrast. Family sizes range from 3 (e.g., adaptive_collapse for OFF vs ON) to 308 (e.g., regional_reachability_cones_3d for each between-subject contrast). The correction is therefore more stringent in the larger regional families and less stringent in the smaller families, reflecting the asymmetric testing depth across analysis types. The BH-FDR families for the event-locked wrapper contrasts are defined analogously, with rows grouped by contrast and wrapper analysis type.13Parkinson Supplementary Methods14Interpretation boundaryThe review-stable interpretation rule is:•disease-state claims are primary when they survive in cohort_anchored space,•pharmacology claims are primary when they survive in subject_anchored space,•event-window improvements can clean the event contract without implying a stronger paired oddball result,•and negative families are reported explicitly rather than being hidden.This is why the main manuscript no longer treats the paper as a broad ON/OFF success story. In the rerun, disease-state geometry is broad and pharmacology is narrow.What was not variedFor transparency, the following analytical choices were fixed and not varied across the reported runs:•the feature-to-axis mapping and coordinate chart structure (3D and 9D stratification),•the NeuralManifoldDynamics preprocessing pipeline and coordinate export contract,•alternative coordinate projection families (no PCA, ICA, or learned embedding variants),•raw ERP reconstruction at the single-trial level (the wrapper analyzes processed windows, not raw single-trial EEG),•external preprocessing pipeline comparison (no alternative preprocessing was applied to the raw EEG),•independent cohort replication (all analyses use ds003490 only),•and site or scanner control variables (no reliable site identifier is available in this bundle).14Parkinson Supplementary Methods15These boundaries are listed because they define where the current analysis ends and where future work begins.
