This row is not discussed in the main text. Its interpretation — whether it reflects a genuine compensatory or attention-regulation mechanism in the frontal network during eyes-open transitions, or a regional artifact — remains unresolved.10.The e_m_mean, e_m_median, and e_m_std subcoordinate metrics are global quantities repli­cated identically across all regional network rows by the NeuralManifoldDynamics export. The raw retained standard-row count of 30 at q < 0.05 includes 8 such regional replications of 4 distinct global observations; the corresponding deduplicated distinct count is 22.
