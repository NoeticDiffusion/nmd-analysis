Article type: Research ArticleShared-Reference Disease-State Geometry Exceeds Paired Pharmacology Effects in Parkinson ON/OFF Auditory Odd­ball EEGA mixed-contract NeuralManifoldDynamics analysis of ds003490Short title: Parkinson Disease-State GeometryRobin Langell11 Langell Konsult AB, Vallentuna, SwedenCorresponding author: Robin Langell, hello@noeticdiffusion.comKeywords: Parkinson disease, auditory oddball, EEG, trajectory geometry, NeuralManifoldDynamics, MNPS, reach­abilityAbstractParkinson ON/OFF EEG raises two different inference problems: disease-state structure is a between-subject comparison, whereas medication response is a paired within-subject comparison. We reran the NeuralManifoldDynamics ds003490 bundle (75 runs; 25 Parkinson ON/OFF pairs and 25 controls) with a mixed-contract design, using cohort_anchored coordinates for Control vs Parkinson OFF and Control vs Parkinson ON, and subject_anchored coordinates for Parkinson OFF vs ON. The article-local trajectory wrapper also replaced nearest-time oddball mapping with H5 event_windows/* start/stop indices when available. At the stricter main-text threshold of BH-FDR q < 0.05, 42 rows survived: 30 standard rows, 12 event-locked trajectory rows, and 0 trajectory ESB rows. Although the source dataset is auditory oddball EEG, the strongest retained 1Parkinson Disease-State Geometry2effects were not primarily paired oddball medication-response effects; they were shared-reference disease-state and eyes-closed trajectory effects. Control vs Parkinson OFF was characterized by lower cohort-anchor 9D dispersion and lower global eyes-closed trajectory speed and kinetic cost, including regional frontal d_n_std (d = -1.268, q = 0.013) and global eyes-closed speed_median (d = -1.212, q = 0.0025). Control vs Parkinson ON was led by global 9D Jacobian mdr_median (d = 1.478, q = 0.0095) and by global tone-loop asymmetry, especially novel_tone hysteresis_area_me (d = -1.039, q = 0.0269). By contrast, the paired medication comparison retained only one subject-anchor row, tier2_jacobian signed_divergence_balance_mean_neg (d = -0.711, q = 0.044), and no event-locked or ESB row. Medication changes some aspects of the exported dynamics, but does not collapse the Parkinson state onto the control state. These results support a bounded conclusion: within this processed ds003490 bundle, shared-reference disease-state geometry is internally stable under influence and trajectory-label sanity checks, whereas paired medication geometry remains narrow and hypothesis-generating.Author SummaryThis rerun asks the Parkinson question in a way that is easier to defend under review. Disease-state contrasts against controls are evaluated in a cohort-shared coordinate frame, while the paired OFF versus ON medication contrast remains in a subject-shared frame. At q < 0.05, disease-state structure remains broad across both standard and event-locked layers, but the within-subject medication effect remains narrow. The strongest retained disease findings are shared-reference and eyes-closed effects for Parkinson OFF, while the strongest medicated disease-state finding is elevated global 9D Jacobian MDR in Parkinson ON. The resulting paper is therefore best read as a disease-state geometry paper with a narrow, hypothesis-generating pharmacology branch.IntroductionThe central problem in ds003490 is not simply whether Parkinson EEG differs across states, but whether the disease-state question and the medication-response question should be asked in the same geometric frame. Control vs Parkinson is a between-subject reference problem and is therefore best posed in a cohort-shared coordinate system. Parkinson OFF vs ON is a paired within-subject problem and is therefore best posed in a subject-shared coordinate system. The previous manuscript effectively forced both questions into one contract. This rerun treats the contract split as a first-order design choice rather than as an appendix repair.That change matters especially for auditory oddball EEG. The processed bundle con­tains both continuous eye-state structure (eyes_closed, eyes_open) and event-driven structure (Standard Tone, Novel Tone, Target Tone) inside a run normalized as task = rest. This allows one standard NeuralManifoldDynamics layer (MNPS, Jacobian / MNJ-like, and reachability summaries) and one article-local event-locked trajectory layer built from path cost, curvature, hysteresis, and related geometry.[1], [2], [3]The new bundle also improves the event contract itself. The wrapper no longer needs to rely primarily on nearest-time onset mapping, because H5 event_windows/* now provides exact processed-window start and stop indices. That makes the event segmentation cleaner, even if it does not automatically produce more retained pharmacology rows.Primary design principle3Parkinson Disease-State Geometry4cohort_anchored is primary for disease-state inference (Control vs Parkinson OFF, Control vs Parkinson ON).subject_anchored is primary for paired pharmacology inference (Parkinson OFF vs ON).The main text is deliberately restricted to seven article-facing representative rows from the merged primary inventory, with extended methods and tables moved to S1-S3.Cohort and analysis designThe analyzed bundle contains 75 H5 runs from 50 unique subjects: 25 control runs, 25 Parkinson OFF runs, and 25 Parkinson ON runs. See S2 and S3 for details.MethodsNoetic Diffusion and NeuralManifoldDynamics framingThe main article treats Noetic Diffusion as the broader dynamical framing and NeuralMan­ifoldDynamics as the operational measurement layer used to export analyzable coordinates and local summaries.[4], [5], [6] At the level used here, the exported 3D chart is x_t = [m_t, d_t, e_t]^T, and the stratified 9D chart is x_t^(9) = [m_a, m_e, m_o, d_n, d_l, d_s, e_e, e_s, e_m]^T. In this article, these coordinates are treated as fixed analysis objects rather than as proof of a unique latent biological mechanism.Within that operational framing, NeuralManifoldDynamics contributes three linked sum­mary layers. First, MNPS summarizes where the system sits in the exported chart. Second, the Meta-Noetic Jacobian / Jacobian layer summarizes local deformation of that chart through J_t = partial f / partial x. Third, reachability summaries approximate the short-horizon future-space geometry from local linearization. The article-local trajectory wrapper then adds realized-path summaries above the same exported coordinate contract.Standard and wrapper executionThe standard path used parallel subject-anchor and cohort-anchor configs under ndt-analysis/config/. The wrapper resolved both global and regional coordinate layers by requested 5Parkinson Disease-State Geometry6contract and used contract-specific regional_mnps/<network>/<coordinate_contract>/... groups when available.Oddball blocks were built from event_windows/event_label, event_start_window_index, and event_stop_window_index when those fields were present. The previous /events/onset_sec nearest-time logic was retained only as a guarded fallback. Eye-state blocks were derived from the H5 stage codebook and labels/stage.Focused metric definitions for the main rowsThe wrapper reported speed, curvature, tortuosity, path length, peak displacement, recov­ery residual, hysteresis, entropy-rate, irreversibility, entropy production, self-loop rate, transition sparsity, and noetic-action terms (total, kinetic, jerk, potential). The main paper emphasizes the following operational definitions.•d_n_std: the across-window standard deviation of the stratified d_n coordinate. In the compact NMD notation, d_n = 1 - norm(avg_W(Tr(H(t_h,w)) / |V|)), so d_n_std indexes variability of the network-deviation family across valid windows rather than a single static point estimate.•speed_median: the median over valid steps of || z_(t+1) - z_t || / Delta t.•noetic_action_kinetic: the median kinetic component of the article-local action decomposi­tion, K_t = (1 / 2) || Delta z_t ||_M^2 / Delta t^2, where M is the diagonal variance-stabilized metric.•mdr_median: the median Jacobian-change ratio D_t = || dot(J)_t ||_F / (|| J_t ||_F + epsilon), interpreted here as a size-normalized summary of how rapidly local Jacobian structure changes over time.6Parkinson Disease-State Geometry7•hysteresis_area_me: the polygonal loop area between the forward and recovery paths in the projected (m,e) family plane. Larger values mean greater asymmetry between the outbound and return trajectory in that family projection.•signed_divergence_balance_mean_neg: a sign-sensitive tier-2 Jacobian divergence-balance summary exported by the standard pipeline. Disease-state claims now sit on the correct shared-reference cohort anchor, and paired pharmacology claims sit on the correct subject anchor. Once that alignment is enforced, the data tell a simpler story than before.First, disease-state geometry is broad. Parkinson OFF differs from controls in both cohort-anchor 9D dispersion and eyes-closed event-locked trajectory cost / speed. Parkinson ON remains globally non-control-like in 9D Jacobian MDR and retains tone-hysteresis differences relative to controls. The medicated state is therefore altered, but not normalized.Second, paired medication geometry is narrow. The rerun does not support a broad claim that dopamine state robustly reorganizes event-locked trajectory geometry across the main families. The one retained paired row is real enough to report, but it is not sufficient to carry the article by itself.Third, the wrapper remains valuable even though it does not dominate every layer. Its strongest retained disease-state rows are not generic and not redundant with the standard layer. They add a path-cost and loop-asymmetry view that the static and Jacobian layers do not provide directly.Fourth, the conventional EEG sidecars improve interpretability but do not displace the main geometric story in this article-facing inventory. No classical comparator row survives q < 0.1, which supports incremental descriptive value of the NDT/NMD rows within this processed bundle, but not any general claim of benchmark superiority over standard EEG. The supplement-level focal-row sensitivity analysis against the classical panel also remains limited to partial alignment 13Parkinson Disease-State Geometry14rather than simple reducibility, with the strongest group-adjusted associations concentrated around eyes-closed speed and tone hysteresis rather than the full focal set.Limitations1.The paper remains exploratory even though the main text is now restricted to BH-FDR q < 0.05, and the fuller q < 0.1 inventory is kept only for auditability in S2.2.event_windows/* improves processed-window alignment but does not convert the wrapper into a raw single-trial ERP reconstruction.3.Important clinical severity covariates such as LEDD, UPDRS, and disease duration are still unavailable or incomplete. Without them, the present bundle cannot cleanly separate disease severity, medication exposure, chronic compensation, subtype, fatigue / attention, and related state effects.4.The circular time-shift null discussed in S3 is a low-resolution sanity null on exported trajec­tories rather than a full raw-signal permutation analysis.5.Trajectory ESB remains negative in the current rerun and should not be framed as a validated disease score.6.Classical EEG sidecars were included for comparison, and focal-row sensitivity checks suggest partial alignment rather than simple reducibility, but this is still an internal sidecar analysis rather than a formal incremental-validity benchmark.7.The current paper is built from one processed bundle and is internally stable under influence and trajectory-label sanity checks, but does not yet include external replication.14Parkinson Disease-State Geometry15ConclusionThis mixed-contract analysis is a better article for review because it asks the correct geometric question in the correct coordinate frame. The strongest results are not a broad paired medication normalization story. They are a cohort-anchor disease-state story: within this processed ds003490 export surface, Parkinson OFF differs from controls in stratified dispersion and eyes-closed trajectory cost, while Parkinson ON remains globally non-control-like in 9D Jacobian MDR and retains tone-hysteresis differences relative to controls. Medication changes some aspects of the exported dynamics, but does not collapse the Parkinson state onto the control state.
