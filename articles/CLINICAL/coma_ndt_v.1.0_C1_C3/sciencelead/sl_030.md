# sl_030 — Hour-by-hour audit of the strongest L1 contrasts

Part2 stays sealed. SL-004A is not modified. Hourly Mann–Whitney p-values
are **descriptive**. There is no Holm family across hours.

Question: is the part1 magnitude phenotype a 0–12 h average of a few
unusual hours, or does the sign hold clock-hour by clock-hour?

Grain: **subject-median within each clock hour** of run midpoint. That is
the biological unit. Recording-level tables exist as a companion and are
not inferential (sl_006). Coverage thins after 10 h (45 vs 55; 19 vs 25
in 11–12 h). All twelve 0–12 h bins are testable at n≥10/arm except that
11–12 h is the thin tail.

Frozen metrics: \(\operatorname{Tr}(a)\), cohort \(G_{90}\), \(R_{b/a}\).
Controls: \(\|b\|\), cohort \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}\).

## Sign stability (12 testable hours, subject grain)

| Metric | Direction match | Hours MW p<0.05 | Median \(d\) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| \(\operatorname{Tr}(a)\) CPC1 < CPC5 | **12/12** | 11 | −0.55 |
| \(\|b\|\) CPC1 < CPC5 | **12/12** | 12 | −0.57 |
| cohort \(G_{90}\) CPC1 < CPC5 | 10/12 | 4 | −0.29 |
| \(R_{b/a}\) CPC1 < CPC5 | 9/12 | 6 | −0.22 |
| cohort \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}\) CPC1 > CPC5 | 11/12 | **0** | +0.23 |

## What is stable

\(\operatorname{Tr}(a)\) is lower in CPC1 in every populated hour. Typical
within-hour \(d\) is about −0.4 to −0.7. The only hour without MW p<0.05
is the thin 11–12 h bin (d=−0.52, p=0.065). \(\|b\|\) tracks it.

This is **internal validated** as a sign-stability audit: the magnitude
phenotype is not an artefact of pooling 0–12 h, and it is not confined to
the 3–6 h window that looked strongest in the older recording-level G90
table.

## What is not stable

Cohort \(G_{90}\) does **not** separate in the first two hours (d=+0.16
and +0.02). \(R_{b/a}\) also fails the predicted direction at 0–1 h, 1–2 h,
and 8–9 h. Those are the same coupling/amplification objects that were
already weaker than \(\operatorname{Tr}(a)\) at the 0–12 h median.

Cohort \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}\) never reaches p<0.05 in any hour. Hourly slicing
does not rescue the null shape contrast.

## Claim ledger

**Internal validated:** 0–12 h subject coverage by hour; \(\operatorname{Tr}(a)\)
and \(\|b\|\) sign-stable across all twelve bins; \(d_{\mathrm{eff}}\) hourly
null.

**Plausible:** the recovery-linked object on this dump is a **persistent
magnitude offset** (lower local spread and lower mean speed in CPC1), not
an amplification effect that turns on after a delay.

**Rejected here:** treating hourly p-values as a new confirmatory family;
choosing a “best hour” (e.g. 3–6 h) as the primary endpoint; adding these
rows to SL-004A; opening part2.
