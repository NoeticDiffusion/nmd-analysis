## Primary vs. secondary framing

The discovery phenotype is:

> CPC1 is not faster. It has broader local **conditional increment-covariance** geometry and less
> extreme finite-step amplification.

That is the `sl_001`/`sl_002` dissociation in outline:

\[
d_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(a)}\uparrow \quad\text{and}\quad G_{90}\downarrow
\]

Median separation is weaker than \(G_{90}\). Do not promote cloud-volume
or \(f_\perp\) into the primary family after seeing them.

L0 speed matching CPC1≈CPC5 is useful: this is not “CPC5 moves less.”
