## The actual curve, before any exponent

CPC1: \(0.148\to 0.160\to 0.168\). CPC5: \(0.301\to 0.320\to 0.329\).
From \(h=2\) to \(h=8\) the traces grow by a factor \(\approx 1.13\), not
the factor 4 of the RW gate. The finite-horizon spread rate
\(\operatorname{Tr}(C_h)/(h\Delta t)\) therefore falls (0.019→0.005 in CPC1;
0.038→0.010 in CPC5). Exploratory \(\beta_S\) is 0.043 vs 0.059, \(d=0.018\),
p=0.55 — a CPC-null near-zero exponent, consistent with a nearly flat curve.

**Internal validated:** CPC5’s larger local spread is already present at
8 s and **does not compound** out to 32 s the way independent increments
would. The CPC gap persists; it does not widen.

**Plausible:** the part1 magnitude phenotype is a quickly saturating
finite-horizon spread difference, not a Brownian-like variance clock.

**Rejected here:** calling \(\operatorname{Tr}(C_h)/(h\Delta t)\) a diffusion
coefficient; leading the result with \(\beta_S\); treating Holm as three
independent magnitude claims.
