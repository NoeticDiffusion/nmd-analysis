## COMA-PRED-L1-001

Frozen inputs, patient-level 0–12 h, cohort-anchored:

\(\widetilde H_a,\ \log\operatorname{Tr}(a),\ \log R_{b/a},\ \log G_{90}\).

Ridge logistic, \(C=1\), transforms inside folds, repeated 5×20, one AUROC
from each subject's mean OOF probability.

| Quantity | Part1 |
|---|---|
| n | 141 (71 / 70) |
| OOF AUROC | **0.680** [0.590, 0.774] |
| AUPRC | 0.614 (chance 0.504) |
| Brier | 0.226 |
| LOSO AUROC | 0.668 |
| AUC ≥ 0.80 flag | false |

This is **internal validation**. It sits at the lower end of sl_015's
illustrative 0.70–0.75 band, which assumed more independence than the data
have: Spearman \(\widetilde H_a\) vs \(\log G_{90}\) is −0.60, and
\(\log G_{90}\) vs \(\log R_{b/a}\) is 0.47. Univariate \(\operatorname{Tr}(a)\)
already has direct AUROC 0.685. The four-axis model does not buy a large
joint lift over the strongest single magnitude axis.

Full-fit signs for \(P(\mathrm{CPC1})\): \(\widetilde H_a>0\),
\(\log\operatorname{Tr}(a)<0\), \(\log R_{b/a}<0\). The partial
\(\log G_{90}\) coefficient is weakly positive after those three are in;
treat that as **collinearity**, not a reversed amplification phenotype.

Hospital E (n=17, 2 CPC1) is a noisy LOSO cell (AUROC 0.47) and should not
drive the narrative. The pooled LOSO 0.668 is close to repeated-CV 0.680.
