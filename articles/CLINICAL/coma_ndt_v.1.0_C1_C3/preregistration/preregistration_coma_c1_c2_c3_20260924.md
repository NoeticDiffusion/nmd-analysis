## 1. Question and interpretation ceiling

The three commitments are different questions:

- **C1, reduced-state insufficiency:** Does the recent chart history improve the held-out law of a future chart state over current 3D chart location alone?
- **C2, non-redundant accessibility:** In an independently defined neural regime contrast, do predeclared functionals of the chart-conditional future law add reproducible held-out information beyond current chart location and ordinary static/spectral EEG summaries?
- **C3, conditional control:** Among observations matched on current chart location, does predeclared history or physiological context change the future chart-state law?

C1 is a property of the declared *reduced measurement chart*, not proof about the latent brain state. C1 alone is not distinctive evidence for NDT. C3 here is observational and does not establish causal control. No C1–C3 result identifies an Itô generator, neural noise, committor, consciousness, treatment effect, or a bedside prognosis. A CPC1/CPC5 contrast of an existing scalar is not by itself a test of C2 or C3.

The first planned empirical branch is **chart-history C1 and C3**, conditional on a fold-safe re-export. The copied/current H5 is suitable for feasibility inventory but **cannot pass the present protocol's G1/G3 leakage and signal-support requirements as it stands**. A stronger **physiological-context C3 and neural-regime C2** branch is gated on an independently defined, epoch-aligned conventional EEG background pack. CPC prediction is a separate secondary clinical question.

## 2. Prior exposure, data boundary, and fixed measurement contract

The discovery/part1 and historical part2 I-CARE patients have already been used in the predecessor article and subsequent analyses. Existing v3.0.1 part1 results include outcome contrasts, background adjustment, residual spread, and horizons 2/4/8. A new analysis on these patients is **internal development or retrospective validation**, whatever the statistical significance. A claim of independent confirmation requires a genuinely uninspected third cohort or a prospective collection, with this protocol, software, and thresholds locked first.

Candidate inventory input is the v3.0.1 I-CARE bundle `neuralmanifolddynamics_physionet_icare_2_1_20260917_194039`, **part1 only** until the data-identity audit in Gate 0. The nominal chart is 8-second EEG windows stepped every 4 seconds. Use canonical MNPS `[m,d,e]` and its existing 9D definition without retuning. The existing part1 H5 reports cohort anchoring from `all_subjects_features` and an external anchor fit scope/status that are not documented as fold-safe; ComBat/post-feature processing and local feature fitting also used data beyond the proposed training blocks. Neither stored cohort- nor subject-anchored coordinate array may be treated as held-out by model-level splitting alone. G4/G5 require a re-export or reconstruction in which **all** preprocessing, feature fitting, ComBat/nuisance transforms, and chart anchoring are fitted on the relevant training fold and then applied unchanged to its test fold. A verified independent fixed reference fitted before cohort selection is an allowable alternative, with its population and source hashes recorded. If no fold-safe chart can be produced, G4/G5 remain `BLOCKED`; a calculation on the old arrays may be labelled only an exploratory processed-chart diagnostic and cannot satisfy C1/C3. Cross-patient C2 additionally needs a verified common chart frame. Do not mix frames in one estimand.

The measured future is an **observed chart transition** at horizon `h=4` native steps (nominally 16 seconds), with `h=2` and `h=8` as fixed sensitivities. These are finite-horizon chart laws, not Itô diffusion. For horizon comparison use a common source set with valid past and **all** intermediate successors through `h=8` within one continuous recording; report horizon-maximal support separately as descriptive sensitivity. Gaps, run edges, filtering support uncertainty, and artifact status are recorded separately; no interpolation or bridging. An 8-second window stepped by 4 seconds overlaps its immediate neighbor. The primary 16-second horizon provides separation between nominal source and destination windows; Gate 1 must still audit effective filter support rather than assume independence.

All preprocessing, feature fitting, scaling, centroids, context thresholds, nuisance models, neighbor selection, and calibration use training observations only. The independent unit for inference is the **patient**. Windows, matched pairs, and recordings are repeated observations. Hospital and patient identifiers must never leak across a held-out-patient split. Recordings from the same patient never appear in both training and test sets for a cross-patient claim.

## 3. Gate sequence and stop rules

Failure of a prerequisite stops dependent claims. It does not turn missing or invalid measurements into zero and does not invite a replacement endpoint chosen from the observed CPC effect.

## 4. Predeclared C1 analysis: does history improve the future law?

**Estimand.** At the same eligible sources, compare out-of-sample prediction of `x[t+4] - x[t]` from (M0) current `x[t]` and (M1) current `x[t]` plus `x[t] - x[t-2]` and `x[t-2] - x[t-4]`. Thus M1 adds only information available before the forecast origin. The target and candidate histories stay within one continuous recording. The matched comparator M0/M1 uses the same probabilistic estimator family, regularization search budget, training observations, and held-out sources; only the extra history terms differ.

**Estimator.** Use a regularized multivariate conditional Gaussian predictive law, with mean and full covariance learned in the training portion. Standardize predictors using training medians and scales, select regularization by inner time-block validation, and evaluate a proper multivariate log score on outer held-out blocks. This is an operational test of a chart-conditional law, not an assertion that true EEG transitions are Gaussian. Check residual calibration by held-out coverage of 50% and 90% predictive regions. As a robustness check, use a nonparametric conditional-energy-score estimator with the same folds; it cannot replace a failed primary after results are seen.

**Splits.** Primary C1 is within patient and forward in time. Order recordings by verified `run_start_from_anchor_sec` from the run inventory plus within-recording local time, using recording ID as tie breaker. G0 verifies this mapping against H5 `time` and source metadata; H5 local time alone is **not** a global sort key. If global ordering is unavailable or contradictory, the patient is ineligible. Histories and targets never cross recording boundaries. Reserve the earliest 40% of eligible time for initial training; partition the remaining eligible time chronologically into three test blocks, keeping recording boundaries intact and excluding any proposed block that lacks the G2 minimum. Before each test block, fit only on earlier data, with a purge/embargo covering at least the longest used history plus horizon and verified effective filter support on both sides of the boundary. Update training only after a block has been scored. Equal source sets are mandatory for M0/M1. Aggregate log-score improvement `log p_M1(target) - log p_M0(target)` first within each patient, then across patients. Report the number of patients, blocks, valid transitions, and hospitals. A leave-patient-out generalization analysis is secondary and requires a verified common chart transform.

**Decision.** Calibration passes if held-out empirical coverage of the nominal 50% and 90% predictive ellipsoids is within 10 percentage points of target in both M0 and M1, with finite covariance and score for at least 95% of otherwise eligible targets; report calibration by patient as well. C1 receives internal `PASS` only if the patient-bootstrap 95% interval (10,000 resamples, fixed seed `20260924`) for mean patient improvement is wholly above zero, its Holm-adjusted two-sided permutation `p<0.05`, at least 60% of included patients improve, both nominal `h=2` and `h=8` sensitivities have nonnegative point estimates, and G3/calibration pass. Otherwise report `FAIL` for a well-supported, calibrated null/opposite result or `INCONCLUSIVE` if its interval includes zero. Do not select a better horizon, model, or chart after looking at C1. Report effect size in nats per transition and the complete patient distribution, not only a p-value.

## 5. Predeclared C3 analysis: matched present, different historical context

**Primary context.** A chart-history context is defined *without future data*. For each patient and forward test block, calculate the centroid `c` from that patient's earlier training sources in the same fold-safe chart. Let `r_t = x[t]-c` and `v_t=x[t]-x[t-2]`; the signed radial component is `s_t = dot(v_t,r_t)/||r_t||`. Exclude sources with `||r_t||` below the **training** first percentile of positive radii and log them. From that patient's earlier training sources, freeze the 1/3 and 2/3 quantiles of `s`; test sources below/above these cuts are “inward”/“outward,” while the middle is excluded. No pooling across patients. This is a narrow historical C3 test, not a sedation effect or a claim about physiological control. Repeat at `h=2` and `h=8` as sensitivities without choosing a best lag.

**Matching.** Thin eligible source centers before matching to spacing `L = max(16 s, verified effective temporal support)` within each continuous recording; this is the **primary**, not a post hoc independence sensitivity. Match inward and outward sources **within patient and test block** on current 3D `x[t]` using one-to-one nearest neighbors without replacement. No future destination, CPC, diffusion, or amplification value enters matching. Standardize each chart axis by that patient's earlier training median and positive IQR; a zero-IQR axis blocks matching. The distance caliper is the 75th percentile of *cross-context* nearest-neighbor distances in that patient's preceding training blocks, fixed before test matching. Require standardized absolute mean difference <=0.1 on each present-state axis using the corresponding training IQR as scale; report distance distributions, per-context retention, and unmatched reasons. A prespecified sensitivity additionally balances exactly `eeg_delta`, `eeg_alpha_theta`, `eeg_hjorth_mobility`, and `eeg_hjorth_complexity` from current `features_raw`, each scaled by that patient's preceding-training median/positive IQR. Missing/zero-IQR columns make this sensitivity ineligible for that patient and are reported; they are not imputed from test data. These are spectral/complexity features, not ACNS background labels. If balancing destroys overlap, report the loss rather than relaxing the caliper.

**Endpoint and null.** For matched sources, compare the *distribution* of future displacement `x[t+4] - x[t]` between contexts with a patient-level energy distance. Raw energy distance is positive under finite-sample nulls. For each patient, construct a sham context by circularly shifting the *entire historical-context sequence* within each eligible continuous recording by an offset at least `max(5 min, 2L)` and at most half the recording length, with no wraparound observations retained near the cut; draw 200 **distinct, without-replacement, native-step offsets** from the eligible range using seed `20260924`. If fewer than 200 eligible offsets exist, that patient is ineligible for C3. Reapply the same frozen matching rules to each sham; sham runs failing G2 support are logged, not replaced. The patient estimand is **excess energy distance**: observed distance minus the mean valid sham distance. If fewer than 100 valid sham matches remain for a patient, that patient is ineligible for C3. Aggregate one excess-distance value per patient, test the patient-grain mean by 2,000 patient-level sign flips (seed `20260924`), and report patient-bootstrap 95% interval. Neither time-shift sham nor sign flipping is assumed to be an exact null: **G3 must demonstrate calibration on a no-effect process with the same context-generation rule, chart-dependent dynamics, serial correlation, and matching/retention mechanism**. Mean displacement and covariance trace are descriptive decompositions, not alternative primaries.

**Decision.** Internal C3 `PASS` requires G2/G3, state balance, Holm-adjusted patient-level sign-flip `p<0.05`, and a positive patient-bootstrap lower 95% bound on **excess** distance. A well-supported balanced null is `FAIL` for this *historical-context implementation*, not for all possible physiology. A detected difference without matched balance or a valid sham null is `METHOD-LIMITED`.

**Physiological-context extension.** A separate dated addendum may nominate ACNS continuity/suppression, EEG reactivity, or a documented medication/temperature transition. It must define source alignment, clinical annotation reliability, time-varying confounders, a fixed matched-state estimand, and enough within-patient switches *before* any such contrast is viewed. The current H5 has per-epoch spectral/complexity features but does not materialize raw EEG voltage, an ACNS background label, or the conventional suppression/burst/continuity pack. Spectral tertiles alone cannot be relabelled clinical suppression.

## 6. C2 gate and clinical secondary analysis

C2 is not evaluated by reusing C1's forecasting gain as a second positive test. Nominate **one independent neural-regime outcome** (for example blinded ACNS background transition in the next fixed interval) and one training-derived, prespecified future-law functional before a new cohort is opened. Compare patient-held-out models:

1. `B0`: current chart `x[t]`, age/site/time and ordinary static/spectral EEG summaries available at `t`, fit with a flexible nonlinear learner and nested tuning;
2. `B1`: identical learner, tuning budget and `B0` inputs plus the single locked future-law functional estimated only from past/training observations. Match or penalize model complexity so that a win is not merely extra nonlinear capacity.

Use the same patients, folds, missingness rules, training-only imputation, and calibration. Primary incremental estimand is held-out proper log-score improvement at patient grain. AUROC and calibration slope are secondary; report both, including by site. Before running C2, a dated addendum must fix its label, functional, model, minimum support, site-transport tolerance, scoring rule, and multiplicity. A future-derived label or a feature computed using its own future target is leakage and blocks C2. If no independent label or comparator is available, C2 is `BLOCKED` rather than inferred from CPC separation. Only a genuinely uninspected-cohort repeat can be called confirmation.

For **clinical relevance**, repair the documented index misalignment in the former clinical+qEEG comparator as a separately versioned analysis; the old claimed `+0.138` AUROC increment is not a valid clinical headline. Predefine all-comer CPC1–5 or a clinically justified CPC1–2 versus CPC3–5 endpoint, assessment time, censoring/WLST handling, sedation/TTM data, and a standard EEG comparator before modelling. A CPC1-versus-CPC5 extreme-group analysis remains descriptive. Evaluate a locked metric for discrimination, calibration, false-pessimism, and hospital transport. No 0–12 h model is proposed for treatment limitation.

## 7. Multiplicity, reporting, and provenance

The only primary tests after a qualifying re-export are one C1 score contrast and one historical C3 excess-energy-distance contrast, in that gate order. For C1, sign-flip the patient-level score improvements (`2,000` permutations, seed `20260924`) to obtain a two-sided p-value. Apply Holm correction across this and the two-sided C3 **patient-level excess-distance sign-flip** p-value, with familywise `alpha=0.05`. The decision rules above also remain necessary; a small adjusted p-value does not bypass support, calibration, balance, or an independent confirmation requirement. C2 is a *new* family conditional on G6 and must be separately frozen. Sensitivity analyses, alternate charts, CPC stratification, and mechanistic decompositions are labelled exploratory and cannot rescue a failed primary.

Before any run, write a lock manifest containing: this document's SHA-256; input H5 identities and hashes; exact subject split; chart transform and provenance; resolved configs; code-tree hash; package versions; all thresholds and seeds; output directory; and data-access/exposure ledger. Write new outputs only under a new run-specific `results/c1_c2_c3_*` directory in this study home. Do not overwrite copied results, manuscript tables, or historical outputs. Maintain row-level exclusion reasons and patient-level counts without exposing identifying data.

An independent reviewer must inspect the locked protocol, synthetic failures, data support, split/embargo implementation, matched-state balance, and final diff before any claim is promoted. Report every gate as passed, failed, inconclusive, blocked, or method-limited. A serialization success, `computed` status, or conventional unit test cannot close a scientific gate.
