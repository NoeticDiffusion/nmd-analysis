Using an exploratory-frozen ESB-2 specification, the primary score combined parietal-occipital tube_d_eff_median with global rotational_power_median after subject-level ds004504 baseline subtraction. The strongest disease-versus-Healthy separation localized to PHOTO 15 Hz: AD vs Healthy reached 𝑑=0.821 and 𝑞=0.00626, while FTD vs Healthy reached 𝑑=1.038 and 𝑞=0.00269. Cross-fitted Healthy rescoring preserved the same 1Photic ESB2PHOTO 15 Hz effect, though with smaller magnitudes (𝑑=0.676 for AD vs Healthy and 𝑑=0.841 for FTD vs Healthy). Axis decomposition showed that the deviation was mainly posterior-capacity-led, with corrected capacity rows at PHOTO 5/10/15 Hz for AD and no comparably stable guidance-only effect. A conventional comparator panel showed partial overlap with AD alpha-linked power change, but its direct association with ESB-2 remained weak and non-corrected. Leave-one-out, permutation, and recovery-subset QC supported the PHOTO 15 Hz ESB-2 result, whereas the secondary profile-based PRL test remained negative. The bounded conclusion is that ESB-2 is an internally stabilized disease-versus-Healthy perturbational deviation score on this cohort, not an externally validated biomarker or resonance-loss theorem.IntroductionThe earlier photic EEG article in this repository [1] established a narrow but corrected AD-versus-Healthy signal on a global Meta-Noetic Jacobian surface and showed that the overlapping resting-state reference was much weaker at baseline [2], [3], [4]. That result was intentionally compact and single-metric. The present follow-up asks a different question: can the same cohort support a perturbational deviation score defined relative to a healthy evoked response template rather than a single exported endpoint?Rhythmic visual forcing is a natural test bed for that question. Human EEG responses to photic flicker can entrain ongoing oscillations and reveal frequency-specific response structure that is not obvious in passive baseline recordings [5], [6]. This is clinically relevant because Alzheimer disease and behavioural-variant frontotemporal dementia do not primarily damage the 2Photic ESB3same systems: AD more strongly emphasizes posterior and temporoparietal vulnerability, whereas bvFTD preferentially affects frontal-insular and salience-network organization [7], [8], [9].This manuscript centers on one empirical score: Evoked Structural Brittleness, abbreviated ESB-2. ESB-2 measures how far a subject’s rest-to-photic response vector deviates from the Healthy template in a two-axis space formed by posterior reachability capacity and global dynam­ical guidance. A secondary profile-based extension, Photic Resonance Loss, was also evaluated, but it is not treated as a co-equal headline finding because it did not stabilize as an article-level discriminator on the current four-frequency surface.For readers outside the broader Noetic Diffusion framework, the analysis can be read operationally as follows: windowed EEG dynamics were summarized on a fixed low-dimensional measurement contract; subject-level photic-stage summaries were compared to subject-matched resting baselines; and group differences were tested on these exported measurements. The present claims concern only those exported measurements and their article-local audit trail, not the full theoretical scope of Noetic Diffusion Theory [10] or the full NeuralManifoldDynamics interpre­tation [11].The paper is therefore bounded by three questions:1.Does ds004504-anchored ESB-2 produce reproducible disease-versus-healthy separation on the frozen photic surface?2.If so, is the deviation mainly capacity-led, guidance-led, or both?3.Do secondary profile or recovery extensions add stable information beyond the core ESB-2 result?3Photic ESB4Detailed score construction, full inventories, the full QC bundle, and clinician-facing interpretation are moved to standalone S1-S4 supplements so that the main paper can stay focused on the review-stable article surface.MethodsNeuralManifoldDynamics and Noetic Diffusion framingNeuralManifoldDynamics is the manuscript’s operational measurement layer rather than the full theory. In practice, preprocessed EEG is partitioned into short windows and exported into fixed low-dimensional charts under a versioned measurement contract, after which local geometric and dynamical summaries are computed on that charted surface [11]. In the present article, those exported summaries include reachability-derived capacity terms and Meta-Noetic Jacobian guidance terms, which are later aggregated by subject and photic stage.More broadly, Noetic Diffusion Theory provides the organizing structure in which neural activity is modeled as motion on a constrained state space with both local geometry and local update structure [10]. At a minimal level, the framework separates a measurement layer that defines what is exported, a local-dynamics layer that characterizes how nearby states evolve, and a higher-level interpretive layer about diffusion, access, and control across the state surface. The present article uses only the bounded export and local-dynamics layers and tests group differences on those exported observables; it does not attempt to validate the full NDT interpretation.Datasets and comparability contractThe analysis couples the ds006036 eyes-open photic cohort [12] with the ds004504 resting-state cohort [13]. Subject matching was performed by subject ID, and subject-level ds004504 baselines were available for all 88 photic participants. It does, however, reinforce the decision to keep recovery-weighted interpretations outside the article’s main claim boundary.DiscussionThe most defensible conclusion is that ds004504-anchored ESB-2 is feasible, interpretable, and stronger than a null result, but not yet a universal perturbational biomarker.First, the score is reproducible on its own terms on the exact-block/native-block surface. The main PHOTO 15 Hz row was prefigured by the exploratory pass, was recovered by the article-local build, remained stable under leave-one-out template perturbation, and retained low empirical permutation p-values. That is enough to claim that a rest-anchored capacity-guidance deviation score can be measured on this cohort.Second, the score changes the disease story relative to the narrower MNJ paper [1]. The updated mdr_median line is strongest for AD vs Healthy at PHOTO 20 Hz, whereas the present ESB-2 line is strongest at PHOTO 15 Hz and separates both AD and FTD from Healthy. Composite deviation is therefore broader than the earlier single-metric signal, but also less diagnosis-specific. In practical terms, ESB-2 looks more like a disease-versus-Healthy perturbational score than an AD-only marker.Third, the decomposition matters. The strongest signed movement is capacity-led: AD shows corrected negative posterior Delta C at PHOTO 5/10/15 Hz, and FTD shows the same broad signed direction even though its capacity-only rows are weaker than the full composite. Guidance contributes additional spread, but it is not independently convincing on this surface. That makes the current article more about abnormal response geometry than about a pure rotational-collapse narrative.17Photic ESB18Fourth, the comparator panel makes the interpretation more credible. Some ordinary poste­rior measures, especially alpha-linked power change, do show disease-versus-Healthy separation on the same rest-subtracted surface. But their relationship to ESB-2 is weak and becomes near-null within the disease-only subset. That suggests that ESB-2 partly overlaps with familiar posterior photic response changes without collapsing to a single conventional measure. Because exact narrowband stimulation-frequency amplitude was not available on the frozen feature surface, this remains a broad posterior control rather than a definitive entrainment control.Fifth, PHOTO 15 Hz should not be overread. It is the strongest observed frequency on the present four-frequency surface and survives a small frequency-only audit within the primary specification, but that does not make it a confirmed resonance peak. The additional corrected FTD rows at PHOTO 5 Hz and PHOTO 10 Hz argue more for a broader disease-sensitive frequency band than for a single sharp resonance point. The negative PRL result is especially important here because it argues against a stable frequency-profile claim.Sixth, the negative PRL result is still useful. A four-point correlation-based profile score was simple and auditable, but it did not survive as an article-level discriminator. The current data therefore support a strong stage-local perturbational score more clearly than a resonance-loss theorem. A future PRL revision may still succeed, but it would likely require denser frequency support or a different profile summary.LimitationsSeven limitations define the present claim boundary.18Photic ESB191.The article’s central comparison uses a resting, eyes-closed ds004504 anchor against an eyes-open, stimulus-evoked ds006036 surface. That is suitable for subject-aligned baseline subtraction, but not for a state-matched recovery theorem.2.The primary ESB-2 specification was selected from exploratory work before the article-local rerun. The present manuscript therefore reports an internally stabilized development-stage metric rather than an externally validated one.3.The frozen primary surface retains 86 matched subjects rather than all 88 photic participants, because the composite score requires both posterior capacity and global guidance on both datasets.4.The planned global-capacity sensitivity branch did not yield analyzable primary-stage contrasts on the frozen export.5.PRL was tested over only four frequencies and did not stabilize as a corrected group discrimi­nator. It therefore remains secondary.6.The comparator panel is limited to exported broad-band and entropy features. Exact narrow­band stimulation-frequency SSVEP amplitude was not available on the frozen contract, so the nearest-band posterior power proxy should be read as an approximation rather than as a direct entrainment estimate.7.Coverage drops at PHOTO 20 Hz, especially in FTD and the matched Healthy subset, and age- / sex-adjusted ESB-2 sensitivity was not included in the present build. Cross-frequency disease profiles should therefore not be interpreted as complete response curves.Within those limits, the new paper still shows something concrete: disease groups deviate from the Healthy photic response template more strongly than Healthy controls do, and the cleanest 19Photic ESB20departure localizes to a posterior-capacity-led composite rather than to a simple resting-state baseline deficit or a single conventional posterior comparator.ConclusionThis follow-up article supports a bounded perturbational-deviation claim. On the validated exact-block ds006036 plus ds004504 surface, ESB-2 is reproducibly elevated in both AD and FTD relative to Healthy, with the clearest shared peak at PHOTO 15 Hz. The signal is capacity-led, remains present under cross-fitted Healthy rescoring, and is only weakly tracked by the conventional posterior comparator panel. At the same time, the current data describe an internally stabilized development-stage metric rather than an externally validated biomarker, and they do not justify broader claims about resonance loss or recovery failure.
