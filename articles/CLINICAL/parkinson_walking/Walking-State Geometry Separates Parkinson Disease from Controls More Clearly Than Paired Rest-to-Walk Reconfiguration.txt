Walking-State Geometry Separates Parkinson Disease from Controls More Clearly Than Paired Rest-to-Walk ReconfigurationA mixed-contract NeuralManifoldDynamics analysis of ds007526Article type: Research ArticleShort title: Parkinson Walking GeometryKeywords: Parkinson disease, walking EEG, EEG, NeuralManifoldDynamics, MNPS, reachability, trajectory geometryRobin Langell11 Langell Konsult AB, Vallentuna, SwedenFor correspondence: Robin Langell, hello@noeticdiffusion.comAbstractThe walking EEG export ds007526 contains two different inference problems that should not be forced into one coordinate contract. PD walk vs HC walk is a between-subject disease-state question and is therefore evaluated here in cohort_anchored space, whereas PD walk vs rest is a within-subject task-reconfiguration question and is therefore evaluated in subject_anchored space. We analyzed 277 processed runs from 144 participants (116 Parkinson disease, 28 healthy controls), using the refreshed bundle data/raw/neuralmanifolddynamics_ds007526_20260610_121725. In the base standard family, BH-FDR q < 0.05 retained 4 cohort-walk rows, 3 Parkinson paired rows, and 1 healthy-control paired context row. The cohort-walk headline was concentrated in the d family: global walk d_n_mean (d = 0.766, q = 0.030), d_n_median (d = 0.746, q = 0.030), d_mean (d = 0.711, q = 0.036), and d_median (d = 0.718, q = 0.036). A targeted subject-anchor dispersion extension that added within-run IQR and MAD summaries sharpened the paired Parkinson branch more strongly than the original central-tendency-only pass, led by m_a_iqr (d_z = 0.472, q = 6.55e-4), d_iqr (d_z = 0.403, q = 7.28e-4), d_mad (d_z = 0.412, q = 7.28e-4), together with d_n_mean (d_z = 0.409, q = 7.28e-4). The secondary healthy-control paired context remained dominated by m_a_mean (d_z = 0.888, q = 0.0176). The same dispersion extension also revealed strong rest-side clinical associations, especially global d_s_iqr with age (rho = -0.377, q = 8.38e-4) and CTT (rho = -0.351, q = 0.00489), plus parietal-occipital e_iqr with CTT (rho = 0.439, q = 1.25e-4). Supplement-level supportive layers still showed a rest-weighted global reachability branch and an exploratory compact-summary trajectory layer. The present bundle did not expose conventional_eeg, so no classical comparator claim is made. Within this export surface, walking in a cohort-shared frame separates Parkinson disease from controls more clearly than paired rest-to-walk reconfiguration, but the paired Parkinson branch becomes materially stronger once dispersion summaries are included.Author SummaryThis paper asks a simpler Parkinson walking question than the earlier ON/OFF manuscript family. The between-group question is whether Parkinson participants differ from controls during walking, which is a cohort-anchor problem. The paired question is whether Parkinson participants change from rest to walk, which is a subject-anchor problem. Once that split is enforced, the main signal is clear: Parkinson walk runs separate from healthy-control walk runs on four corrected global d-family rows. A targeted IQR/MAD dispersion extension then sharpens the paired Parkinson branch more than the original central-tendency-only pass, led by m_a_iqr, d_iqr, and d_mad, all at q < 0.001. Healthy-control walk-vs-rest change is real, but treated only as context. The strongest IQR/MAD clinical associations are rest-side, especially global d_s_iqr and regional parietal-occipital e_iqr relations with age, CTT, and UPDRS total.IntroductionWalking EEG in Parkinson disease presents two linked but non-identical questions. One question is whether Parkinson participants remain geometrically distinct from healthy controls while walking. That is a between-subject disease-state comparison. The other question is whether the same Parkinson participants reconfigure from rest to walk. That is a within-subject paired comparison. The present article treats those as different infer­ence contracts rather than as one pooled effect. This is the same design principle that stabilized the Parkinson ON/OFF paper, but here it is applied to a simpler and more clinically intuitive behavioral contrast.The broader EEG literature already suggests that Parkinson disease is expressed as both altered resting-state organization and altered large-scale dynamics rather than as one universal scalar biomarker.[1], [2], [3] More recent dynamical and microstate-oriented work likewise points toward altered flexibility, abnormal temporal structure, and disease-linked reorganization of whole-brain electrophysiology.[4], [5] Those observations moti­vate a geometry-first reading of the walking dataset, but they also caution against treating the walking question and the paired rest-to-walk question as interchangeable.In this article, Noetic Diffusion Theory provides the broader dynamical framing, while NeuralManifold­Dynamics provides the operational measurement contract used to export auditable coordinates, Jacobian summaries, and reachability summaries.[6], [7] The analysis then adds one article-local exploratory trajectory layer above those exported surfaces, following the same general logic used in other state-space and neural-manifold work: separate where the system sits, how local flow deforms, what short-horizon future capacity looks like, and how the realized path actually moves.[8], [9]Primary design principlecohort_anchored is primary for the between-subject disease-state question: PD walk vs HC walk.subject_anchored is primary for the within-subject task-reconfiguration question: PD walk vs rest.HC walk vs rest is retained as secondary context, not as an equal-weight headline.Cohort and analysis designThe refreshed export contains 277 processed runs from 144 unique participants:•116 Parkinson disease (PD) participants,•28 healthy controls (HC),•144 rest runs,•and 133 walk runs.The walk disease-state comparison therefore uses 23 HC walk runs and 110 PD walk runs. The paired task-reconfiguration branch uses 110 PD rest/walk pairs. The main text also reports a targeted paired IQR/MAD dispersion extension, but those rows are kept outside this compact base-family inventory so the original branch counts remain auditable.MethodsNoetic Diffusion and NeuralManifoldDynamics framingThe exported 3D state is treated here as x_t = [m_t, d_t, e_t]^T, with the stratified 9D extension treated as a fixed versioned chart inherited from the upstream NeuralManifoldDynamics contract rather than as an article-specific latent fit.[7] The present article therefore does not claim to discover a new manifold. It evaluates already exported coordinates, Jacobian summaries, and reachability summaries under a contract-aware reporting rule.Operationally, the standard layers separate:•MNPS: where the state sits in 3D or 9D,•Jacobian / MNJ summaries: how local deformation changes around the current point,•reachability summaries: how broad or constrained short-horizon future geometry appears under local linearization,•and exploratory trajectory summaries: how the realized path moves through the exported chart.Contract logicThe mixed-contract rule is the core design choice.•PD walk vs HC walk is a between-subject comparison and therefore uses cohort_anchored coordinates.•PD walk vs rest is a within-subject paired comparison and therefore uses subject_anchored coordinates.•HC walk vs rest follows the same paired logic, but remains secondary context.This article therefore does not claim that one anchor is globally superior. It claims that the inferential question determines the appropriate contract.Standard and wrapper executionThe article-local support stack used src/parkinson_walking_support.py together with the run_parkinson_walking_* scripts already present in the repository. The standard layer supplied global 3D, global 9D, global Jacobian, and paired/task contrast tables. A targeted rerun then extended the same stack with within-run IQR and MAD summaries for global and regional MNPS / Jacobian streams, allowing a dispersion-focused read without overwriting the base standard family. The regional layer supplied network-level MNPS and Jacobian summaries for frontal, central, temporal, and parietal-occipital groups. The reachability wrapper pointed into ndt_analysis.reachability_cones.py and ndt_analysis.reachability_core.py, while the classical EEG wrapper confirmed that the current bundle does not expose the conventional_eeg extension and therefore cannot support article-facing classical-comparator claims from this export.The compact-summary trajectory layer was run as exploratory context only. In the present dataset it remained rest-dominant and did not outrun the main standard walk rows.Statistical boundaryAll contrast and association families were corrected with BH-FDR inside their respective script-defined families. Supplementary tables provide the full retained surfaces.DiscussionThe main advantage of this manuscript is inferential alignment. Once the walking disease-state question is asked in a cohort-shared frame and the rest-to-walk question is asked in a subject-shared frame, the dataset tells a simpler story than a one-contract analysis would suggest.First, walking is the clearest disease-state surface in this export. The primary PD walk vs HC walk branch retains four corrected global rows, all concentrated in the d family, and two stronger frontal replications in the regional layer. That is not a diffuse or accidental pattern. It is a compact disease-state walk geometry.Second, the paired Parkinson branch is stronger once dispersion is allowed into the summary family. The original base-standard pass retained three rows, but the targeted IQR/MAD extension added three sharper paired rows (m_a_iqr, d_iqr, and d_mad), all with q < 0.001. The paper therefore supports a revised but still bounded interpretation: Parkinson participants do reconfigure from rest to walk, and the clearest paired signatures include dispersion rather than central tendency alone, but the walking disease-state separation from controls still remains the broader between-subject headline.Third, rest remains clinically informative even in a walking-centered paper. Both the original central-tendency rho surface and the new IQR/MAD rho surface are dominated by rest-side structure, and the reachability wrapper is likewise strongest in rest. That combination suggests that baseline geometry and baseline future-capacity structure may carry more disease-burden information than the walk disease-state contrast alone. The walk branch is the cleanest between-group discriminator; the rest branch is the richer clinical-association surface.Fourth, the absence of the conventional_eeg extension matters. Because the current export does not expose classical comparator sidecars, this article cannot make benchmark claims against relative power, Hjorth, or related classical EEG features. That is a limitation of the current bundle, not a proof that the geometric rows are unmatched by classical summaries.The resulting paper is therefore narrower than a generic “walking EEG biomarker” manuscript. Within this processed export, the best-supported statement is that walking exposes a coherent Parkinson disease-state offset in cohort space, whereas paired Parkinson rest-to-walk change is real and stronger than the original central-tendency-only pass suggested once dispersion summaries are included.Limitations1.The paper is exploratory. The main text is restricted to q < 0.05, but no focal row was preregistered.2.The current export does not expose conventional_eeg, so no classical-comparator benchmark can be made from this bundle.3.Regional rows are sensor-group summaries, not source-localized anatomical estimates.4.The reachability and clinical association surfaces are strongest in rest, not walk, and should not be over-read as direct support for the walk headline.5.The compact-summary trajectory layer remains exploratory and rest-weighted in this dataset.6.The results are bounded to one processed export and do not yet constitute external replication.ConclusionThis mixed-contract walking analysis is strongest when kept simple but not when it is artificially restricted. In the current ds007526 export, PD walk vs HC walk still yields the clearest compact disease-state headline in cohort space. But the paired PD walk vs rest branch becomes materially stronger once within-run dispersion summaries are included, especially m_a_iqr, d_iqr, and d_mad. Healthy-control walk-vs-rest change is present but secondary, and rest-side reachability plus IQR/MAD clinical associations provide additional disease-burden structure rather than replacing the walk headline. The present bundle therefore supports a bounded conclusion: walking-state geometry separates Parkinson disease from controls more clearly than paired rest-to-walk reconfiguration, but the paired Parkinson branch is stronger and more dispersion-weighted than the original central-tendency-only pass suggested.AcknowledgementsThis work emerged through structured correspondence among human and AI collaborators; large language models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google assisted in literature synthesis, drafting, peer review, and editorial refinement.
