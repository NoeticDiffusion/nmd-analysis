The bounded conclusion is that the analysis supports a stable but narrow group signal centered on AD-versus-Healthy evoked MNJ differences, alongside a broader diagnosis- and frequency-specific severity map.IntroductionClinically, Alzheimer disease and frontotemporal dementia are distinct but partially over­lapping neurodegenerative syndromes, and EEG remains attractive as a comparatively inexpensive, non-invasive readout of functional change in both conditions [1], [2], [3].Intermittent photic stimulation offers a different perturbation frame from resting-state analysis. In human EEG, rhythmic visual flicker can elicit frequency-tagged responses across a broad range of stimulation rates and can entrain ongoing oscillations under appropriate conditions [4], [5]. Instead of asking only how dementia reshapes spontaneous neural geometry, the photic dataset asks whether external rhythmic driving reveals frequency-specific changes in local capacity and local-linear dynamics that remain muted at baseline.That perturbational frame is also biologically interesting because AD and bvFTD do not emphasize identical large-scale systems: AD more strongly affects posterior default-mode and temporoparietal vulnerability patterns, whereas bvFTD disproportionately impacts frontal-insular and salience-network organization [6], [7], [8].This standalone report applies an article-local NeuralManifoldDynamics 2.1 export work­flow [9] within the broader Noetic Diffusion framing [10] to the ds006036 photic EEG cohort 2Photic AD and FTD Geometry3(Healthy, AD, FTD) [11] and pairs it with an article-local ds004504 resting-state reference [12] on the overlapping global MNJ mdr_median surface. The aim is intentionally narrow and bounded by the validated analysis contract:1.Can recovered 5/10/15/20 Hz photic blocks reveal any defensible group separation in reacha­bility or MNJ metrics?2.Do the same evoked metrics align with MMSE within the dementia cohort or within specific diagnoses?3.On the exact overlapping MNJ surface, is the main AD vs Healthy effect weaker at rest than under photic driving?This is not presented as a general photic biomarker paper. The evidence still tilts toward MNJ as the richer surface here, but it does not support the stronger claim that corrected MMSE structure is exclusively higher-dimensional or exclusively rotational.DiscussionThe most defensible interpretation is narrower than a broader disease-dissociation hypoth­esis.First, the ds006036 photic export does contain non-trivial dementia-related structure, but the stable between-group signal is narrow and asymmetric. In 093859, all corrected group rows come from AD vs Healthy global MNJ mdr_median, strongest at PHOTO 10 Hz and PHOTO 20 Hz, while direct AD vs FTD and FTD vs Healthy contrasts do not survive correction.Second, the resting-state comparison is included as an explicit article-local reference rather than as background motivation alone. On the overlapping global 9D mdr_median surface, resting-state baseline was essentially null for AD vs Healthy, whereas the same contrast became large and corrected under photic driving. That supports a bounded stress-test reading: the MNJ effect is more visible under evoked rhythmic forcing than at baseline on this specific shared surface. It still does not justify the broader theorem that resting-state is generally inadequate for all dementia-related geometry questions.Third, the severity map is broader than the group-contrast map and crosses both diagnosis and metric-family boundaries. Global 9D MNJ at PHOTO 20 Hz is the clearest pooled-dementia surface, AD_only retains a lighter but corrected global MNJ signature, FTD_only shows strong frontal regional MNJ organization, and one central reachability d_eff row also survives correction 14Photic AD and FTD Geometry15in pooled dementia. That pattern does not prove a single unified mechanism, but it does show that symptom-linked structure is richer than the group-linked surface in this analysis.Fourth, the block-Jacobian appendix remains complementary rather than primary. After mirrored-pair condensation, the corrected block-Jacobian group signal collapses to a narrower appendix-only surface, so it is treated as supporting exploratory geometry rather than as part of the main argument.LimitationsSeveral limits must remain explicit.1.The photic stage surface is recovered heuristically from sparse onset labels. It is useful and auditable, but it is still a recovery layer rather than the original event log.2.The article-level analysis supports only PHOTO 5/10/15/20 Hz. Raw 25/30 Hz labels do exist, but cohort-level support remains too sparse for manuscript-grade claims.3.The H5 contracts expose four realized regions (frontal, central, parietal_occipital, temporal) rather than a seven-region idealized partition, so Figure 3 is intentionally a four-region summary rather than a full scalp reconstruction.4.Many stage-specific cells are modest in size, especially once the cohort is split by diagnosis and frequency.5.The resting-state reference is intentionally narrow and was limited to the overlapping global 3D/9D MNJ mdr_median surface, so it should not be read as an exhaustive rest-versus-photic comparison across all available metrics.6.Corrected group contrasts are much sparser than corrected MMSE rows, so between-group conclusions should be read more narrowly than symptom-linked ones.15Photic AD and FTD Geometry167.Because photic driving depends on peripheral sensory and early visual pathways, part of the observed AD vs Healthy evoked difference could reflect retinal or primary visual-system degradation rather than cortical manifold failure alone.ConclusionThe standalone ds006036 photic analysis rests on the cohort-anchored 093859 export, where photic stage labels are largely explicit and require only light regularization. The corrected between-group surface narrows to AD vs Healthy global MNJ mdr_median reductions at PHOTO 10/15/20 Hz, with an additional global 3D PHOTO 20 Hz row. The ds004504 resting-state reference shows that the overlapping AD vs Healthy 9D mdr_median contrast is near null at baseline, so the main manuscript signal is stronger under evoked photic stress on that shared surface. MMSE struc­ture remains broader than the group map: the clearest pooled-dementia cluster appears at PHOTO 20 Hz global 9D MNJ, while FTD_only shows the strongest diagnosis-specific frontal regional associ­ation at PHOTO 10 Hz. The most defensible next step is explicit replication on similarly well-labeled exports, especially to test whether this broader severity map remains stable once higher-frequency photic stages become dense enough for manuscript-grade 25/30 Hz analysis.Appendix: Exploratory Block-Jacobian FamiliesAn exploratory run-level block-Jacobian pass complements the main photic export.
