Noetic Diffusion Theory: Neural State, Dynamical Accessi­bility, and the Geometry of Possible FuturesA multiscale framework for rhythmically and contextually controlled neural dynamicsArticle type: Research Article — theoretical frameworkShort title: NDT: state, accessibility, and possible futuresKeywords: neural dynamics, dynamical accessibility, state-space geometry, stochastic diffusion, consciousness, measurement contractsRobin Langell11 Langell Konsult AB, Vallentuna, SwedenFor correspondence: Robin Langell, hello@noeticdiffusion.comAbstractNoetic Diffusion Theory (NDT) proposes that neural states relevant to cognition and consciousness are characterized not only by their current configuration, but also by the structured distribution of futures available from that configuration. NDT formalizes this property as dynamical accessibility: a control-dependent stochastic law governing how neural activity tends to move, how fluctuations spread, which nearby states remain probable, which longer-term destinations are favoured, and how the system responds to perturbation. Deterministic drift, diffusion geometry, local stability and reac­tivity, short-horizon predictive spread, basin commitment, and finite-amplitude resilience therefore describe non-equivalent aspects of the same evolving system. These properties may dissociate even when present measured states are similar, because rhythm, context, history, thalamocortical inter­actions, bodily state, and slower physiological constraints can reshape future transition structure without simply relocating the system in state space. NDT accordingly predicts that reduced state coordinates need not be sufficient statistics for future neural dynamics, and that matched present states can exhibit different future laws under different control conditions. Measurement charts are treated as operational interfaces to latent dynamics rather than as the theory itself. NDT is intended as a falsifiable framework for neural organization across wakefulness, sleep, altered states and pathology; it does not define a scalar of consciousness or explain why neural activity is accompanied by experience. Its central question is therefore not only where the brain is, but what it can still become.Author SummaryNoetic Diffusion Theory is organized around a simple idea: a reduced chart of neural state is not, in general, a sufficient statistic for nearby futures. What matters scientifically is the control-dependent transition law — which directions the system tends to move, how fluctuations spread, which destinations remain open, and what processes change those possibilities. We keep a measurable state-space chart, but we treat it as an observation map rather than as the theory itself.1.Scope and epistemic statusThis article advances Noetic Diffusion Theory as a framework for neural dynamical organization associated with conscious states and cognition, not as a completed theory of why neural activity is accompanied by experience (Langell, 2026a; Storm et al., 2024). The scientifically relevant object is a neural state’s dynamical accessibility: the geometry of a control-dependent law over possible futures, not merely a set of reachable points. A reduced chart location is not assumed to be a sufficient statistic for that law.That object is distinct from several things with which it is easily confused. NDT is not a consciousness scalar. It is not identical to Meta–Noetic Phase Space, which is a measurement chart. It is not identical to NeuralMan­ifoldDynamics, which is a versioned empirical implementation of selected charts and estimators. It does not claim that one thalamic circuit, one rhythm, or one diffusion parameter uniquely implements consciousness, nor that every quantity named below currently has a validated empirical estimator.1.1.Evidence classesEvery major statement in this manuscript is intended to be readable as one of five classes:1.Established external result — supported by peer-reviewed literature or other independent evidence outside the present theory.2.Internal validated result — supported by auditable companion code, tests, or frozen simulation/empirical contracts.3.Plausible interpretation — compatible with present evidence but not directly demonstrated.4.Speculative extension — a hypothesis, analogy, or forward model.5.Rejected or method-limited path — tested and not supported under the stated assumptions.Evidence class and inferential claim class are orthogonal. An internally validated result may remain only a claim-class-1 association, while a speculative mechanism can concern a claim-class-4 operator question without being empirically established.Promotion ruleSuccess at one inferential level does not authorize a stronger claim at another. Correlation is not mechanism. Chart separation is not latent-state identification. Preserved latent ordering is not recovery of a local dynamical operator. Truth-known estimator recovery is not empirical biological validation.The remainder of the article is organized by four layers: state geometry, local dynamical geometry, geometry of possible futures, and control of accessibility. MNPS belongs primarily to the first layer. Local Jacobians, diffusion tensors, reachability, destination geometry, and finite-amplitude questions belong to the latter three.2.IntroductionNeural population activity is increasingly studied as trajectories through lower-dimensional state spaces rather than as collections of independently tuned variables (Breakspear, 2017; Cunningham & Yu, 2014; Vyas et al., 2020). This dynamical perspective has revealed structured population trajectories, metastable regimes, context-dependent recurrent computations, and state repertoires associated with cognition and consciousness (Barttfeld et al., 2015; Hancock et al., 2025; Hudson et al., 2014). Yet a coordinate or manifold description does not by itself identify the law governing future evolution: two systems occupying similar measured states may differ in local stability, stochastic spread, transition probability, destination structure, or response to perturbation (Duncker & Sahani, 2021).Noetic Diffusion Theory begins from this distinction. It proposes that the scientifically relevant description of a neural regime includes not only its present measured location but its dynamical accessibility — the struc­tured, context-dependent distribution of futures available from that state. Recovery from anesthesia can traverse a network of metastable activity states rather than a monotonic return to wakefulness (Hudson et al., 2014), and wakefulness has been associated with a richer repertoire of functional configurations than anesthesia (Barttfeld et al., 2015). That literature establishes the neighbourhood. NDT proposes a more explicit decomposition of the conditional future law.NDT combines this accessibility perspective with a specific hypothesis about state-dependent control. Its minimal formulation describes rhythmically modulated stochastic dynamics on a structured landscape: Rhyth­mic Variance Control modulates exploratory variance, while Meta–Noetic Phase Space provides one operational chart in which state reconfiguration can be measured (Langell, 2026a). In the broader formulation developed here, this scalar variance schedule is one special case of accessibility control. Context and physiology may also alter deterministic flow, diffusion orientation, landscape mobility, or transition gates. The resulting architecture can be summarized asstate → local dynamical law → possible futures → state-dependent control of those futures.The central claim is that the functional significance of a neural state depends partly on this transition structure, not only on its present location.Figure 1: The NDT architecture. Dashed bands separate control, latent dynamics, and measurement.One state, several questionsImagine two hikers standing at the same GPS coordinate. Their present location is identical. But one may stand on stable rock and the other on loose scree; wind may spread them in different directions; one route may lead toward one valley and another toward a second; and gates or weather may change which routes remain available.NDT treats these as different scientific questions. State location describes where the system is. Drift describes its local tendency. Diffusion describes stochastic spread. Local deformation describes what happens to small perturbations. Predictive spread describes the distribution of nearby futures. Committors describe destination bias. Finite-amplitude resilience asks whether a larger displacement changes the regime. Context and physiology may alter these futures without necessarily changing the present measured location. This is the measurement-chart question. A low-dimensional representation𝑥𝑡=[𝑚𝑡,𝑑𝑡,𝑒𝑡]⊤(1)or a stratified nine-dimensional refinement, reports an operational location. It answers a state question.What can happen from here? This is the dynamical core of NDT. It includes deterministic drift, stochastic diffusion, transient amplification, short-horizon reachability, basin commitment, and finite-amplitude resilience. These objects answer different dynamical questions and are not interchangeable summaries of one another.What changes what can happen? This is the control layer: rhythms, candidate thalamic access, attention and context, history and replay, bodily state, metabolic feasibility, and — as an explicitly speculative micro substrate — slow structural accessibility at dendritic scale. Control may act on drift, diffusion, effective geometry, landscape, or transition gates, not only on a scalar noise amplitude.2.2.Noetic accessibilityNoetic accessibility is the geometry of a control-dependent transition law over future neural states and destina­tions. “Noetic” is retained as the historical name of the theory and marks its intended domain of mind-relevant neural dynamics; no semantic, epistemic, or phenomenal property is inferred from the coordinates themselves (Langell, 2026a). It does not mean conscious access in the global-workspace sense (Dehaene & Changeux, 2011). Stochastic short-horizon spread is not workspace ignition. Chart-level accessibility is not a report. Its exact dimensionality and biological substrate need not be known for the theory to be well posed as a modelling class. Measurements arise through an observation map𝑌𝑡=𝑔(𝑋𝑡)+𝜀𝑡,(2)where 𝑌𝑡 may be spikes, local field potentials, EEG, MEG, fMRI, or physiological channels, and 𝜀𝑡 is obser­vation noise. A subsequent feature and chart construction produces an operational representation𝑌𝑡→𝑧𝑡→𝑥𝑡.(3)The chart 𝑥𝑡 is a versioned summary of the measurement. It is not automatically the latent manifold. Different modalities, preprocessing decisions, neighbourhood definitions, and projections yield different charts, and their uncertainty is inherited by every later estimate of drift, Jacobian, diffusion, or reachability.3.2.Four claim levelsCompanion studies of observability and local-operator recovery under known ground-truth dynamics motivate four inferential claim classes that govern strength throughout this article. The numbering reflects increasing typical inferential burden, not a strict logical ordering:1.Association. Does an observable covary with another measured or latent quantity under stated nulls?2.Mechanism discrimination. Can observations distinguish alternative mechanisms that produce similar task-level outputs?3.Latent-order preservation. Does the measured representation preserve a predefined ordering of a latent summary?4.Dynamical-operator identification. Can the representation recover a local operator that correctly predicts responses to perturbation or short-horizon evolution?These levels are not a general nonlinear-observability theorem. They are claim classes, and they are one of the paper’s methodological contributions: an operational chart can succeed for one scientific question while failing for another. Detectable population information, chart association, local-operator recovery, and mechanism identification are not interchangeable. Related recent work likewise emphasizes that latent state-space fits can capture statistical associations without automatically identifying dynamics that predict responses to perturbation (Nejatbakhsh & Wang, 2025), and that low-dimensional manifold descriptions do not by themselves identify the dynamical rule on that manifold (Cunningham & Yu, 2014; Duncker & Sahani, 2021). The order is not monotonic: a chart can discriminate mechanisms while failing to preserve order or to identify an operator.A companion meso-to-macro analysis illustrates the distinction: cross-scale associations survive declared nulls, whereas the corresponding EEG chart fails its local-operator estimability criterion (Claar et al., 2023; Langell, 2026b). A complementary IBL analysis similarly finds decodable population information without a valid nominated chart-transition operator (Section 10) (Langell, 2026c). Failure of a dynamical readout need not imply absence of information in the underlying population.ConsequenceMNPS separations can be meaningful at claim classes 1–3. Let 𝑐𝑡 denote a contextual or control state. The latent dynamics are written𝑑𝑋𝑡=𝑏(𝑋𝑡,𝑡|𝑐𝑡)𝑑𝑡+Σ(𝑋𝑡,𝑡|𝑐𝑡)𝑑𝑊𝑡,(4)where 𝑏 is local deterministic drift, Σ determines the amplitude and orientation of stochastic forcing, and 𝑊𝑡 is Brownian motion. Equation 4 is a modelling class, not a fitted neural SDE. The diffusion formulation is an effective continuous-state approximation, not a claim that neural fluctuations are literally Brownian or that all relevant neural dynamics are Markovian. Hybrid, colored-noise, delayed, or non-Markov formulations remain compatible extensions when required by the observation scale.4.2.Dynamical accessibility as a transition lawIf 𝑋𝑡 were a complete Markov state and the future control protocol were known, the present latent location would determine the conditional future law. That is not the generic observation that adding covariates improves prediction. Its empirical content is that predefined physiological or contextual variables systematically alter nominated features of the future law at matched chart state, beyond standard state, spectral, and nuisance covariates. That is the common form of rhythmic, thalamic, historical, and embodied control. Context-dependent recurrent computation is an established external precedent for such protocol-conditional dynamics; it does not test NDT (Mante et al., 2013).C1 asks whether present chart state determines nearby futures. C2 asks whether accessibility quantities add non-redundant information. C3 asks whether matched states can have different futures under different control conditions.4.3.Diffusion geometryDiffusion in this theory is not a scalar noise amplitude. Define the local diffusion tensor𝑎(𝜉,𝑡)=Σ(𝜉,𝑡)Σ(𝜉,𝑡)⊤.(8)The tensor 𝑎 determines the magnitude, effective dimensionality, anisotropy, and preferred directions of stochastic spread. Two states with identical mean drift may differ profoundly in what fluctuations permit. Scalar 𝜎(𝑡) is the isotropic special case.4.4.Potential-driven and non-conservative driftPotential-driven dynamics form one structured component of the drift, not an assumption that every neural trajectory is gradient descent:𝑏(𝜉,𝑡|𝑐)=−𝑀(𝜉,𝑡|𝑐)∇𝑉(𝜉,𝑡|𝑐)+𝑢nc(𝜉,𝑡|𝑐).(9)Here 𝑉 is a state-dependent potential (not IIT’s Φ) and 𝑀≻0 is a mobility tensor: the matrix that converts a Euclidean gradient into potential-driven drift. If a Riemannian metric 𝑔 is later declared, 𝑀=𝑔−1. NDT does not currently assume a fluctuation–dissipation relation linking 𝑀 to the diffusion tensor 𝑎. The residual 𝑢nc is permitted because circulation-like, cyclic, or asymmetric flows need not be gradient descent under the declared geometry. The decomposition is not uniquely identified from trajectories: anisotropic or state-dependent mobility can itself generate non-symmetric chart Jacobians and apparent non-normality. Jacobian asymmetry is therefore not sufficient evidence for a non-conservative mechanism.Dummy 𝜉 remains a realization of latent 𝑋𝑡. Later sections discuss empirical counterparts as chart-level estimates 𝑏̂𝑥, 𝑎̂𝑥, and 𝐽̂𝑥 on a declared measurement chart 𝑥.4.5.Rhythmic variance control as the minimal special caseRhythmic Variance Control is the minimal isotropic accessibility-control model (Langell, 2026a). A plausible embedding does not cancel those limits.6.Local dynamical geometryEmpirical local flow is estimated on a declared measurement chart. Write 𝑏̂𝑥 for a chart-level drift estimate. Instantaneous Jacobian recovery does not automatically license ordered finite-time summaries, and simulator success does not automatically transfer to empirical charts. Those boundaries stay in force here: “stable” is not a sufficient dynamical phenotype.Coordinate boundary. The quantities 𝐽, 𝛼, 𝜔, and Ω used here are local summaries in a declared measurement chart. NDT therefore treats current MNJ-derived quantities as chart-level dynamical measurements rather than intrinsic manifold tensors. An intrinsic formulation would require an explicitly declared geometric connection and a covariant derivative; that object is not currently estimated and is not claimed.7.Local diffusion geometryEquation 8 already makes diffusion a tensor. This section collects objects that ask what can happen over a finite horizon, toward which destination, and after a perturbation that is no longer infinitesimal.8.1.Stochastic short-horizon reachabilityStochastic short-horizon reachability asks how probability mass over nearby future states spreads under 𝐾ℎ. It is a covariance-based approximation to finite-horizon predictive spread, not classical control-theoretic reachability and not a Gramian of a structural connectome. A local stochastic linearization𝑥𝑡+1=𝐴𝑡𝑥𝑡+𝑏𝑡+𝜀𝑡,𝜀𝑡∼(0,𝑄𝑡)(16)induces the covariance recursionΣ(ℎ+1)𝑡=𝐴𝑡Σ(ℎ)𝑡𝐴⊤𝑡+𝑄𝑡,(17)with Σ(0)𝑡=0. Eigenvalues of Σ(ℎ)𝑡 yield volume, anisotropy, and effective-dimension summaries of a predic­tive-spread ellipsoid. That ellipsoid is not an unqualified reachable set: it describes second-moment spread under a local linearization, not the support of every admissible control.Predictive spread is not occupancy, entropy, traversability, controllability, or consciousness. Large 𝑄𝑡 can inflate the ellipsoid without richer controllable geometry. Horizon and neighbourhood leakage are validity conditions. Companion simulator work can calibrate aggregate covariance propagation while failing tests of memory-specific or target-private directional spread; calibration is not destination geometry. Empirical claims that invoke accessible futures therefore require their own estimator validation; they cannot be read off chart location.8.2.Committor geometryFor competing destinations 𝐴 and 𝐵, the committor𝑞(𝑥)=𝑃𝑥(𝜏𝐵<𝜏𝐴)(18)is the probability of hitting 𝐵 before 𝐴. It is destination geometry, not local expansion and not reachable volume. The object is theoretically central and is the continuous-process counterpart of transition-path theory (E & Vanden-Eijnden, 2010; Metzner et al., 2009). Its empirical and even finite-sample status is severely bounded. Current validation does not support empirical committor estimation within the NMD pipeline. Stage labels are not committor truth. Committor geometry is therefore retained as a theoretical target rather than treated as a currently validated empirical measurement.8.3.Attractors and finite-amplitude resilienceAttractor and basin structure — locations, depth, boundaries, corridors, metastability, escape — organize longer-run trajectories (Deco et al., 2017; Hancock et al., 2025; Tognoli & Kelso, 2014). Visual clustering is not evidence of an attractor. Finite-amplitude resilience (FAR) asks how large a perturbation the system can tolerate before leaving its current dynamical regime. Local stability does not imply FAR; Jacobian or finite-time tangent gain does not substitute for an observed perturbation protocol. Without such a protocol, FAR is not an empirical measurement.8.4.Non-equivalent dynamical questionsThe point of this hierarchy is not to brand a new metric for each row. Its defensible claim is narrower: it is a framework for dynamical accessibility and a versioned measurement framework on which predictions from several of those theories can be expressed and, in places, cross-checked (Storm et al., 2024). The comparison that follows is therefore a map of partly different questions, not a contest for a single title. A longer thematic treatment of the same neighbourhood exists as a companion essay (Langell, 2026n); this section imports only the bridges and non-equivalences that constrain the present theory.PositioningIIT proposes what consciousness is claimed to be. GNWT proposes when content becomes globally available. NDT proposes how neural trajectories are locally generated, spread, gated, and made more or less reachable. Those are not interchangeable answers to one question.The mathematical machinery — generators, Markov semigroups, transition-path theory and committors, trans­fer operators, and finite-horizon stochastic spread — is established (Deco et al., 2017; Klus et al., 2018; Metzner et al., 2009; Tognoli & Kelso, 2014). NDT’s proposed novelty is not the discovery of a transition kernel. It is a neuroscientific synthesis: treating the geometry of a control-dependent future law as a primary object of neural-state organization; decomposing that law into non-equivalent empirical questions; separating measurement-chart claims from latent-process claims; testing whether context, history, or physiology changes the future law at matched chart state (C3); and applying that synthesis to consciousness-relevant regimes. Generators, diffusion tensors, committors, and related stochastic objects are established mathematics. The proposed contribution is their joint organization into present state, local law, future-law structure, and conditional access; the requirement that those objects not be treated as interchangeable; the measurement / latent-process separation; and the empirical commitments C2 and C3. The established stochastic-process machinery provides the mathematical scaffolding; the NDT claim lies in that decomposition, measurement discipline, and conditional-accessibility hypotheses.11.1.Different questions, not rival scalars“What does consciousness correspond to?” is not a single question. Neighbouring frameworks answer at least six different ones: whether experience is present (level); what is experienced (content); whether information is available to report and action (access); whether the system represents itself as being in a state (meta-awareness); whether experience is embodied; and what range of neural transitions remains available (dynamical capacity). NDT addresses the last of these. It does not currently identify NCC proper, and its charts are closest to enabling state dynamics — NCC prerequisites and the dynamical context around experience — rather than to report, confidence, or phenomenal identity.Theories can therefore be nested as explanatory layers without being jointly validated (Storm et al., 2024). Predictive inference can supply candidate content (Clark, 2013; Friston, 2010). Recurrent processing can stabi­lize sensory representations (Lamme, 2006; 2010). A workspace can broadcast selected content (Baars, 1988; Dehaene & Changeux, 2011; Mashour et al., 2020). Higher-order or attention-schema systems can represent aspects of that access (Brown et al., 2019; Lau & Rosenthal, 2011; Webb & Graziano, 2015). Embodied inference can bias the background state (Seth, 2013). Dynamical geometry can describe the trajectory through which those processes unfold. Structural network control can constrain which transitions are cheap or costly (Gu et al., 2015; Tang & Bassett, 2018). Compatibility of layers is not evidence that every theory is true.11.2.Closest family: landscapes, metastability, and controlNDT’s nearest relatives are dynamical-landscape, metastability, and network-control accounts (Breakspear, 2017; Deco et al., 2011; Deco et al., 2017; Gu et al., 2015; Hancock et al., 2025; Kelso, 2012; Tognoli & Kelso, 2014). They already deny that regional activity magnitude is a sufficient description. A manifold visualization likewise does not identify the dynamical rule (Duncker & Sahani, 2021). NDT inherits that denial and adds five commitments that those families do not jointly require:1.Stochastic diffusion geometry as a first-class local object 𝑎(𝜉,𝑡), not only noise around an attractor.2.Accessibility decomposition: location, local flow, stochastic spread, reachability, commitment, and finite-amplitude resilience answer different questions (Table 1).3.Explicit measurement contracts: MNPS and NeuralManifoldDynamics are operational charts and estima­tors, not unique latent identification.4.State-dependent control, of which rhythmic variance scheduling is the minimal special case, not the whole theory.5.Recoverability discipline: association does not license a local operator (Section 3).Network control theory shares the concern with possible transitions. NDT does not currently assume an explicit input matrix 𝐵 or minimize control energy. Its reachability objects are empirical, chart-level, and short-horizon; they are not Gramian controllability of a structural connectome. Metastability shares the concern with a regime between lock-in and fragmentation. MNPS 𝑚 is an operational mobility/coordination proxy, not a Kuramoto order parameter computed from pairwise phase relations. Landscape models share potential descent. In NDT that descent is a structured component of drift, not the general law: non-conservative flow, non-normal transients, and anisotropic diffusion remain in 𝑢nc and 𝑎.The claim “conscious states have geometry” is therefore not distinctive. The testable NDT increment is that neural variability and transition structure are systematically and state-dependently modulated, and that the resulting local flow, diffusion, and accessible futures can contain information not recoverable from location, power, or generic complexity alone.11.3.Constitutive, access, and inference theoriesFrameworkPossible bridgeNon-equivalencePP / FEPprecision; landscape descent; state es­timationfitted accessibility is not a generative model with explicit priorsIITboth reject activity magnitude as suffi­cientNDT does not identify consciousness with Φ or intrinsic causal structureGNWTaccess geometry may enable or disable global availabilitydynamical reachability is not workspace ignitionRPTrotational / local recurrent flow Ωchart-level rotation is not anatomical feedbackHOTnone proposedNDT does not model representation-of-represen­tationASTattention-related coordinates as possi­ble inputsan attention proxy is not an attention schemaLandscapes / metastabil­ityclosest dynamical familyNDT adds diffusion geometry, control, and mea­surement contractsNetwork controlpossible transitions; future state spaceno current 𝐵 matrix or control-energy objectiveTable 4: Bridges are analogies unless a shared formal object is identified. Predicted posterior synchronization and predicted prefrontal offset ignition were both challenged in the specified form. The methodological lesson is prior commitment: mature theories become stronger when they state in advance what would count against them. NDT has not undergone an adversarial test of comparable independence or scale.State discrimination is not detection of experience. Complexity measures can separate wakefulness from sedation while failing to distinguish reported dreaming from no reported experience within the sedated condition (Bajwa et al., 2025). Companion coma geometry is recov­ery-linked capacity (Section 10), not a within-state experience assay. Dynamic patterns of brain coordination also differ across conscious and unconscious conditions, including disorders of consciousness (Demertzi et al., 2019). Rhythmic variance scheduling provides one experimentally specific control hypothesis; learned landscapes provide one structured component of drift; MNPS provides one measurement chart; and diffusion, Jacobian dynamics, reachability, committors, and finite-amplitude resilience interrogate different aspects of the conditional future law. None is individually synonymous with NDT. This formulation subsumes the earlier rhythmically scheduled denoising model as a restricted case in which control acts primarily through scalar stochastic variance (Langell, 2026a).Companion constraints illustrate the decomposition rather than a single signature: spindle events reconfigure a stratified chart without licensing causal denoising; early post-arrest EEG separates more clearly on reachable futures and local deformation than on static spread; meso-to-macro contact does not recover a local operator (Section 10). Charts and estimators can fail without making dynamical accessibility meaningless, and they can succeed as measurements without identifying the latent law. A consciousness scalar, a universal clinical fingerprint, and any reading in which MNPS location is the NDT claim remain outside the theory.13.2.Why this may matterA conscious brain state may not be distinguished purely by complexity, entropy, synchrony, occupancy, or global activity. Candidate regimes may also differ in what the system remains capable of becoming next. That is a hypothesis about the organization of neural states associated with wakefulness, sleep, anesthesia, task engagement, and pathology. It is not an identity claim about experience, not a meter of awareness, and not a solution to the hard problem.The hypothesis is useful only if the layers dissociate. If reachability, local flow, and diffusion were redundant encodings of location or power, the accessibility hypothesis would collapse. The present empirical map is not a validation of the chain. It is evidence that the collapse has not already occurred in every tested regime, and that different conditions move different layers (Table 3). Consciousness research already contains neighbouring theories of content, access, recurrence, and intrinsic causal structure (Section 11). NDT’s contribution, if it holds, is a measurement surface for dynamical capacity and its control — a surface on which those theories can make distinct predictions, not a replacement for them.The next informative experiment is therefore not another broad disease contrast. It is a design that separates global state, responsiveness, reportability, and momentary experience, and that asks whether rhythm-locked or context-locked changes in local flow, diffusion, and short-horizon reachability explain future trajectories beyond power, generic complexity, and stationary dynamics (Section 12). A positive result would still support a neural mechanism, not the claim that reconstruction is consciousness.14.LimitationsNo dataset currently validates the NDT chain end to end. Many nominated objects remain estimator-sensitive: MNPS is operational and chart-dependent; drift and diffusion are identifiable only under explicit assumptions; local linearization fails in important empirical regimes, including the EEG chart tested on DANDI 000458; reachability depends on horizon and noise model; committor estimation requires meaningful destinations that current measurement contracts do not emit; and finite-amplitude resilience depends on perturbation family and amplitude scale.Biological bridges remain uneven. Rhythmic variance control is a distinctive prediction, not a demonstrated executor. Thalamic access has synthetic operator existence and associative biology, not circuit identification. History-dependent landscape writing is open; access on a frozen landscape is a computational existence result, not scored hippocampal mechanism. Embodiment and metabolism are contextual and feasibility constraints, not consciousness coordinates. Dendritic slow-branch accessibility is a simulator discriminator and a speculative micro substrate.Empirical companions are bounded. Sleep, coma, and meso-to-macro analyses constrain selected layers in selected cohorts. Clinical fingerprints are not biomarkers. Treatment-limitation and sedation confounds remain unresolved where they were unavailable. NDT has not undergone an independent adversarial test comparable to Cogitate. Apparent geometric effects must still survive alternative charts, power-matched and temporally shuffled nulls, and standard spectral, connectivity, and complexity baselines.These limits are scope, not temporary embarrassment. NDT is a framework for neural dynamical organi­zation associated with conscious-state regimes. It does not explain why those dynamics are accompanied by experience.15.ConclusionNoetic Diffusion Theory proposes that neural-state organization is incompletely described by state position alone. A neural state also carries a local dynamical structure: deterministic tendencies, stochastic directions, transient sensitivities, reachable futures, basin commitments, and finite-amplitude resilience. Those structures are themselves modulated by rhythm, context, history, physiology, and candidate access-control systems. MNPS supplies one measurable chart through which this organization can be studied. NeuralManifoldDynamics supplies one implementation rather than the definition of the theory. Rhythmic Variance Control supplies the minimal control case rather than the whole mechanism.The resulting framework predicts structured dissociations among location, stability, diffusion, accessibility, commitment, and resilience, and treats those dissociations as empirically testable correlates of changing neural regimes — not as a scalar of consciousness, and not as a complete explanation of experience. What a neural state is, scientifically, includes what it can still become.16.AcknowledgementsDataset contributors, pipeline authors, and archival records will be acknowledged in full at submission.
