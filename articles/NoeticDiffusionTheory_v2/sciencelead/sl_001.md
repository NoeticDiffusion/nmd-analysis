# Noetic Diffusion Theory: Neural State, Dynamical Accessibility, and the Geometry of Possible Futures

### A multiscale framework for rhythmically and contextually controlled neural dynamics

**Article type:** Theoretical Framework / Research Synthesis
**Possible target:** *Neuroscience of Consciousness* / *Network Neuroscience*

## Abstract

* Noetic Diffusion Theory (NDT) proposes that neural-state organization should be characterized not only by **where a system is located in state space**, but by **what transitions, perturbations, and future states remain dynamically accessible from that location**.
* Earlier formulations emphasized rhythmic variance control, denoising on a learned potential landscape, and representation in Meta-Noetic Phase Space (MNPS).
* The present framework generalizes that account into a layered dynamical theory combining:

  * measured state geometry,
  * local deterministic flow,
  * local stochastic diffusion geometry,
  * stability and reactivity,
  * short-horizon reachability,
  * basin commitment,
  * finite-amplitude resilience,
  * and candidate biological mechanisms controlling accessibility.
* MNPS 3D and Stratified MNPS 9D are retained as canonical **measurement charts**, while NeuralManifoldDynamics is separated from NDT proper as one versioned empirical realization of those charts.
* Rhythmic Variance Control is retained as a minimal mechanism but generalized into a broader concept of **state-dependent accessibility control**.
* Candidate biological contributors include thalamic/thalamoreticular gating, history-dependent landscape modification, embodied state, and slower metabolic constraints.
* Companion truth-known simulation studies provide estimator-level validation for selected diffusion and local-Jacobian quantities under restricted conditions, while empirical work across sleep, altered states, clinical cohorts, coma, and meso-to-macro datasets supplies bounded constraints rather than validation of the whole theory.
* NDT therefore predicts structured dissociations between state location, local stability, stochastic spread, accessible futures, basin commitment, and recovery from perturbation rather than a single scalar measure of consciousness.
* The framework is falsifiable at multiple levels and explicitly separates measurement association, latent-state inference, dynamical-operator identification, and biological mechanism.

# 1. Scope and epistemic status

## 1.1 What NDT is

* A theory of **neural dynamical organization associated with conscious states and cognition**.
* A framework for asking:

  * where the neural system currently lies,
  * how it is locally evolving,
  * what perturbations grow or decay,
  * what nearby futures remain available,
  * what longer-run destinations are probable,
  * and what mechanisms reshape these possibilities.
* A proposal that **dynamical accessibility** is a more informative organizing principle than state position or signal complexity alone.

## 1.2 What NDT is not

* Not a completed theory explaining why neural activity is accompanied by experience.
* Not a consciousness scalar.
* Not identical to MNPS.
* Not identical to NeuralManifoldDynamics.
* Not a claim that one thalamic circuit, one rhythm, or one diffusion parameter uniquely implements consciousness.
* Not a claim that every candidate quantity currently has a validated empirical estimator.

## 1.3 Evidence classes used throughout

Every major statement should be explicitly identifiable as:

1. **Established external result**
2. **Internal validated result**
3. **Plausible interpretation**
4. **Speculative extension**
5. **Rejected/falsified path**

### Key editorial rule

> Success at one level does not authorize a stronger claim at another level.

Examples:

* correlation ≠ mechanism;
* chart separation ≠ latent-state identification;
* latent ordering ≠ local dynamical operator;
* synthetic estimator recovery ≠ empirical biological validation.

This principle already forms an important part of the meso-to-macro draft.

# 2. From neural state to neural accessibility

## 2.1 The central theoretical move

Earlier NDT can be summarized approximately as:

> rhythmic control → variance scheduling → exploration/denoising → MNPS geometry.

The revised formulation becomes:

> **state → local dynamical law → possible futures → state-dependent control of those futures.**

The core claim is therefore no longer merely that conscious-state correlates occupy characteristic regions of neural geometry.

Instead:

> **The functional significance of a neural state depends partly on the structured set of transitions available from it.**

## 2.2 Three fundamental questions

### Question 1 — Where is the system?

Measured state geometry.

[
x_t=[m_t,d_t,e_t]^\top
]

or a richer stratified chart.

### Question 2 — What can happen from here?

Dynamical accessibility:

* deterministic flow,
* stochastic diffusion,
* transient amplification,
* reachability,
* basin commitment,
* resilience.

### Question 3 — What changes what can happen?

Accessibility control:

* rhythms,
* thalamic circuitry,
* attention/context,
* history and replay,
* bodily state,
* metabolic constraints.

## 2.3 Working definition

**Noetic accessibility**

> The structured, state-dependent set of dynamically attainable neural transitions and destinations under the current deterministic, stochastic, contextual, and physiological constraints.

Important boundary:

* accessibility ≠ conscious access in the GNWT sense;
* accessibility here is a **dynamical-systems concept**.

## 3.2 Observation

Examples:

* fMRI,
* physiological channels.

## 3.3 Measurement chart

A feature/chart transformation produces:

[
Y_t \rightarrow z_t \rightarrow x_t.
]

The chart is an operational representation of the measured system.

It is not automatically the latent neural manifold.

## 3.4 Four levels of recoverability

Retain and expand the ladder from the meso-to-macro draft. 

### Level 1 — Association

Does an observable covary with another measured or latent quantity?

### Level 2 — Mechanism discrimination

Can observations distinguish alternative mechanisms producing similar outputs?

### Level 3 — Latent-order preservation

Does the measured representation preserve a predefined latent ordering?

### Level 4 — Dynamical-operator identification

Can the representation recover a local operator that correctly predicts responses to perturbation or temporal evolution?

### Consequence

* MNPS separation can be meaningful at Levels 1–3.
* Jacobian, diffusion, reachability, and related dynamical claims require stronger validity conditions.

# 4. Core NDT dynamics

## 4.1 General stochastic formulation

Proposed new canonical equation:

[
dX_t
====

b(X_t,t;c_t),dt
+
\Sigma(X_t,t;c_t),dW_t
]

where:

* (X_t): latent neural state;
* (b): deterministic local drift;
* (\Sigma): stochastic amplitude and orientation;
* (W_t): stochastic forcing;
* (c_t): contextual/control state.

This should replace the strict assumption that all NDT dynamics are purely potential-driven.

## 4.2 Diffusion geometry

Define:

[
a(x,t)=\Sigma(x,t)\Sigma(x,t)^\top.
]

Interpretation:

* scalar noise amplitude is only a special case;
* (a) determines:

  * magnitude of stochastic spread,
  * dimensionality,
  * anisotropy,
  * preferred diffusion directions.

### NDT implication

Two states with identical mean drift may differ profoundly in what fluctuations permit.

## 4.3 Potential-driven component

Preserve the original NDT landscape idea through:

[
b_{\mathrm{pot}}
================

-G(x,t;c)\nabla\Phi(x,t;c).
]

Where:

* (\Phi): state-dependent potential / landscape;
* (G): effective metric or mobility geometry.

The earlier NDT formulation strongly foregrounds this potential-based description. 

## 4.4 Non-conservative flow

Generalize to:

[
b
=

-G\nabla\Phi
+
u_{\mathrm{nc}}.
]

(u_{\mathrm{nc}}) may contain:

* rotational flow,
* recurrent cycling,
* asymmetric transport,
* non-normal transient dynamics,
* context-conditioned routing.

### Rationale

Pure gradient descent cannot naturally represent all observed or simulated local dynamical structure.

This extension allows NDT to incorporate reactivity and rotation without abandoning its landscape interpretation.

# 6. Local dynamical geometry

## 6.1 Drift

[
b(x,t)
======

\lim_{\Delta t\rightarrow 0}
\frac{\mathbb E[X_{t+\Delta t}-X_t\mid X_t=x]}{\Delta t}.
]

Question:

> In which direction does the system tend to move from here?

## 6.2 Local Jacobian

[
J(x,t)=\frac{\partial b}{\partial x}.
]

Question:

> How do nearby perturbations change under the local flow?

Important terminology:

* generic (J) = local Jacobian;
* **MNJ** should be reserved for a Jacobian specifically estimated in an MNPS chart.

## 6.3 Modal stability

Spectral abscissa:

[
\alpha(J)=\max_i\Re[\lambda_i(J)].
]

Interpretation:

* dominant modal growth/decay;
* signed continuous-time stability quantity.

## 6.4 Instantaneous reactivity

Numerical abscissa:

[
\omega(J)
=========

\lambda_{\max}
\left(
\frac{J+J^\top}{2}
\right).
]

Interpretation:

* maximal instantaneous growth direction;
* can be positive even when (\alpha<0).

## 6.5 Reactivity gap

[
\Delta_{\mathrm{react}}
=======================

\omega-\alpha.
]

Interpret as:

* separation between instantaneous and modal growth;
* non-normality-sensitive summary;
* not a complete measure of non-normality.

## 6.6 Rotational component

[
\Omega
======

\frac{J-J^\top}{2}.
]

Potential summaries:

* (|\Omega|_F);
* rotational power;
* directionality.

## 6.7 Finite-time amplification

Keep conceptually separate from instantaneous Jacobian metrics.

Example:

[
G_{\mathrm{peak}}(T)
====================

\max_{0<t<T}|e^{Jt}|.
]

Status should follow current validation evidence and failed gates rather than being automatically promoted.

## 6.8 Main conceptual dissociation

A system can be:

* asymptotically stable,
* instantaneously reactive,
* rotational,
* highly anisotropic,
* yet finite-amplitude fragile.

Therefore:

> **“stable” is not a sufficient dynamical phenotype.**

# 17. What would falsify or substantially narrow NDT?

NDT should be revised if:

* rhythmic scheduling provides no predictive advantage;
* MNPS effects fail under reasonable alternative chart constructions;
* local-dynamics estimators repeatedly fail truth-known or empirical validity tests;
* diffusion/reachability/FAR reduce to redundant versions of simpler statistics;
* history adds no conditional information;
* candidate access-control mechanisms fail causal or discriminative tests;
* apparent clinical/altered-state effects are explained entirely by simple power, movement, arousal, or preprocessing artifacts.

Important:

> Falsification of one subsystem does not automatically falsify all NDT.

Examples:

* TRN failure would falsify a biological implementation;
* RVC failure would strongly constrain the rhythmic mechanism;
* MNPS failure would invalidate that measurement chart;
* none alone establishes that general neural dynamical accessibility is meaningless.

This hierarchy needs to be explicit.

# 20. Conclusion

Possible eventual concluding thesis:

> Noetic Diffusion Theory proposes that neural-state organization is incompletely described by state position alone. A neural state also carries a local dynamical structure: deterministic tendencies, stochastic directions, transient sensitivities, reachable futures, basin commitments, and finite-amplitude resilience. These structures are themselves modulated by rhythm, context, history, physiology, and candidate thalamic control systems. MNPS supplies one measurable chart through which this dynamical organization can be studied, while NeuralManifoldDynamics provides one implementation rather than the definition of the theory. The resulting framework predicts structured dissociations between state, stability, diffusion, accessibility, commitment, and resilience, and treats those dissociations as empirically testable correlates of changing neural regimes rather than as a scalar measure or complete explanation of consciousness.

## One editorial decision I would freeze immediately

I would make this the conceptual hierarchy of the entire paper:

> **State geometry → Local dynamical geometry → Geometry of possible futures → Control of accessibility**

And then explicitly say that **MNPS 3D/9D belongs primarily to the first level**, whereas much of the recent research programme—Jacobian dynamics, spectral/numerical abscissa, diffusion geometry, reachability, committor, attractors and FAR—belongs to the latter three.

That gives us a very useful criterion when we later import the individual articles: **each companion result must earn a place in one of these levels rather than simply being appended because it exists.**

I think this is a good enough architecture now to become the basis of an actual manuscript branch.
