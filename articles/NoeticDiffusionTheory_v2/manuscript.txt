Noetic Diffusion Theory: Neural State, Dynamical Accessi­bility, and the Geometry of Possible FuturesA multiscale framework for rhythmically and contextually controlled neural dynamicsArticle type: Research Article — theoretical frameworkShort title: NDT: state, accessibility, and possible futuresKeywords: neural dynamics, dynamical accessibility, state-space geometry, stochastic diffusion, consciousness, measurement contractsRobin Langell11 Langell Konsult AB, Vallentuna, SwedenFor correspondence: Robin Langell, hello@noeticdiffusion.comAbstractNoetic Diffusion Theory (NDT) proposes that neural-state organization should be characterized not only by where a system is located in state space, but by which transitions, perturbations, and future states remain dynamically accessible from that location. Earlier formulations emphasized rhythmic variance control, denoising on a learned potential, and representation in Meta–Noetic Phase Space (MNPS). The present framework generalizes that account into a layered dynamical theory: measured state geometry, local deterministic flow, local stochastic diffusion geometry, stability and reactivity, short-horizon reachability, basin commitment, finite-amplitude resilience, and candidate biological mechanisms that control accessibility. MNPS 3D and stratified MNPS 9D are retained as canonical measurement charts. NeuralManifoldDynamics is separated from NDT proper as one versioned empirical realization of those charts. Rhythmic Variance Control is retained as the minimal accessibility-control mechanism, not as the whole theory. Companion truth-known simulations qualify selected diffusion and Jacobian-derived summaries under restricted conditions; empirical work across sleep, altered states, clinical cohorts, and meso-to-macro recordings supplies bounded constraints rather than validation of the chain as a whole. NDT therefore predicts struc­tured dissociations among state location, local stability, stochastic spread, accessible futures, basin commitment, and recovery from perturbation, rather than a single scalar of consciousness.Author SummaryThis paper rebuilds Noetic Diffusion Theory around a simple idea: a neural state is not fully described by where it sits. What matters scientifically is also what the system can still do from there — which directions it tends to move, how fluctuations spread, which nearby futures remain open, and what control processes change those possibilities. We keep a measurable state-space chart, but we treat it as an observation map rather than as the theory itself.1.Scope and epistemic statusThis article advances Noetic Diffusion Theory as a framework for neural dynamical organization associated with conscious states and cognition, not as a completed theory of why neural activity is accompanied by experience (Langell, 2025; Storm et al., 2024). The scientifically relevant object is a neural state’s dynamical accessibility: the structured set of transitions, perturbations, destinations, and recoveries that remain available from the current condition, under deterministic, stochastic, contextual, and physiological constraints.That object is distinct from several things with which it is easily confused. NDT is not a consciousness scalar. It is not identical to Meta–Noetic Phase Space, which is a measurement chart. It is not identical to NeuralMan­ifoldDynamics, which is a versioned empirical implementation of selected charts and estimators. It does not claim that one thalamic circuit, one rhythm, or one diffusion parameter uniquely implements consciousness, nor that every quantity named below currently has a validated empirical estimator.1.1.Evidence classesEvery major statement in this manuscript is intended to be readable as one of five classes:1.Established external result — supported by peer-reviewed literature or other independent evidence outside this programme.2.Internal validated result — supported by auditable companion code, tests, or frozen simulation/empirical contracts.3.Plausible interpretation — compatible with present evidence but not directly demonstrated.4.Speculative extension — a hypothesis, analogy, or forward model.5.Rejected or method-limited path — tested and not supported under the stated assumptions.Promotion ruleSuccess at one inferential level does not authorize a stronger claim at another. Correlation is not mechanism. Chart separation is not latent-state identification. Preserved latent ordering is not recovery of a local dynamical operator. Truth-known estimator recovery is not empirical biological validation.The remainder of the article is organized by four layers that will be used as an editorial filter when companion results are imported: state geometry, local dynamical geometry, geometry of possible futures, and control of accessibility. MNPS belongs primarily to the first layer. Much of the recent programme — local Jacobians, diffusion tensors, reachability, destination geometry, and finite-amplitude questions — belongs to the latter three.2.IntroductionNeural systems are often summarized by where they sit: a coordinate in a reduced state space, a complexity index, a spectral signature, a reported conscious level. Those summaries can be useful, and metastable state-space descriptions are a natural neighbourhood for the present work (Deco et al., 2017; Tognoli & Kelso, 2014). They are incomplete as an account of regime. Two systems can occupy similar locations and still differ in how nearby perturbations grow, how stochastic variation spreads, which future volumes remain reachable, which basins are favoured, and whether a finite kick can dislodge the current regime.Earlier NDT made that incompleteness only partly explicit. Its identity was rhythmic reconstruction: a Meta–Noetic Diffusion Model (MNDM) as guided stochastic flow, Rhythmic Variance Control (RVC) as a schedule on scalar noise amplitude, and MNPS as the Stage-1 chart in which reconstruction should become visible (Langell, 2025). The implied chain was approximatelyrhythmic control → variance scheduling → exploration or denoising → MNPS geometry.That chain remains a legitimate special case. It is no longer an adequate centre of gravity. The programme has since been forced, by both positive and negative companion results, to treat local flow, diffusion geometry, and accessible futures as first-class theoretical objects, and to treat measurement charts as operational rather than ontological.The revised chain is:state → local dynamical law → possible futures → state-dependent control of those futures.The core claim is therefore not merely that conscious-state correlates occupy characteristic regions of neural geometry. It is that the functional significance of a neural state depends partly on the structured set of transitions available from it.Figure 1: The NDT 1.0 architecture. A measured point 𝑥𝑡 is surrounded by local dynamical objects (drift, diffusion, Jacobian deformation, reachability, committor basins, finite-amplitude perturbation). Dashed bands separate control, latent dynamics, and measurement.Core questionHow should neural-state organization associated with cognition and conscious regimes be character­ized if location and complexity are insufficient — that is, if the scientifically relevant object includes local flow, stochastic spread, reachable futures, basin commitment, and the processes that reshape them — without mistaking a measurement chart for the latent process or a correlation for a dynamical operator?2.1.Three questionsThe framework is organized around three questions that later sections develop in turn.Where is the system? This is the measurement-chart question. A low-dimensional representation𝑥𝑡=[𝑚𝑡,𝑑𝑡,𝑒𝑡]⊤(1)or a stratified nine-dimensional refinement, reports an operational location. It answers a state question.What can happen from here? This is NDT proper. It includes deterministic drift, stochastic diffusion, transient amplification, short-horizon reachability, basin commitment, and finite-amplitude resilience. These objects answer different dynamical questions and are not interchangeable summaries of one another.What changes what can happen? This is the control layer: rhythms, candidate thalamic access, attention and context, history and replay, bodily state, metabolic feasibility, and — as an explicitly speculative micro substrate — slow structural accessibility at dendritic scale. Control may act on drift, diffusion, effective geometry, landscape, or transition gates, not only on a scalar noise amplitude.2.2.Noetic accessibilityNoetic accessibility, as used here, is a dynamical-systems concept: the structured, state-dependent set of dynamically attainable neural transitions and destinations under the current deterministic, stochastic, contextual, and physiological constraints. The adjective “noetic” retains its earlier sense of knowledge-bearing or intelli­gible structure (Langell, 2025). It does not mean conscious access in the global-workspace sense (Dehaene & Changeux, 2011). Reachable volume is not workspace ignition. Chart-level accessibility is not a report.3.The NDT scale and inference ladder3.1.Latent process, observation, and chartLet 𝑋𝑡 denote a latent neural dynamical state. Its exact dimensionality and biological substrate need not be known for the theory to be well posed as a modelling class. Measurements arise through an observation map𝑌𝑡=𝑔(𝑋𝑡)+𝜀𝑡,(2)where 𝑌𝑡 may be spikes, local field potentials, EEG, MEG, fMRI, or physiological channels, and 𝜀𝑡 is obser­vation noise. A subsequent feature and chart construction produces an operational representation𝑌𝑡→𝑧𝑡→𝑥𝑡.(3)The chart 𝑥𝑡 is a versioned summary of the measurement. It is not automatically the latent manifold. Different modalities, preprocessing decisions, neighbourhood definitions, and projections yield different charts, and their uncertainty is inherited by every later estimate of drift, Jacobian, diffusion, or reachability.3.2.Four levels of recoverabilityCompanion truth-known work on observability and local-operator recovery motivates an operational ladder that governs claim strength throughout this article:1.Association. Does an observable covary with another measured or latent quantity under stated nulls?2.Mechanism discrimination. Can observations distinguish alternative mechanisms that produce similar task-level outputs?3.Latent-order preservation. Does the measured representation preserve a predefined ordering of a latent summary?4.Dynamical-operator identification. Can the representation recover a local operator that correctly predicts responses to perturbation or short-horizon evolution?These levels are not a general nonlinear-observability theorem. They are a claim ladder. The ladder is not monotonic: a chart can discriminate mechanisms while failing to preserve order or to identify an operator, and an observation map can destroy a relation that was recoverable in a cleaner representation.A companion meso-to-macro analysis of simultaneous Neuropixels and surface EEG illustrates the rule rather than validating NDT geometry. Bounded associations between mesoscopic population summaries and EEG variance or spectral entropy survive temporal and statistical nulls at the level of association. The same EEG-only local-linear chart does not support Jacobian or reachability inference: the residual-variance fraction of the fitted local operator remains too large for operator identification. Cross-scale contact is therefore a Level-1 constraint. It is not common latent-state identity, unique NDT geometry, or a validated chart Jacobian.ConsequenceMNPS separations can be meaningful at Levels 1–3. Claims about Jacobians, diffusion tensors, reachability, committors, or finite-amplitude resilience require stronger validity conditions and fail closed when those conditions are not met.Figure 2: Observation chain and recoverability ladder. Let 𝑐𝑡 denote a contextual or control state. The latent dynamics are written𝑑𝑋𝑡=𝑏(𝑋𝑡,𝑡|𝑐𝑡)𝑑𝑡+Σ(𝑋𝑡,𝑡|𝑐𝑡)𝑑𝑊𝑡,(4)where 𝑏 is local deterministic drift, Σ determines the amplitude and orientation of stochastic forcing, and 𝑊𝑡 is Brownian motion. Equation 4 is a modelling class, not a fitted neural SDE. Data constrain a local generator more directly than they identify a unique decomposition into metric, potential, and noise, and the representation remains subject to chart changes, time reparametrization, and additive constants in any potential.4.2.Diffusion geometryDiffusion in this theory is not a scalar noise amplitude. Define the local diffusion tensor𝑎(𝑥,𝑡)=Σ(𝑥,𝑡)Σ(𝑥,𝑡)⊤.(5)The tensor 𝑎 determines the magnitude, effective dimensionality, anisotropy, and preferred directions of stochastic spread. Two states with identical mean drift may differ profoundly in what fluctuations permit. Scalar 𝜎(𝑡) is the isotropic special case.4.3.Potential-driven and non-conservative driftThe original landscape intuition is preserved as a structured component of drift, not as an assumption that every neural trajectory is gradient descent:𝑏(𝑥,𝑡|𝑐)=−𝐺(𝑥,𝑡|𝑐)∇Φ(𝑥,𝑡|𝑐)+𝑢nc(𝑥,𝑡|𝑐).(6)Here Φ is a state-dependent potential, 𝐺 is an effective metric or mobility geometry, and 𝑢nc collects non-conservative contributions: rotational flow, recurrent cycling, asymmetric transport, non-normal transient dynamics, and context-conditioned routing. Pure potential descent cannot naturally represent reactivity with decaying modes, rotational structure, or asymmetric access. The split in Eq. 6 keeps the landscape reading available while making those phenomena first-class.4.4.Rhythmic variance control as the minimal special caseRhythmic Variance Control remains the simplest accessibility-control mechanism (Langell, 2025). In the original scalar form,𝜎(𝑡)=𝜎min+𝜎0(1−𝑟(𝑡)),(7)with 𝑟(𝑡)∈[0,1] an operational rhythmic-control proxy: high 𝑟 lowers noise amplitude. The present framework retains Eq. 7 as a special case of𝑟(𝑡),𝑐(𝑡)→{𝑏𝑡,Σ𝑡,𝐺𝑡,transition gates}.(8)Rhythm may still schedule exploration versus stabilization. It is no longer asked to carry the whole theory. Stronger specificity claims are treated as control-layer hypotheses in Section 9, not as denoising proofs.5.Measured state: MNPS and the NeuralManifoldDynamics boundary5.1.Canonical and stratified chartsThe canonical three-dimensional observation chart remains Eq. 1. Operational families are unchanged in spirit: 𝑚 indexes mobility, metastability, or rhythmic coordination; 𝑑 indexes deviation from an integration–segrega­tion balance; 𝑒 indexes entropy or complexity pressure. These are chart semantics, not unique biological axes.A stratified nine-dimensional chart𝑥(9)𝑡=[𝑚𝑎,𝑚𝑒,𝑚𝑜,𝑑𝑛,𝑑𝑙,𝑑𝑠,𝑒𝑒,𝑒𝑠,𝑒𝑚]⊤(9)is retained as higher-resolution measurement, with recomposition into the three families through a release-fixed projection 𝑥𝑡=𝑃fixed𝑥(9)𝑡. The rationale is to reduce dimensional masking and to expose compensatory changes hidden in 3D. 𝑃fixed is an auditable operational contract, not a claim of unique ontology. Downstream local-dynamics claims inherit that contract.5.2.MNPS is not NDTMNPS provides coordinates in which some NDT-related quantities can be estimated. It does not exhaust NDT. The theory remains meaningful if another valid chart replaces MNPS, if some dynamical objects are more identifiable than the coordinates themselves, or if modality-specific charts differ. Identifying the latent state 𝑋𝑡 with (𝑚,𝑑,𝑒) is rejected as a homology.5.3.NeuralManifoldDynamics as a measurement contractNeuralManifoldDynamics (NMD) is a versioned empirical measurement contract that implements selected MNPS and local-dynamics quantities. MNPS is an observation chart through which some NDT quantities can be estimated, not the theory itself.NDT owns latent interpretation, the objects 𝑏, 𝑎, 𝐽, reachability, committor, and finite-amplitude questions, predicted dissociations, and mechanism hypotheses. NMD owns windows, transforms, coverage, feature-to-chart maps, the fixed projection 𝑃fixed, HDF5 export semantics, and particular estimators where implemented.In current NMD releases the three- and nine-dimensional charts are core serialized objects. A chart Jacobian 𝐽hat estimated in MNPS is an MNJ — a Jacobian in that chart — not a generic 𝐽(𝑋𝑡). Derived stability and reactivity summaries, when emitted, are per-recording mathematical measurements with provenance; they are not cohort statistics and not biological claims. Stochastic reachability is gated: derivative-residual covariance is not an admissible one-step noise tensor without an explicit conversion model, and production summarization does not currently emit computed reachability without that contract. Committor and finite-amplitude-resilience families, where present at all, are opt-in and refuse computation without explicit regime sets or perturbation protocols. Companion truth-known recovery of diffusion tensors and Jacobian summaries on linear SDEs does not, by itself, license literal ingest of the same quantities through the current NMD pipeline.5.4.Measurement inheritance ruleAny downstream inference inherits the validity limits of (i) raw measurement, (ii) preprocessing, (iii) feature construction, (iv) chart definition, and (v) local estimator. A plausible embedding does not cancel those limits.6.Local dynamical geometryGiven a representation in which a drift 𝑏 is defined, the local Jacobian is𝐽(𝑥,𝑡)=𝜕𝑏𝜕𝑥.(10)The generic object is this 𝐽. The term MNJ is reserved for a Jacobian estimated in an MNPS chart. 𝐽 asks how nearby perturbations change under the local flow. It does not ask where the system sits, how noise spreads, or which basin is favoured.Two derived summaries are conceptually distinct and must remain so. The spectral abscissa𝛼(𝐽)=max𝑖ℜ[𝜆𝑖(𝐽)](11)is the dominant modal growth or decay rate. The numerical abscissa𝜔(𝐽)=𝜆max(𝐽+𝐽⊤2)(12)is the maximal instantaneous growth rate. A system can satisfy 𝛼<0 while 𝜔>0: every eigenmode decays, yet some perturbations grow at short times. The reactivity gap Δreact=𝜔−𝛼 is a derived, non-normality-sensitive summary of that separation. It is not a complete measure of non-normality. The antisymmetric part Ω=𝐽−𝐽⊤2 supplies a rotational component distinct from reactivity.Finite-time amplification, for example a peak gain 𝐺peak(𝑇) of a local propagator over a horizon 𝑇, is a dif­ferent object again. Companion truth-known work recovers 𝛼, 𝜔, and Δreact on declared constant-linear systems under restricted envelopes, and shows that they are not interchangeable. Instantaneous Jacobian recovery does not automatically license ordered finite-time promotion, and simulator success does not automatically transfer to literal NMD ingest. Those boundaries stay in force here: “stable” is not a sufficient dynamical phenotype.7.Local diffusion geometryEquation 5 already makes diffusion a tensor. The questions it answers are orthogonal to those answered by 𝐽. Natural summaries include total diffusion 𝐷total=tr(𝑎), an effective diffusion dimensionality, a concentration or anisotropy index, and orientation-specific alignments relative to a declared probe direction.Two states can share a similar 𝐽 and differ in 𝑎, or share a similar 𝑎 and differ in 𝐽. That dissociation is part of what “diffusion” is supposed to mean in NDT. Companion truth-known recovery of 𝑎 on linear SDEs supports the object as a mathematical measurement under inspectable envelopes (support, timestep, bandwidth, obser­vation noise). Gauge matters: orthogonal rotations leave tensor summaries invariant to numerical tolerance, whereas nonuniform rescaling does not. Recovered 𝑎 is therefore chart-dependent unless a chart is declared. Empirical interpretation through NMD remains a separate translation problem; the current pipeline treats diffusion geometry as an opt-in family with explicit qualification gates, not as a silent by-product of Jacobian residuals.8.Geometry of possible futuresLocal linearization does not exhaust accessibility. This section collects objects that ask what can happen over a finite horizon, toward which destination, and after a perturbation that is no longer infinitesimal.8.1.ReachabilityShort-horizon reachability asks which nearby future states remain dynamically available. A local stochastic linearization𝑥𝑡+1=𝐴𝑡𝑥𝑡+𝑏𝑡+𝜀𝑡,𝜀𝑡∼(0,𝑄𝑡)(13)induces the covariance recursionΣ(ℎ+1)𝑡=𝐴𝑡Σ(ℎ)𝑡𝐴⊤𝑡+𝑄𝑡,(14)with Σ(0)𝑡=0. Eigenvalues of Σ(ℎ)𝑡 yield volume, anisotropy, and effective-dimension summaries of an ellip­soidal reachable set.Reachability is not occupancy, entropy, traversability, controllability, or consciousness. Large 𝑄𝑡 can inflate cones without richer controllable geometry. Horizon and neighbourhood leakage are validity conditions. Com­panion simulator work can calibrate aggregate covariance propagation while failing tests of memory-specific or target-private directional spread; calibration is not destination geometry. On the measurement side, NMD does not currently emit computed stochastic reachability without an admissible one-step residual contract. Empirical claims that invoke reachable futures therefore require their own estimator audit; they cannot be read off chart location.8.2.Committor geometryFor competing destinations 𝐴 and 𝐵, the committor𝑞(𝑥)=𝑃𝑥(𝜏𝐵<𝜏𝐴)(15)is the probability of hitting 𝐵 before 𝐴. It is destination geometry, not local expansion and not reachable volume. The object is theoretically central. Its empirical and even finite-sample status is severely bounded. One-dimensional local-law recovery can be qualified under restricted contracts; two-dimensional finite-sample recovery has not been licensed under the estimator families so far tested, and a product-level committor export in NMD is not authorized by that stop. Stage labels are not committor truth. The flagship therefore promotes the question and refuses silent computation.8.3.Attractors and finite-amplitude resilienceAttractor and basin structure — locations, depth, boundaries, corridors, metastability, escape — organize longer-run trajectories (Deco et al., 2017; Tognoli & Kelso, 2014). Visual clustering is not evidence of an attractor. Finite-amplitude resilience (FAR) asks how large a perturbation the system can tolerate before leaving its current dynamical regime. Local stability does not imply FAR; Jacobian or finite-time tangent gain does not substitute for an observed perturbation protocol. NMD retrospective ingest correctly reports FAR as not testable without such a protocol.8.4.Orthogonal dynamical questionsThe point of this hierarchy is not to brand a new metric for each row. The present hypothesis is weaker anatomically and stronger computationally: thalamocortical and thalamoreticular circuitry is a candidate access-control architecture. Equation 6 can specify motion given Φ. It does not say where Φ came from. A history-conditioned landscapeΦ(𝑥,𝑡|𝐻𝑡)(17)is the natural object for later transition probabilities, basin depth, corridor availability, and hysteresis. The original Hippocampal–Entorhinal Landscape System (HELS) programme asked whether replay and related hippocampal dynamics write that landscape. Current infrastructure and decoder-feasibility work on public hippocampal archives do not constitute evidence of landscape writing. That HELS question remains a specu­lative mechanistic subsystem.HELS-A is a different question, and collapsing the two names is a category error. HELS-A asks whether a context-conditioned access operator can change which routes remain available while Φ is held fixed:𝑑𝑋𝑡=−𝐴𝐻∇Φ(𝑋𝑡)𝑑𝑡+𝜎𝑑𝑊𝑡.(18)Companion truth-known development gates support that split on declared geometries: directional 𝐴𝐻 can saturate first-passage toward a context-matched target where speed-matched scalar gain does not, and an unsaturated double-well committor can be biased by context, reversed by the wrong context, and collapsed by shuffle. A neighbourhood extension failed its coded robustness gate and is not treated as a pass. Official confirmatory seeds remain unconsumed. The licensed statement is therefore narrow. A fixed stored potential does not uniquely determine route accessibility: Φ fixed and 𝑋0 fixed do not imply 𝑞(𝑋0) fixed when 𝐴𝐻 changes. That is not a hippocampal, thalamic, memory, or MNPS result. It also does not answer “where does Φ come from?” HELS-A shows that the landscape problem and the access problem can come apart.The biological HELS-A1 gap is still open. A simultaneous CA1–prefrontal candidate archive can be struc­turally eligible at the header level — region labels, trial clocks, and behavioural 𝑈𝑡 sufficient to attempt a later design — without that eligibility being a mechanism. Fabricated-estimator qualification for a state-matched incremental test of residual hippocampal information about future cortical direction has not passed family false-positive control. Conditional predictive information is not causal access. Incomplete observation of current state and of task/context state are identifiability ceilings, not evidence that CA1 implements 𝐴𝐻. Spike-time scoring is closed until an estimator is independently qualified. Header yield is not a hippocampal result.A separate empirical observation — that true recent history can improve linear prediction of post-stimulation measured EEG beyond the current measured state on one meso-to-macro contract — is a P6-adjacent measured-state constraint. It is not HELS landscape writing and not HELS-A1.9.4.Embodied state and metabolic feasibilityBodily signals are treated as context variables that may condition accessibility, not as coordinates of consciousness. Fast candidates include cardiac and respiratory phase, autonomic state, interoception, and heartbeat-evoked responses. Companion work can support bounded autonomic–chart co-modulation without identifying causal body-to-manifold gating. Any such coupling belongs in 𝑐body𝑡, not in (𝑚,𝑑,𝑒) by default.Slower physiology is a feasibility boundary rather than another rhythmic anchor. Let 𝑞met(𝑡) denote metabolic adequacy. A trajectory may be geometrically available in the abstract model yet physiologically unavailable because the substrate cannot support it. Metabolism is not a consciousness scalar, not an MNPS axis, and not equivalent to arousal. Equations that fold 𝑞met into an effective rhythmic schedule remain speculative extensions for later tests.9.5.Slow structural accessibility at dendritic scaleAccessibility control is not confined a priori to thalamic or EEG scales. A companion branch-resolved memory simulator separates fast dendritic access from a slower phenomenological bias 𝑀𝑏, with effective access𝐴𝑏(𝑡)=𝐴𝑓𝑏(𝑡)𝐴𝑠𝑏(𝑀𝑏(𝑡)).(19)Replay-dependent updating of 𝑀𝑏 can reproduce a joint profile of overlap-branch strengthening, linking gain, perturbation-sensitive loss, and targeted rescue that tested comparators do not reproduce, inside a bounded write–replay and non-hub motif regime. The flagship import is conceptual: the same logical move — control changing futures without rewriting content — can be stated at the dendrite, and the micro–meso–macro chain is still mostly dashed.10.Empirical constraintsNo companion result validates the NDT chain end to end. The role of evidence here is to constrain layers, not to define the theory. Three anchors enter the main text because they answer different questions in the hierarchy of Table 1. Everything else is summarized as structured dissociation rather than narrated dataset by dataset.Companion truth-known simulations, already used to bound 𝑎, 𝛼, and 𝜔 in Section 6 and Section 7, belong in this map as methodological qualification: they ask whether nominated mathematical objects recover known latent targets under inspectable envelopes. They do not identify simulator coordinates with MNPS and they do not replace empirical anchors.10.1.Anchor 1: spindle-locked state reconfigurationThe first question is whether a physiologically discrete rhythmic event coincides with local chart reconfiguration — not whether spindles prove denoising, memory writing, or a thalamic variance clamp.A companion three-dataset analysis of N2 spindles on a frozen stratified MNPS export finds a replicable within-event rise in the network-deviation coordinate 𝑑𝑛 when the spindle window is compared with a far pre-event baseline (ANPHY high-density EEG, 𝑁=27; BOAS / OpenNeuro ds005555, 𝑁=128) (López-Larraz et al., 2025; Wei et al., 2024). Effect sizes on that timing contrast are modest (𝑑𝑛 shifts of order 0.11–0.17 after FDR). A third overnight polysomnography sample is underpowered for the same timing estimand and is treated as corroboration, not as an independent timing replication. Jacobian, diffusion-tensor, and reachability endpoints were not the primary objects.Matched-control specificity is incomplete. Several family endpoints are dominated by baseline differences rather than by the event increment, and the event-versus-matched-control sign for 𝑑𝑛 conflicts across datasets for that reason. Autonomic composites do not moderate the geometry effect under the primary protocol. Sigma-band power residualization was not part of the frozen schema.Anchor 1 ceilingDiscrete spindle events coincide with a replicable, timing-locked rise in stratified 𝑑𝑛. That is chart-level state reconfiguration at a rhythm-defined micro-event (recoverability Level 1). It is not causal denoising, not a pure spindle-versus-matched-N2 mechanism, not a reachability change, and not TRN identification.10.2.Anchor 2: recovery-linked geometry after cardiac-arrest comaThe second question is whether a clinically important state difference appears more clearly in accessible futures and local flow than in static location alone.A companion pooled analysis of early post-arrest EEG from I-CARE PhysioNet 2.1 evaluates MNPS spread, local Jacobian deformation, and short-horizon reachability jointly in two independently processed, non-overlapping cohorts (301 analyzable recordings; 281 in the CPC 1 versus CPC 5 contrast; window 0–12 h after the EEG clock, not the ROSC clock) (Amorim et al., 2023). All five prespecified reachability summaries survive BH-FDR in the pooled sample, led by persistence and log-det volume proxies (absolute Cohen 𝑑 near 0.9). Local Jacobian summaries are directionally aligned — lower expansion and deformation in later-good-outcome recordings — but heterogeneous in magnitude across cohorts. Static MNPS spread is lower in better-outcome patients and is restored as a descriptive anchor, not as the lead. The leading reachability separator is already detectable in the first analyzable hour; a subject-wise circular-shift null does not support a claim about which hour is uniquely strongest. Adding the geometry layer to a feature-limited clinical-plus-qEEG baseline raises internal AUROC from 0.599 to 0.662, a bounded increment that is not multimodal bedside prognostication.Because withdrawal-of-life-sustaining-therapy, time-to-death, and sedation annotations were unavailable, the contrast is recovery-linked geometry, not a pure viability assay and not a consciousness meter.Anchor 2 ceilingEarly post-arrest EEG contains layered, recovery-linked geometry: short-horizon reachability and local deformation carry information that static severity/location does not exhaust. These are empirical NMD-chart estimators. Residual quality and perturbation-response were not the study design, so the result motivates the accessible-futures layer. It does not constitute truth-known dynamical-operator identifica­tion.10.3.Anchor 3: meso-to-macro contact without operator recoveryThe third question is methodological and was already used as a worked example in Section 3. Jacobian and reachability inference on that chart are therefore rejected.The pair is the epistemic spine of the empirical map. Cross-scale contact is real at association. It is not common latent-state identity, unique NDT geometry, causal direction, TRN identification, or a validated MNJ. A visually plausible chart plus a correlation does not climb the recoverability ladder.Anchor 3 ceilingMeso and macro observables can covary under stimulation-aligned windows after temporal and statistical nulls (Level 1). Its defensible claim is narrower: it is a framework for dynamical accessibility and a versioned measurement programme on which predictions from several of those theories can be expressed and, in places, cross-checked (Storm et al., 2024). The comparison that follows is therefore a map of partly different questions, not a contest for a single title. A longer thematic treatment of the same neighbourhood exists as a companion essay; this section imports only the bridges and non-equivalences that constrain NDT 1.0.PositioningIIT proposes what consciousness is claimed to be. GNWT proposes when content becomes globally available. NDT proposes how neural trajectories are locally generated, spread, gated, and made more or less reachable. Those are not interchangeable answers to one question.11.1.Different questions, not rival scalars“What does consciousness correspond to?” is not a single question. Neighbouring frameworks answer at least six different ones: whether experience is present (level); what is experienced (content); whether information is available to report and action (access); whether the system represents itself as being in a state (meta-awareness); whether experience is embodied; and what range of neural transitions remains available (dynamical capacity). NDT addresses the last of these. It does not currently identify NCC proper, and its charts are closest to enabling state dynamics — NCC prerequisites and the dynamical context around experience — rather than to report, confidence, or phenomenal identity.Theories can therefore be nested as explanatory layers without being jointly validated (Storm et al., 2024). Predictive inference can supply candidate content (Clark, 2013; Friston, 2010). Recurrent processing can stabi­lize sensory representations (Lamme, 2006; 2010). A workspace can broadcast selected content (Baars, 1988; Dehaene & Changeux, 2011; Mashour et al., 2020). Higher-order or attention-schema systems can represent aspects of that access (Brown et al., 2019; Lau & Rosenthal, 2011; Webb & Graziano, 2015). Embodied inference can bias the background state (Seth, 2013). Dynamical geometry can describe the trajectory through which those processes unfold. Structural network control can constrain which transitions are cheap or costly (Gu et al., 2015; Tang & Bassett, 2018). Compatibility of layers is not evidence that every theory is true.11.2.Closest family: landscapes, metastability, and controlNDT’s nearest relatives are dynamical-landscape, metastability, and network-control accounts (Deco et al., 2011; Deco et al., 2017; Gu et al., 2015; Kelso, 2012; Tognoli & Kelso, 2014). They already deny that regional activity magnitude is a sufficient description. NDT inherits that denial and adds five commitments that those families do not jointly require:1.Stochastic diffusion geometry as a first-class local object 𝑎(𝑥,𝑡), not only noise around an attractor.2.Accessibility decomposition: location, local flow, stochastic spread, reachability, commitment, and finite-amplitude resilience answer different questions (Table 1).3.Explicit measurement contracts: MNPS and NeuralManifoldDynamics are operational charts and estima­tors, not unique latent identification.4.State-dependent control, of which rhythmic variance scheduling is the minimal special case, not the whole theory.5.Recoverability discipline: association does not license a local operator (Section 3).Network control theory shares the concern with possible transitions. NDT does not currently assume an explicit input matrix 𝐵 or minimize control energy. Its reachability objects are empirical, chart-level, and short-horizon; they are not Gramian controllability of a structural connectome. Metastability shares the concern with a regime between lock-in and fragmentation. MNPS 𝑚 is an operational mobility/coordination proxy, not a Kuramoto order parameter computed from pairwise phase relations. Landscape models share potential descent. In NDT that descent is a structured component of drift, not the general law: non-conservative flow, non-normal transients, and anisotropic diffusion remain in 𝑢nc and 𝑎.The claim “conscious states have geometry” is therefore not distinctive. The testable NDT increment is that neural variability and transition structure are actively controlled, and that the resulting local flow, diffusion, and accessible futures can contain information not recoverable from location, power, or generic complexity alone.11.3.Constitutive, access, and inference theoriesFrameworkPossible bridgeNon-equivalencePP / FEPprecision; landscape descent; state es­timationfitted accessibility is not a generative model with explicit priorsIITboth reject activity magnitude as suffi­cientNDT does not identify consciousness with Φ or intrinsic causal structureGNWTaccess geometry may enable or disable global availabilitydynamical reachability is not workspace ignitionRPTrotational / local recurrent flow Ωchart-level rotation is not anatomical feedbackHOTnone proposedNDT does not model representation-of-represen­tationASTattention-related coordinates as possi­ble inputsan attention proxy is not an attention schemaLandscapes / metastabil­ityclosest dynamical familyNDT adds diffusion geometry, control, and mea­surement contractsNetwork controlpossible transitions; future state spaceno current 𝐵 matrix or control-energy objectiveTable 4: Bridges are analogies unless a shared formal object is identified. The relevant comparison is a specified generative model, not the umbrella principle. NDT’s emphasis is measured, state-dependent transition structure. A fitted drift term is not variational free energy, and 𝑟(𝑡) is not precision by another name.Integrated information. IIT identifies consciousness with irreducible intrinsic cause–effect structure, quantified as Φ (Oizumi et al., 2014; Tononi et al., 2016). Perturbational complexity is clinically important and IIT-inspired, but it is not an empirical calculation of Φ (Casali et al., 2013). NDT refuses a structural “amount of consciousness” scalar of that kind. Shared rejection of simple magnitude is not a shared ontology.Global workspace. GNWT targets access: information becomes globally available after a competitive ignition-like transition (Baars, 1988; Dehaene & Changeux, 2011; Mashour et al., 2020). Dynamical accessi­bility in NDT may describe conditions that make such a transition more or less available. It does not model broadcast, report, or workspace competition. Reachable volume is not ignition.Recurrent processing. RPT locates a threshold in recurrent sensory processing rather than in global broadcast (Lamme, 2006; 2010). NDT’s antisymmetric Jacobian Ω is compatible with rotational or recurrent flow. Compatibility is not anatomy: a chart rotation does not establish feedback in sensory cortex, and recurrence is not treated as sufficient for experience.Higher-order theories and attention schema theory. These target meta-representation and a simplified internal model of attention (Brown et al., 2019; Lau & Rosenthal, 2011; Webb & Graziano, 2015). NDT currently has no corresponding state variable. Metacognitive sensitivity and awareness attribution are neighbouring empirical programmes, not NDT objects.11.4.Lessons that apply across theoriesTwo external results discipline every framework in this neighbourhood, including NDT.The Cogitate Consortium’s preregistered adversarial collaboration did not declare a winner between IIT and GNWT (Cogitate Consortium et al., 2025). Predicted posterior synchronization and predicted prefrontal offset ignition were both challenged in the specified form. The methodological lesson is prior commitment: mature theories become stronger when they state in advance what would count against them. NDT has not undergone an adversarial test of comparable independence or scale.State discrimination is not detection of experience. Complexity measures can separate wakefulness from sedation while failing to distinguish reported dreaming from no reported experience within the sedated condition (Bajwa et al., 2025). PCI, ignition markers, and NDT reachability may all index regimes that make experience more or less likely. None is licensed as a consciousness meter by that fact. Companion coma geometry is recovery-linked capacity (Section 10), not a within-state experience assay.12.Predictions and failure conditionsNDT is revised by layer, not as a single slogan. It currently adds no discriminating empirical prediction beyond the dynamical model.13.Discussion13.1.From denoising to accessibilityNDT began as a theory of rhythmic reconstruction: noise, coherence, a learned potential, a variance schedule, and a geometric state chart. That identity is not discarded. It is no longer the centre. The scientifically load-bearing object is now the local dynamical neighbourhood of a neural state — what the system tends to do, how fluctuations spread, which nearby futures remain open, and which processes change those possibilities.The original emphasis was thereforenoise → coherence → denoising → rhythmic schedule → geometric state.The revised emphasis isstate → local law → perturbation → possible futures → control of those futures.That is a change of question, not a relabeling of MNPS. Location remains necessary. It is incomplete as an account of organization. Companion evidence already forces the shift: spindle events reconfigure a stratified chart without licensing causal denoising; early post-arrest EEG separates more clearly on reachable futures and local deformation than on static spread; meso-to-macro contact does not recover a local operator (Section 10). The theory follows those constraints rather than protecting an earlier slogan.13.2.What is retained, promoted, and separatedRetained from the original programme are the exploration–stabilization trade-off, learned landscapes as a struc­tured component of drift, rhythmic control as a distinctive temporal hypothesis, geometric state representations, metastability as a neighbouring dynamical regime, and explicit layered falsification.Promoted to first-class objects are the diffusion tensor, the local Jacobian and its stability/reactivity sum­maries, short-horizon reachability, committor geometry, finite-amplitude resilience, and attractor/basin structure — each as an answer to a different dynamical question, not as interchangeable complexity scores (Table 1).Separated from the theory proper is NeuralManifoldDynamics. Charts and estimators are versioned empir­ical realizations. They can fail without making dynamical accessibility meaningless, and they can succeed as measurements without identifying the latent law. Generalized from a scalar schedule is accessibility control: rhythm may act on 𝜎(𝑡), but context, history, embodiment, and metabolism may act on drift, mobility, landscape, or transition gates (Section 9). RVC remains the minimal special case.Demoted, relative to earlier drafts, are a consciousness scalar, a universal clinical fingerprint, Mapper/topology-as-theory, and any reading in which MNPS location is the NDT claim.13.3.Why this may matterA conscious brain state may not be distinguished purely by complexity, entropy, synchrony, occupancy, or global activity. Candidate regimes may also differ in what the system remains capable of becoming next. That is a hypothesis about the organization of neural states associated with wakefulness, sleep, anesthesia, task engagement, and pathology. It is not an identity claim about experience, not a meter of awareness, and not a solution to the hard problem.The hypothesis is useful only if the layers dissociate. If reachability, local flow, and diffusion were redundant encodings of location or power, the revised centre would collapse. The present empirical map is not a validation of the chain. It is evidence that the collapse has not already occurred in every tested regime, and that different conditions move different layers (Table 3). Consciousness research already contains neighbouring theories of content, access, recurrence, and intrinsic causal structure (Section 11). NDT’s contribution, if it holds, is a measurement surface for dynamical capacity and its control — a surface on which those theories can make distinct predictions, not a replacement for them.The next informative experiment is therefore not another broad disease contrast. It is a design that separates global state, responsiveness, reportability, and momentary experience, and that asks whether rhythm-locked or context-locked changes in local flow, diffusion, and short-horizon reachability explain future trajectories beyond power, generic complexity, and stationary dynamics (Section 12). A positive result would still support a neural mechanism, not the claim that reconstruction is consciousness.14.LimitationsNo dataset currently validates the NDT chain end to end. Many nominated objects remain estimator-sensitive: MNPS is operational and chart-dependent; drift and diffusion are identifiable only under explicit assumptions; local linearization fails in important empirical regimes, including the EEG chart tested on DANDI 000458; reachability depends on horizon and noise model; committor estimation requires meaningful destinations that current measurement contracts do not emit; and finite-amplitude resilience depends on perturbation family and amplitude scale.Biological bridges remain uneven. Rhythmic variance control is a distinctive prediction, not a demonstrated executor. Thalamic access has synthetic operator existence and associative biology, not circuit identification. History-dependent landscape writing is open; access on a frozen landscape is a computational existence result, not scored hippocampal mechanism. Embodiment and metabolism are contextual and feasibility constraints, not consciousness coordinates. Dendritic slow-branch accessibility is a simulator discriminator and a speculative micro substrate.Empirical companions are bounded. Sleep, coma, and meso-to-macro analyses constrain selected layers in selected cohorts. Clinical fingerprints are not biomarkers. Treatment-limitation and sedation confounds remain unresolved where they were unavailable. NDT has not undergone an independent adversarial test comparable to Cogitate. Apparent geometric effects must still survive alternative charts, power-matched and temporally shuffled nulls, and standard spectral, connectivity, and complexity baselines.These limits are scope, not temporary embarrassment. NDT is a framework for neural dynamical organi­zation associated with conscious-state regimes. It does not explain why those dynamics are accompanied by experience.15.ConclusionNoetic Diffusion Theory proposes that neural-state organization is incompletely described by state position alone. A neural state also carries a local dynamical structure: deterministic tendencies, stochastic directions, transient sensitivities, reachable futures, basin commitments, and finite-amplitude resilience. Those structures are themselves modulated by rhythm, context, history, physiology, and candidate access-control systems. MNPS supplies one measurable chart through which this organization can be studied. NeuralManifoldDynamics supplies one implementation rather than the definition of the theory. Rhythmic Variance Control supplies the minimal control case rather than the whole mechanism.The resulting framework predicts structured dissociations among location, stability, diffusion, accessibility, commitment, and resilience, and treats those dissociations as empirically testable correlates of changing neural regimes — not as a scalar of consciousness, and not as a complete explanation of experience. What a neural state is, scientifically, includes what it can still become.16.AcknowledgementsDataset contributors, pipeline authors, and archival records will be acknowledged in full at submission.
