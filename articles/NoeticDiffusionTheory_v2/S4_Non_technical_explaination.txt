    S4 Conceptual Guide to Dynamical Accessibility                      Where a neural state sits is not the same as what it can still becomeArticle type: Supplementary MaterialShort title: S4 Conceptual guide - NDTKeywords: accessibility, conceptual guide, NDT, possible futuresRobin Langell11 Langell Konsult AB, Vallentuna, SwedenFor correspondence: Robin Langell, hello@noeticdiffusion.comAbstract  A conceptual reading of Noetic Diffusion Theory. The central move is that a map of the present              is not the law governing what happens next: the same measured location need not imply the same          dynamical future. Location, local flow, stochastic spread, destination structure, and control of access            are different questions about that law. The measurement chart is a map, not the land. Rhythmic            control is one candidate control mechanism, not the whole theory.Who this is forThis note is a conceptual decoder for the article, not a watered-down second theory. The metaphors are borrowedfrom a longer essay written for a general audience. The claims are those of the present article. In particular, NDTis not identified with rhythmic denoising, with a consciousness scalar, or with the measurement chart calledMeta–Noetic Phase Space (MNPS).  One sentence    A useful description of a neural state includes not only where it is, but what can still happen from   there, and what processes change those possibilities.             Figure 1: Reading map for this note. Each panel is a different question about the same moment.Same place, different futuresYou wake, and for a second the room is only shapes. Then the bedside table returns, the day returns, and youare again a person in a place. Something in the brain took ambiguous input and assembled a usable world.   That moment is a good intuition pump. It is not a proof. Earlier NDT used rhythmic reconstruction as itsprimary explanatory framing. The present formulation asks a broader dynamical question: given a measuredstate now, what distribution of nearby futures remains available, and what can reshape that distribution?   Two people can occupy similar coordinates on a coarse map and still differ in what happens next. One canleave a shock and return to ordinary life; another stays in the same valley. A weather map that only reports“here” cannot tell those stories apart. NDT keeps “here” and “what can happen from here” as separate scientificobjects                  (Langell, 2026a).Figure 2: Same measured state, different future law. Matching the present pin does not imply matching drift, stochastic spread,                               destination probabilities, or finite-horizon transition structure.That is C1, and much of C2, in one picture. The pin on a reduced map need not determine the law of nearbyfutures. In some distinct neural regimes, summaries of that future law carry information that location andordinary spectral scores do not.One state, several questionsImagine a hiker on a ridge in fog. A GPS pin answers one question: where? That pin does not answer the others.1. Where is the system? A low-dimensional chart — in this theory, MNPS, with axes that operationally   track mobility or coordination (𝑚), deviation from an integration–segregation balance (𝑑), and entropy or   complexity pressure (𝑒) — reports a location. A nine-dimensional refinement adds texture, as elevation is   not the same as geology. Two recordings can occupy similar 3D coordinates while differing in local flow,   stochastic spread, or short-horizon prediction. The chart is a map. It is not the latent neural process, and it    is not NDT.2. Which way does the ground slope? Local drift is the systematic tendency of motion: downhill on a potential,   around a cycle, or along some other flow.3. What happens to a small nudge? A Jacobian describes how the local flow changes around the point: if two   nearby states are nudged apart, does their separation grow, shrink, shear, or rotate? Drift is which way the   hiker is already being carried; the Jacobian is what happens to nearby hikers. Current Jacobian summaries   here are chart-level measurements. They are not intrinsic tensors of a unique brain manifold.4. How does the weather spread? Stochastic diffusion is not a single noise knob. It has size, shape, and   preferred directions. Two states can share a slope and differ in how fluctuations fan out — a narrow corridor   versus a broad fan.5. What nearby futures remain likely? Short-horizon predictive spread is a second-moment picture of those   futures, not classical control-theoretic reachability and not a list of every physically possible brain state. A   large occupied cloud on the map is not the same as a wide horizon of possible next moves.6. Toward which destination? A committor asks a different question still: if two basins compete, which one    is favoured?7. What if the shove is no longer tiny? Finite-amplitude resilience asks whether a larger displacement changes   the regime.These objects can come apart. That is why they must not be collapsed into a single “dynamical complexity”score. The main article’s Figure 3 is the formal                                   companion; this panel is the hiker version.The theory’s three commitments, in the same language:• C1. The pin on a reduced map need not determine the law of nearby futures.• C2 (non-redundant accessibility). In some distinct neural regimes, summaries of that future law carry  information that location and ordinary spectral scores do not.• C3. Context, history, or physiology can change the future law among observations that sit at the same pin.A coarse map losing information is not, by itself, evidence for NDT. The stronger test is whether the missingfuture structure can be measured reproducibly and whether defined conditions systematically reshape it. C3 isnot the generic observation that extra covariates improve prediction. It asks whether nominated physiologicalor contextual variables change defined features of the future law at a matched pin.   That is the whole load-bearing structure. Rhythm, thalamus, sleep spindles, coma geometry, and body signalsare candidate ways of changing the law. They are not extra definitions of NDT.The map is not the landBrain recordings — EEG, MEG, spikes, field potentials — are observations of a latent process we do not fullysee. A pipeline then builds features and projects them onto a chart. NeuralManifoldDynamics is one versionedcontract for doing that. When the contract changes, the version number should change. A pretty embedding plusa correlation is still only association. Recovering a local dynamical operator — something that correctly predictsa short future or a perturbation — is a much stronger claim, and on at least one meso-to-macro recording it failseven when coarser association succeeds.   Think of a subway map. It is invaluable for getting across town. It is a terrible geological survey. MNPSis closer to the subway map. Using it as if it were the terrain is how a measurement chart gets mistaken for atheory of mind.Figure 4: The map is useful precisely because it is not the land. C3 is the plain-language question: what canreshape the distribution without moving the present point? The theory groups those somethings into a fewclasses, not a catalogue of subsystems.Figure 5: Conditional accessibility. Context can change the future law at matched present state. In the strongest version of the             example, even the underlying landscape may remain unchanged while access to routes changes.Fast rhythmic and access control. The simplest special case is still Rhythmic Variance Control: a rhythmicproxy that turns isotropic noise up or down, as a conductor might ask the orchestra for forte or piano. Thatis one slot in the local law. It is not a proof that sleep spindles write memory, clamp variance, or implementconsciousness. Thalamic circuitry is a candidate access architecture — a possible way to rotate spread or openchannels — not an identified biological executor.    History. A stored landscape of preferences is one question: where did the hills come from? A differentquestion is whether, with the hills held fixed, a context operator can still change which routes are easy.   Physiology. Heartbeat, breath, and metabolic adequacy may condition what is feasible, as a bass line condi­tions a room without composing the melody. They are not extra MNPS axes and not consciousness coordinates.Autonomic composites did not moderate the primary spindle-geometry effect under the tested protocol.Structure is not accessNDT repeatedly separates what is stored or structurally available from what is dynamically accessible now.Changing access need not require rewriting the underlying structure.   That distinction is not only a hippocampal story. It is the same logical move at several scales: memorycontent versus retrieval; a potential 𝑉 versus mobility or gating; a stored landscape versus a context-conditionedroute; an attractor versus a committor; a dendritic substrate versus which branch is currently easy. The currentlyaudited hippocampal datasets have not established landscape writing. The access-on-frozen-landscape questionis a different question, and it remains a question.What the companion studies actually showedThese are constraints, not a validation of the chain.   Spindles. In two overnight EEG datasets, a discrete spindle event coincides with a modest, timing-lockedrise in one stratified chart coordinate (𝑑𝑛). That is event-locked state reconfiguration. It is not causal denoising,not a complete spindle-versus-matched-N2 mechanism, and not thalamic identification. Matched-control speci­ficity is incomplete                  (Langell, 2026j).   Coma. In early EEG after cardiac arrest, short-horizon spread and local deformation carry recovery-linkedinformation that static location does not exhaust. Geometry added a bounded increment on a feature-limitedclinical-plus-qEEG baseline. Withdrawal of life-sustaining therapy and sedation were not available. Read asrecovery-linked geometry, not as a viability assay and not as a consciousness meter                  (Langell,2026k).   Meso to macro. Simultaneous Neuropixels and surface EEG can covary under careful nulls. The same EEG-only chart does not recover a local flow or reachability operator. Contact across scales is real at association. Itis not operator identification                  (Langell, 2026b).   Other companions — depression, psychosis, gambling versus cognitive control, hypoglycaemia, infant age— mainly show that different conditions move different layers. That pattern is the result. Reduced spread isnot itself reduced consciousness. Complexity scores can separate global state from reported experience withina state; NDT has not been shown to do better (Bajwa et al., 2025).What this is not• Not a bedside score, CPC substitute, or test of awareness.• Not Integrated Information Theory’s Φ, workspace ignition, or a report.• Not the claim that “noetic” means knowledge-bearing structure in the coordinates. The name marks the  intended domain of mind-relevant dynamics.• Not a completed theory of why any of this should feel like anything.• Not a licence to treat every named object (committor, finite-amplitude resilience, dendritic access) as an  empirical export. Several remain questions.If, after matching state, responsiveness, arousal, power, and complexity, the future law still failed to distinguishreported experience from no reported experience, NDT would remain a theory of enabling dynamics. That testis named in the main text.
