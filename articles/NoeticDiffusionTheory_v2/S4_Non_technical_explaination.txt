S4 Non-technical ExplanationWhere a neural state sits is not the same as what it can still becomeArticle type: Supplementary MaterialShort title: S4 Plain language - NDTKeywords: accessibility, plain language, NDT, possible futuresRobin Langell11 Langell Konsult AB, Vallentuna, SwedenFor correspondence: Robin Langell, hello@noeticdiffusion.comAbstractA conceptual reading of Noetic Diffusion Theory. The central move is that a map of the present is not the law governing what happens next: the same measured location need not imply the same dynamical future. Location, local flow, stochastic spread, destination structure, and control of access are different questions about that law. The measurement chart is a map, not the land. Rhythm is one controller, not the whole theory.Who this is forThis note is a conceptual decoder for the article, not a watered-down second theory. The metaphors are borrowed from a longer essay written for a general audience. The claims are those of the present article. In particular, NDT is not identified with rhythmic denoising, with a consciousness scalar, or with the measurement chart called Meta–Noetic Phase Space (MNPS).One sentenceA useful description of a neural state includes not only where it is, but what can still happen from there, and what processes change those possibilities.Figure 1: Reading map for this note. Each panel is a different question about the same moment.Same place, different futuresYou wake, and for a second the room is only shapes. Then the bedside table returns, the day returns, and you are again a person in a place. Something in the brain took ambiguous input and assembled a usable world.That moment is a good intuition pump. It is not a proof. Earlier NDT used rhythmic reconstruction as its primary explanatory framing. The present formulation asks a broader dynamical question: given a measured state now, what distribution of nearby futures remains available, and what can reshape that distribution?Two people can occupy similar coordinates on a coarse map and still differ in what happens next. One can leave a shock and return to ordinary life; another stays in the same valley. A weather map that only reports “here” cannot tell those stories apart. NDT keeps “here” and “what can happen from here” as separate scientific objects (Langell, 2026a).Figure 2: Same measured state, different future law. Matching the present pin does not imply matching drift, stochastic spread, destination probabilities, or finite-horizon transition structure.That is C1, and much of C2, in one picture. The pin on a reduced map need not determine the law of nearby futures. In some distinct neural regimes, summaries of that future law carry information that location and ordinary spectral scores do not.One state, several questionsImagine a hiker on a ridge in fog. A GPS pin answers one question: where? That pin does not answer the others.1.Where is the system? A low-dimensional chart — in this theory, MNPS, with axes that operationally track mobility or coordination (𝑚), deviation from an integration–segregation balance (𝑑), and entropy or complexity pressure (𝑒) — reports a location. A nine-dimensional refinement adds texture, as elevation is not the same as geology. Two minds can share a 3D pin and differ in whether information is travelling locally or globally. The chart is a map. It is not the latent neural process, and it is not NDT.2.Which way does the ground slope? Local drift is the systematic tendency of motion: downhill on a potential, around a cycle, or along some other flow.3.What happens to a small nudge? A Jacobian is a map of local slopes — how a small perturbation is amplified, damped, or rotated. It is the difference between standing on granite and standing on scree. Current Jacobian summaries here are chart-level measurements. They are not intrinsic tensors of a unique brain manifold.4.How does the weather spread? Stochastic diffusion is not a single noise knob. It has size, shape, and preferred directions. Two states can share a slope and differ in how fluctuations fan out — a narrow corridor versus a broad fan.5.What nearby futures remain likely? Short-horizon predictive spread is a second-moment picture of those futures, not classical control-theoretic reachability and not a list of every physically possible brain state. A large occupied cloud on the map is not the same as a wide horizon of possible next moves.6.Toward which destination? A committor asks a different question still: if two basins compete, which one is favoured?7.What if the shove is no longer tiny? Finite-amplitude resilience asks whether a larger displacement changes the regime.These objects can come apart. That is why they must not be collapsed into a single “dynamical complexity” score.Figure 3: The same moment supports non-equivalent dynamical questions. The main article’s Figure 3 is the formal companion; this panel is the hiker version.The theory’s three commitments, in the same language:•C1. The pin on a reduced map need not determine the law of nearby futures.•C2 (non-redundant accessibility). In some distinct neural regimes, summaries of that future law carry information that location and ordinary spectral scores do not.•C3. Context, history, or physiology can change the future law among observations that sit at the same pin.That is the whole load-bearing structure. Rhythm, thalamus, sleep spindles, coma geometry, and body signals are candidate ways of changing the law. They are not extra definitions of NDT.The map is not the landBrain recordings — EEG, MEG, spikes, field potentials — are observations of a latent process we do not fully see. A pipeline then builds features and projects them onto a chart. NeuralManifoldDynamics is one versioned contract for doing that. When the contract changes, the version number should change. A pretty embedding plus a correlation is still only association. Recovering a local dynamical operator — something that correctly predicts a short future or a perturbation — is a much stronger claim, and on at least one meso-to-macro recording it fails even when coarser association succeeds.Think of a subway map. It is invaluable for getting across town. It is a terrible geological survey. MNPS is closer to the subway map. Using it as if it were the terrain is how a measurement chart gets mistaken for a theory of mind.Figure 4: The map is useful precisely because it is not the land. Estimates of local dynamics are made through the chart, not as transparent views of the latent process.Same place, different controlsIf the present pin does not fix the future law, something else can. C3 is the plain-language question: what can reshape the distribution without moving the present point? The theory groups those somethings into a few classes, not a catalogue of subsystems.Figure 5: Conditional accessibility. Context can change the future law at matched present state. In the strongest version of the example, even the underlying landscape may remain unchanged while access to routes changes.Fast rhythmic and access control. The simplest special case is still Rhythmic Variance Control: a rhythmic proxy that turns isotropic noise up or down, as a conductor might ask the orchestra for forte or piano. That is one slot in the local law. It is not a proof that sleep spindles write memory, clamp variance, or implement consciousness. Thalamic circuitry is a candidate access architecture — a possible way to rotate spread or open channels — not an identified biological executor.History. A stored landscape of preferences is one question: where did the hills come from? A different question is whether, with the hills held fixed, a context operator can still change which routes are easy.Physiology. Heartbeat, breath, and metabolic adequacy may condition what is feasible, as a bass line condi­tions a room without composing the melody. They are not extra MNPS axes and not consciousness coordinates. Spindle geometry here is not explained away by autonomic composites under the primary protocol.Structure is not accessNDT repeatedly separates what is stored or structurally available from what is dynamically accessible now. Changing access need not require rewriting the underlying structure.That distinction is not only a hippocampal story. It is the same logical move at several scales: memory content versus retrieval; a potential 𝑉 versus mobility or gating; a stored landscape versus a context-conditioned route; an attractor versus a committor; a dendritic substrate versus which branch is currently easy. Current hippocampal archives do not show landscape writing. The access-on-frozen-landscape question is a different question, and it remains a question.What the companion studies actually showedThese are constraints, not a validation of the chain.Spindles. In two overnight EEG datasets, a discrete spindle event coincides with a modest, timing-locked rise in one stratified chart coordinate (𝑑𝑛). That is event-locked state reconfiguration. It is not causal denoising, not a complete spindle-versus-matched-N2 mechanism, and not thalamic identification. Matched-control speci­ficity is incomplete (Langell, 2026b).Coma. In early EEG after cardiac arrest, short-horizon spread and local deformation carry recovery-linked information that static location does not exhaust. Geometry added a bounded increment on a feature-limited clinical-plus-qEEG baseline. Withdrawal of life-sustaining therapy and sedation were not available. Read as recovery-linked geometry, not as a viability assay and not as a consciousness meter (Langell, 2026c).Meso to macro. Simultaneous Neuropixels and surface EEG can covary under careful nulls. The same EEG-only chart does not recover a local flow or reachability operator. Contact across scales is real at association. It is not operator identification (Langell, 2026d).Other companions — depression, psychosis, gambling versus cognitive control, hypoglycaemia, infant age — mainly show that different conditions move different layers. That pattern is the result. Reduced spread is not itself reduced consciousness. Complexity scores can separate global state from reported experience within a state; NDT has not been shown to do better (Bajwa et al., 2025).What this is not•Not a bedside score, CPC substitute, or test of awareness.•Not Integrated Information Theory’s Φ, workspace ignition, or a report.•Not the claim that “noetic” means knowledge-bearing structure in the coordinates. The name marks the intended domain of mind-relevant dynamics.•Not a completed theory of why any of this should feel like anything.•Not a licence to treat every named object (committor, finite-amplitude resilience, dendritic access) as an empirical export. Several remain questions.If, after matching state, responsiveness, arousal, power, and complexity, the future law still failed to distinguish reported experience from no reported experience, NDT would remain a theory of enabling dynamics. That test is named in the main text.
