What is not the coma source

contrast in some ledgers), pooled tube_d_eff_median 𝑑= 0.638, and a predictive increment near AUROC

companion is the invalidity-fixed pooled synthesis of 20260606_combined_invalidityfix, which restored
301/281 and the AUROC pair 0.599 →0.662. Numbers in this article follow that companion, not the ComBat
intermediate.

Spindle timing versus specificity
This article uses the timing estimand (event versus far pre-event baseline) as the primary spindle constraint.
The event-versus-matched-N2 contrast is reported in the companion as incomplete: several endpoints are
baseline-dominated, and 𝑑𝑛 changes sign across datasets for that reason. Reachability was not computed. EAP
moderation of the geometry effect is rejected under the primary protocol. Peak-phase respiratory locking is a
separate RichSleep companion and is not a second theoretical anchor.

Dissociation matrix sources
Rows in the main-text dissociation table and Figure 5 are companion constraints, not additional anchors.
Canonical sources are the corresponding companion articles. Anesthesia and epilepsy rows in Figure 5 remain
unpaid (not tested), not hidden positives.
