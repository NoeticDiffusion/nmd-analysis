Context, history, or physiology can alter 𝐾ℎ(⋅|𝑥,𝛾) among observations matched on current chart state.3.Drift split and identifiabilityPotential-driven drift supplies one structured component, 𝑏=−𝑀∇𝑉+𝑢nc, with mobility 𝑀≻0 (and 𝑀=𝑔−1 if a metric 𝑔 is declared). NDT does not currently assume a fluctuation–dissipation relation linking 𝑀 to 𝑎. A residual non-conservative term is permitted because cyclic or asymmetric flow need not be gradient descent under the declared geometry. The split is not uniquely identified from trajectories without extra structure. An anisotropic or state-dependent 𝑀 can make a gradient flow look non-normal in Euclidean coordinates. Chart Jacobian non-symmetry is therefore not proof of a genuine 𝑢nc component. 𝑉 is the NDT potential, not IIT’s Φ.4.Coordinate dependenceMNPS is an operational chart. Away from a fixed point, 𝐽=𝜕𝑏/𝜕𝑥 is not generally an intrinsic tensor under nonlinear reparametrization. An Itô SDE acquires an Itô correction in the transformed drift. Consequently 𝛼, 𝜔, Ω, and local deformation summaries are chart-level measurements unless a connection is declared and a covariant derivative is used. NMD currently estimates the operational chart Jacobian. An intrinsic ∇𝑏 is not implemented and is not claimed.5.Stochastic short-horizon reachabilityThe ellipsoid constructed by propagating local covariance under a locally linear stochastic model is not classical control-theoretic reachability and not a Gramian of a structural connectome. Call it stochastic short-horizon reachability: a covariance-based approximation to finite-horizon predictive spread under 𝐾ℎ. Horizon, residual quality, and neighbourhood leakage are validity conditions. NMD does not emit this object without an admissible one-step residual.6.Committor and finite-amplitude resilienceThe committor 𝑞𝐴𝐵(𝑥) is the probability of hitting 𝐵 before 𝐴. It is destination geometry, not spread. Finite-sample 2-D recovery is not a current empirical export. FAR asks whether a finite kick can dislodge the state; it depends on perturbation family and amplitude and is not a local-linear summary. Neither object is licensed as an NMD v2 export in the present contract.7.What this supplement does not authorizeSuccess at recoverability Level 1 does not license ℒ︀ identification. Simulator envelopes for 𝑎, 𝛼, and 𝜔 are truth-known qualifications under restricted linear SDEs.
