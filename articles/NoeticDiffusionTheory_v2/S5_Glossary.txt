Where relevant, entries distinguish the theoretical object, the operational chart estimator, and what is not established.Reading guide. Object = theoretical quantity. Implemented = what a current contract actually computes. Not established = common over-reading.A – DAccessibility (dynamical / noetic). Object: the geometry of the family of protocol-conditional kernels {𝐾ℎ}ℎ>0 over future states at horizon ℎ. Path-dependent quantities (committors, routes) are defined under the corresponding process, not by a single endpoint kernel. Not workspace ignition, not report, not a consciousness scalar.Attractor / basin. Object: long-run organization of the transition law. Implemented: not recovered as unique wells on MNPS here. Visual clustering is not evidence of an attractor.Committor 𝑞. Object: probability of hitting 𝐵 before 𝐴. Implemented: conceptual in NMD; 2-D finite-sample recovery closed in companion simulation.Control 𝑐𝑡 / protocol 𝛾. Object: local second-moment geometry of stochastic spread, 𝑎=ΣΣ⊤. Implemented: truth-known envelopes in restricted linear SDEs; not a default empirical NMD export without residual gates.E – LEmbodied Anchoring Principle (EAP).
