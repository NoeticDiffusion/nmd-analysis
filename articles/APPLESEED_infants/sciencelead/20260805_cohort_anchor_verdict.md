The cohort anchor is internally validated: all 48 recordings share the same subject-balanced global IQR feature anchor and have cohort-anchored 3D coordinates. This permits descriptive comparisons of coordinate positions across the four age strata within this export.

Use 3D cohort-anchored dynamics as the main layer. Keep 9D Jacobian analysis secondary: two sessions have zero retained 9D windows and the balanced 4-to-8-month complete case is n=10. Do not impute this missing geometry.
