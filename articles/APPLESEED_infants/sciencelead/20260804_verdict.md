# Science-lead verdict: APPLESEED reliability and longitudinal geometry

## Verdict: method/pilot result; do not frame as developmental discovery

The export and wrappers are technically valid for the planned session-level analysis: all 48 H5 sessions passed the geometry contract, coverage matches the dataset description, and the analysis prevents epoch/window pseudo-replication.

However, the primary empirical result is negative/inconclusive. The nine endpoint test--retest ICC(A,1) estimates were low or negative with wide bootstrap intervals, and no longitudinal contrast survived its prespecified BH-FDR correction. The small sample (13 retest children; 11 longitudinal children), event imbalance, and subject anchoring materially constrain inference.

## Claims allowed
- The frozen export can be analyzed reproducibly at session level.
- In this example cohort and configuration, no primary endpoint had robust corrected evidence of a 4-to-12-month within-child change.
- Short-interval absolute-agreement estimates are imprecise and generally low for these session-median endpoints.

## Claims not allowed
- NDT geometry is developmentally invariant or intrinsically unreliable.
- Infants differ in absolute MNPS coordinate position.
- Any endpoint measures maturity, cognitive status, clinical condition, or a latent neural mechanism.

## Recommendation
Proceed as a transparent methods/pilot manuscript or repository case study, with figures/tables and all constraints retained. Do not submit it as confirmatory developmental neuroscience without a larger, independently designed replication.
