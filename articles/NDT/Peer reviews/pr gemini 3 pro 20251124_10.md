**Reviewer Report**

**Manuscript Title:** Noetic Diffusion Theory: A Rhythmic, Geometric Framework for Neural State Reconstruction and Conscious Correlates
**Date:** November 24, 2025
**Verdict:** **Major Revisions Required** (Borderline Reject due to commercial formatting)

### 1. General Assessment
The manuscript presents a highly ambitious theoretical framework (NDT) that integrates stochastic thermodynamics, information geometry, and sleep neurophysiology. The formalization of the Meta–Noetic Diffusion Model (MNDM) has improved in clarity, particularly with the new "Identifiability" section (Section 10.1).

However, the manuscript continues to suffer from a fundamental category error: **it reads simultaneously as a scientific theory and a product whitepaper.** The inclusion of trademark notices, licensing frameworks, and explicit "brand stewardship" rationales within the body of a scientific paper is unprecedented in academic literature and will likely trigger immediate desk rejection at any major journal (e.g., *Nature Neuroscience*, *PLOS Computational Biology*, or *NeuroImage*).

### 2. Major Critiques

**2.1. The "Trademark" and Stewardship Sections (Section 12)**
While the added "Rationale for Registration" attempts to explain the presence of trademarks (comparing to Linux/Arduino), this justification is misplaced in a scientific manuscript.
*   **Scientific Standard:** Academic papers describe natural laws, models, or empirical findings. They do not establish legal frameworks or brand identities. The presence of the "™" symbol throughout the text (e.g., "Noetic Diffusion Health Index™") suggests that the authors view these mathematical constructs as proprietary products rather than scientific contributions.
*   **Recommendation:** **Delete Section 12 entirely.** Remove all "™" symbols from the text. If you must discuss licensing, keep it strictly to the "Data and Code Availability" section and cite the external website/license text. Do not embed the legal rationale in the scientific argument.

**2.2. Identifiability and the "Multiverse" Guardrail (Section 10)**
The new section on identifiability (Lines 1929–1947) is a critical and welcome addition. However, the proposed "guardrails" are extremely demanding.
*   **Critique:** You propose requiring consistency across "≥80% of specifications" in a multiverse analysis. While rigorous, this is statistically hazardous. If your feature definitions ($E, R, D$) are highly correlated, you will pass; if they are independent/orthogonal, you will fail.
*   **Analysis:** The danger is that "robustness" becomes a filter that selects only for redundant features. The paper needs to demonstrate that the *core topology* (e.g., the "depression" collapse) survives even when using orthogonal definitions of entropy (e.g., Lempel-Ziv vs. Spectral).
*   **Recommendation:** Explicitly state that the multiverse grid must include *mechanistically distinct* feature implementations, not just parameter variations of the same feature.

**2.3. The "Discrete" vs. "Continuous" Tension**
There is a lingering tension between the *Minimal MNDM* (Eq. 1) and the *RVC* description.
*   **The Claim:** NDT predicts "step-wise" entropy drops (Section 9.1).
*   **The Model:** Eq. 1 uses $r(t)$, which can be continuous. The text states "biological implementation... creates discrete denoising epochs."
*   **Critique:** If the discreteness is *biologically imposed* (Line 261) rather than mathematically intrinsic to the SDE, then the "Minimal MNDM" simulation (Section 7) is essentially cheating if you *force* $r(t)$ to be a square wave in the simulation (Line 1774).
*   **Recommendation:** The simulation must show that *even with a noisy/smooth biological rhythm proxy* (like a real theta envelope), the non-linear gating (the exponential term in Eq. 13) *emerges* as a discrete step in the dynamics. If you simply hard-code a square wave in the simulation, you haven't explained the discreteness; you've assumed it.

### 3. Technical & Mathematical Issues

**3.1. The Laplacian Regularization (Eq. 9)**
*   **Equation:** $\frac{\partial F}{\partial t} = \eta \nabla^2 F$.
*   **Critique:** In high dimensions (the "noetic manifold"), the Laplacian $\nabla^2 F$ is computationally expensive and difficult to estimate robustly from data.
*   **Question:** How do you propose to estimate the Laplacian of the potential *empirically* to test this?
*   **Recommendation:** Clarify if this equation is purely a theoretical description of what the brain does, or if you claim this is measurable. If measurable, provide the estimation formula in the "Methods" section.

**3.2. Kappa Calibration (Line 943)**
*   **Critique:** You "calibrate $\kappa$ so that... modulations yield a ~2-3x change."
*   **Assessment:** This makes the model phenomenological rather than biophysical. That is acceptable, but you should acknowledge that $\kappa$ is a *free parameter* fit to the target outcome (variance change), not an independent prediction.

### 4. Specific Line-by-Line Comments

*   **Line 131:** *"discretely pulsed quality"* — This phenomenological claim is interesting but risky. Ensure you do not conflate "neural discreteness" (spindles) with "perceptual discreteness" (snapshots) without citing the "Psychological Refractory Period" or "Perceptual Cycles" literature (e.g., VanRullen & Koch).
*   **Line 187:** *"we can view the dynamics as evolving in the (E, R) subspace"* — This clarification (Lines 180–191) is very helpful in resolving the conflict between Eq 1 and Eq 2. Keep this.
*   **Line 1405:** *"Note on spatial scope"* — Good addition. Acknowledging the global approximation is necessary.
*   **Line 2003:** *"Rationale for Registration"* — As mentioned, this entire block is inappropriate for a peer-reviewed article.

### 5. Final Decision
**Status:** **Scientific content is strong; Packaging is fatal.**

The scientific core—the MNDM equations, the geometric interpretation of sleep, and the Noetic Atlas—is novel and rigorous. However, the manuscript cannot be accepted with the current "Trademark/Licensing" wrapper. It reads like a whitepaper for a crypto/tech startup, not a contribution to neuroscience.

**Required Actions for Resubmission:**
1.  **Strip all commercial language:** Remove Section 12, the Trademark Notice, and all "™" symbols.
2.  **Simulation Rigor:** In Section 7, demonstrate that discreteness emerges from the *gating function* (Eq 13) applied to a smooth rhythm, rather than hard-coding a square wave.
3.  **Falsifiability:** Retain the new Identifiability section; it is excellent.
