# Figshare infant EEG developmental analysis handover

- Input: `neuralmanifolddynamics_figshare_infant_eeg_20260805_091838`.
- Sample: 70 visits from 22 TD children; 20 children and 68 valid visit rows contributed to within-child age models.
- Contract: `subject_anchored` only. No cohort anchor, coordinate-position comparison, event windows, or stage labels.
- QC: all 70 geometry contracts were `ok`; 3D and 9D MNJs resolved for every visit.
- Primary result: no 3D endpoint had raw p below 0.292; reachability log-volume was the largest standardized slope (0.20, raw p=0.292).
- 9D sensitivity: lowest raw p=0.248 for trace; no supported secondary result.
- Direct-age sensitivity: log-volume standardized slope 0.38, raw p=0.107; it is not a supported finding.

The manuscript is deliberately framed as a bounded exploratory null result and as a reusable subject-anchored pipeline, not a direct replication or pooled extension of the APPLESEED cohort-anchored study.
