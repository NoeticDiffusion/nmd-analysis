# Science-lead verdict: Figshare infant developmental analysis

## Supported internal findings
- The exported subject-anchored 3D and 9D MNJ contracts resolve for all 70 sessions.
- The specified within-child age analysis includes 20 children and 68 valid visits.
- No primary 3D endpoint showed a nominal child-sign-permutation association with age (minimum raw p=0.292).
- No 9D sensitivity endpoint showed a nominal association (minimum raw p=0.248).

## Bounded interpretation
The current data do not support a claim of monotonic early-age change in the analysed subject-anchored MNJ/reachability summaries. This is not evidence of developmental invariance: sampling is irregular, confidence intervals are wide, and age metadata are partly derived.

## Prohibited claims
- Do not call subject-anchored coordinate values between-child state positions.
- Do not pool this cohort with ds003710 or claim a direct numerical replication of its cohort-anchored coordinate effects.
- Do not infer a neural mechanism, clinical norm, or Bayley association from this analysis.

## Next falsifiable step
Reanalyse an age-balanced external cohort with a frozen cohort-anchor policy and preregistered nonlinear age model; then test whether reachability log-volume changes replicate.
