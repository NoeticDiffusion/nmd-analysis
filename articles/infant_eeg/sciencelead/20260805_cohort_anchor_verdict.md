## Supported internal results
- The global subject-balanced cohort anchor is present and consistent across all exported sessions.
- Independent wrapper GEE models exactly reproduce the supplied 11-coordinate omnibus statistics and FDR values.
- All 12 primary cohort-coordinate age models are FDR-supported; the direct-age sensitivity supports 11/12.

## Interpretation boundary
The data support age association in an EEG-derived common coordinate chart. They do not establish a latent neural-state trajectory, a local-flow mechanism, a normative developmental reference, or causal maturation.

## Key methodological distinction
Cohort-coordinate GEE combines within- and between-child age information under child-clustered inference. The within-child MNJ/reachability analysis isolates a different, local-dynamics estimand. Their divergence is informative and must not be treated as contradiction.
