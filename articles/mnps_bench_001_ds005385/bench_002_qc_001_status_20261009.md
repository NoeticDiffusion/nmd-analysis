# BENCH-002-QC-001 — temporal and signal QC status

## Current evidence

### Source annotation availability

The six-participant source audit found 8,755 BIDS event rows, seven recording
boundary markers, and zero event rows with bad/artifact/gap/break-like labels.
The EEGLAB reject-mask arrays inspected for these recordings were empty. No
participant audio recording or EMG channel was supplied, and the exported task
events do not identify individual vocal onset or retention end.

This means source bad-interval annotations are absent or unavailable. It does
not show that the EEG is artifact-free. The source and task-timing findings are
recorded in outputs/v3_coverage_001_temporal_qc_20261009/results.md.

### Signal-based artifact screening

No signal-based window screening has yet been run. The coverage and temporal-QC
reports therefore cannot assign artifact-free status. A later audit should
report peak-to-peak amplitude, robust channel amplitude, flat-channel support,
and high-frequency power per window and per realization. These are QC
descriptors; no single indicator is a rejection or qualification rule here.

### Filter-edge support

The benchmark preprocessing resamples and applies 50 Hz notch plus 1–45 Hz
zero-phase FIR filtering to continuous EEG before extracting trial windows.
The exact composite edge-safe margin has not been quantified. PCV additionally
applies a window-local 8–13 Hz forward/backward IIR and FFT Hilbert transform;
its candidate-local edge fidelity remains unknown. The measurement metadata
records the declared window and missing edge evidence, but that is not proof of
edge safety.

## Decision

Keep the existing 648 load-13 20-second measurements unqualified. Computed,
finite values and empty source rejection masks do not establish clean-window
support. Report source annotation availability, signal indicators, upstream
filter support, and PCV-local filter support as separate fields in any later
export.

## Remaining work

Before interpreting clean-window sensitivity, run the frozen V3-MULTISCALE-
FIDELITY-002 signal-QC and PCV context comparison on the six-participant pilot.
Do not expand to the remaining cohort until those results and the analysis
contract have been reviewed.
