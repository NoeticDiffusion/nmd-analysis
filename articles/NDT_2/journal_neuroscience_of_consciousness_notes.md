# Neuroscience of Consciousness — submission briefing

Last checked: 2026-07-22  
Target journal: *Neuroscience of Consciousness* (Oxford University Press)

## Editorial fit

The journal is the official journal of the Association for the Scientific Study
of Consciousness and is fully open access. Its stated scope includes the
biological basis of consciousness and welcomes empirical, methodological,
theoretical, and philosophical contributions from neuroscience, cognitive
science, psychology, philosophy, computer science, and allied fields.

This NDT article has a plausible fit only if it foregrounds:

- a falsifiable account of neural dynamics rather than a metaphysical solution
  to consciousness;
- explicit observational and identifiability boundaries;
- a clear relevance to consciousness science, including altered states and
  disorders of consciousness;
- a transparent distinction between established evidence, companion empirical
  results, and speculative mechanisms.

The meso-to-macro structure is preferable to an unsupported macro-only theory:
it starts with candidate population-scale observables, documents the limits of
cross-scale association, and only then introduces MNPS and higher-order
geometric claims.

## Recommended article positioning

**Working type:** Research Article — theoretical framework.

Alternative framing should be considered before submission:

- **Research Article** if the final manuscript contains a substantive,
  reproducible empirical or simulation test with prespecified comparisons.
- **Review Article** if it becomes primarily an integrative synthesis of
  multiscale consciousness and neural-dynamics literature.
- **Brief Communication** only if reduced to one narrow empirical or
  methodological result.

Do not submit it as an empirical Research Article based solely on theory,
companion manuscripts, or unreplicated results. If no new empirical/simulation
result is added, ask the editorial office whether the intended article type is
appropriate.

## Manuscript preparation targets

Use the following as planning targets, not as final compliance rules:

- Draft a structured abstract of **no more than 250 words**.
- Keep the full manuscript within an approximately **10,000-word** planning
  ceiling unless the live author instructions specify otherwise.
- Use a title that says what is proposed and at what scale; avoid implying that
  NDT measures consciousness directly.
- Maintain a claim ledger in the prose: *established external result*,
  *companion/internal result*, *plausible interpretation*, and *speculative
  extension*.
- State data/code availability for every empirical companion result and
  separate public data from repository-internal analyses.
- Use complete, consistent references; verify the journal's required reference
  style before preparing the final submission package.
- Build figures that are legible without relying on colour alone and label
  conceptual figures as conceptual.

## NDT-specific editorial risks to address

1. **Category drift.** Do not let a diffusion-model analogy become a biological
   mechanism or a consciousness theory without direct evidence.
2. **Measurement overreach.** Cross-scale coupling supports association, not a
   common latent manifold, causal direction, or local dynamical operator.
3. **Clinical overstatement.** MNPS, reachability, and metabolic adequacy are
   research constructs, not individual diagnostic or consciousness scores.
4. **Unidentified mechanism.** The TRN mapping, metabolic geometry, and EAP
   equations require comparison with simpler arousal, autonomic, and covariate
   alternatives.
5. **Citation provenance.** Before submission, replace internal manuscript
   references with archived/preprint DOI citations or frame them as
   supplementary methodological material.

- Verify article type, word limits, abstract requirements, reference style,
  figure-file requirements, licence, APC, conflicts, ethics, and data/code
  policy from the live page.
- Confirm whether a theoretical framework without new empirical results is
  appropriate as a Research Article or requires editorial presubmission
  guidance.
- Prepare a cover letter stating the falsifiable contribution, multiscale
  relevance, public/reproducible materials, and explicit claim boundaries.
- Obtain citable, permanent records for the DANDI bridge analysis and
  truth-known simulator, or remove any dependence on their unpublished results.
