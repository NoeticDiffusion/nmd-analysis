## What exists and is usable

All 57 H5s contain 3D MNPS, 9D coordinates, 3D/9D MNJ, subject-anchored 3D/9D coordinates, raw features, robust-z features, and regional MNPS. The task metadata are exclusively `illusion_game`; conditions are empty.

This permits future **IG-only, window-level** feature–geometry analyses after their own QC and model specification. It does not establish synchronization or validate any inferential result.

## Missing export surfaces / blockers

Absent from every H5, as reported by the manifest and confirmed by direct inspection:

- `feature_anchors`, `anchor_state`, `anchor_quality`, and `anchor_coupling`;
- cohort-anchored coordinates;
- `extensions/time_reference`;
- labels/stage, events, and event-window joins;
- block-native windows and block Jacobians.

Thus the current export cannot audibly join geometry to illusion block/trial labels, behavior, or an external clock. It also cannot support formal anchor-state/quality analysis.
