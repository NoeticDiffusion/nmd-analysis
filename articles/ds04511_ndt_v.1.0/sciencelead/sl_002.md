# sl_002 — L4 QC and L2/L3 panel

## Verdict

Keep Rest / CC / GG as the article contrast. Treat occupancy, chart Jacobian,
one-step L2/L3, and L4 reachability as **non-equivalent** families. Do not fold
them into one “geometry” score.

## Green light for a paper branch

Conditional, unchanged. The new tables are exploratory. G3/G4 still open.

## What the second cut actually shows

**Internal validated (this export, complete-case):**

- L4 `a_q` is missing on 3/134 recordings. All three are `invalid` with
  `post_dtype_psd_failure` on gate F. One Rest, one CC, one GG, three
  subjects. Not a task-specific dropout.
- `reach_d_eff` is not a dimension contrast: 109/131 finite values sit within
  `1e-6` of 1, even though the max is 1.31. Wilcoxon on that readout is
  method-limited.
- Chart-Jacobian α is finite on 134/134. Lag-1 L2/L3 is identified on 121/134
  (history-conditioned 115/134). Lag-2 is absent.
- Chart-Jacobian α **rises** in GG vs Rest (`d_z = 1.42`, n=44) and GG vs CC
  (`d_z = 0.91`, n=44). One-step L3 α does **not** copy that sign: GG−Rest
  `d_z = -0.33`, n=32, BH q = 0.18. Chart J α is not one-step L3.
- On the identified-lag-1 subset, GG−Rest **lowers** L2 operator rotation
  (`d_z = -1.10`, n=32) and L3 generator rotation (`d_z = -1.08`). That is a
  different object from chart-Jacobian Frobenius / α in the first panel.

**Plausible:** GG changes local chart linearization and identified one-step
rotation in opposite directions relative to Rest. That is compatible with NDT’s
non-equivalence of J, one-step maps, and reachability, not proof of a unique
mechanism.

**Not established:** Ito identification, destination/FAR, consciousness, or a
confirmatory amplitude claim on `subject_anchored` charts.
