## Design (frozen before this run)

Addendum: `preregistration/addendum_20260921_matched_state_c3.md`.
Greedy 1–1 Euclidean match on `coords_3d_subject_anchored`. Caliper =
median baseline leave-one-out nearest neighbour. Outcomes are realized
one-step \(\|dx/dt\|\) and \(\operatorname{Tr}(\mathrm{Cov}(dx/dt))\), not
Itô \(b\)/\(a\). Holm is only those two matched tests. Unmatched and
random-match are controls. Spectral+MNPS matching is a sensitivity.

## Support (internal validated)

19/20 subjects had ≥20 pairs. Match fraction is typically 0.31–0.49.
sub-024 failed the pair gate (13). Spectral+MNPS matching reached the
gate in 6/20 and is not testable.

\(d_z=-0.43\), sign p = 0.012. Random match (same n, ignore \(x\)) spread

## Claim ledger

**Internal validated:** pair counts and the Holm-null matched primaries;
unmatched increment spread/speed are larger in magnitude than matched
ones on this grain.

**Plausible:** the earlier L1 recording-level drift/diffusion screen is
partly an occupancy shift in the local chart, not a different one-step
law at matched \(x\).

**Rejected here:** treating this run as NDT-C3; calling propofol a
generalized Jacobian/stability collapse; lowering the L2/L3 n-gate;
reinterpreting the 132-test BH as confirmatory.

**Not established:** same physical neural state across phases (local
fits; no documented baseline re-anchor); causal drug effect;
consciousness; identified Itô operators.

## What this does to the paper

The interesting Apollo signal remains a **selective L1 occupancy-level
drift–diffusion change with weak Jacobian-α**. It is **not** currently a
matched-state future-law result. A paper that leads with C3 on this
export would overclaim.
