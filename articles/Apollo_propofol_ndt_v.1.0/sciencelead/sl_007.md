# sl_007 — Occupancy × LOR/ROR; three Results figures

`sl_006` asked for a behaviour-anchored LOR/ROR branch on the frozen
occupancy scalar, and for Results to be structured around three occupancy
figures. Occupancy is not recomputed. L1, Jacobian α, and C3 matching are
not reopened. Protocol phase 3 is not LOR. G3/G4 are not locked.

Addendum: `preregistration/addendum_20260921_occupancy_lor.md`.

## Occupancy-blind labels (internal validated)

Exact binomial 95% Clopper–Pearson intervals on `n_correct_responses` / 40,
later protocol sedation versus baseline. A subject is LOR-impaired if the
moderate interval lies entirely below baseline.

| Split | n |
| --- | ---: |
| LOR-impaired | 7 |
| responsive | 13 |
| missing | 0 |

This matches Chennu et al. 2016 (7 drowsy, 13 responsive). All 20 subjects
are in later protocol sedation; only 7 meet the behavioural rule. Package
`unresponsive` would have labelled 20/20. Among the 7 LOR-impaired subjects,
recovery intervals all overlap baseline (**ROR returned 7 / not returned 0**),
so occupancy cannot be compared between returned and not-returned.

## Primary (exploratory; NOT_TESTABLE)

Occupancy Δ (later sedation − baseline), LOR-impaired versus responsive.
Prediction: LOR-impaired larger Δ. Gate ≥10 per arm.

| med LOR | med resp | Δ med | d | MW p | status |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 5.13 | 7.34 | −2.21 | **+0.026** | 0.76 | NOT_TESTABLE |

The n=7 arm keeps the group contrast below the gate. The point estimate
does not follow the prediction: later-sedation occupancy relocation is not
larger in the LOR group. Cohen's d is near zero because a heavy tail pulls
the means together.

## Testable association (n=20, still exploratory)

Spearman occupancy versus hit count at later protocol sedation:
ρ = −0.08, p = 0.73. Occupancy versus measured concentration at the same
phase: ρ = +0.28, p = 0.22.

Qualitative dissociation: sub-018 occupancy 28.6 with 39/40 correct
(responsive); sub-007 occupancy 26.4 with 6/40 (LOR-impaired). Large
relocation occurs with and without behavioural impairment.

protocol-phase relocation more than behavioural loss of responsiveness.
