Local-chart C3 (`sl_002`) stays as a negative matched-state result on
exported MNPS. The 132-test panel stays discovery. L2/L3 stay unsupported.
Protocol phase 3 is not unresponsiveness. G3/G4 `b`/`a` lock is still not
executed.

`sl_002` asked for one next gate: a **baseline-derived within-subject
reference**, then the same matching rule.

## Support (internal validated)

7/20 subjects had ≥20 matched pairs. Several subjects have 0–4 pairs
(sub-005, sub-006: 0). Match fractions are typically 0.00–0.33 versus
0.31–0.49 on the local chart. The matched primary is **not testable**
(n=7 < 10).

Matched occupancy QC on those 7: 2.11 vs 2.16, d_z = 0.25 — when pairs
exist, the paired states sit at similar radius.

## Claim ledger

**Internal validated:** pair counts; occupancy increase at first sedation
in the baseline-z spectral frame; insufficient common support for the
matched increment test.

**Plausible:** the local-chart L1 drift/diffusion decrement is at least
partly a recording-local standardisation / occupancy effect. In a
baseline-fixed spectral frame the dominant change is **where** the state
sits, not a smaller one-step increment law at matched \(z\). Recovery
has not returned to the awake neighbourhood on this occupancy scalar.

**Rejected here:** treating local-chart C3 as a common physical state;
claiming the local-chart `b`/`a` effect survives a baseline reference;
claiming a testable matched-state C3 result in this frame; lowering the
n=10 subject gate.

**Not established:** NDT-C3; confirmatory H1/H2; consciousness; Itô
operators; that recovery occupancy remaining high is incomplete
pharmacological reversal rather than lingering spectral change.

## What this does to the paper

The clean Apollo story is now **occupancy first**. Propofol moves the
person in a baseline-referenced spectral chart. Same-state different
futures are currently untestable because overlap is too small. A
confirmatory lock of recording-level `chart_drift_b_hat_norm` and
`diffusion_total` on the exported local chart would need this caveat in
the same paragraph, not as a footnote.
