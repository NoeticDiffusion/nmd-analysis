# Manuscript outline: dynamical-family extension

## Framing

Present the work as a measurement-contract and reproducibility extension of
the sleep development study. State that MNPS is the chart and that family
objects retain distinct grain and qualification status.

## Methods

Describe the frozen local-Jacobian metrics and optional recording-level FTR, the
canonical 3D raw-increment diffusion family, and the distinct local-J 3D/9D layers. Identify
window-native series versus recording-parent summaries. Window-native alpha/
omega and diffusion query values may be grouped by center-window label as
descriptive associations under the frozen policy; that does not make the
neighbor covariance or estimator stage-intrinsic. Explain that the
two-recording comparison is descriptive and participant-based.

Describe DF-DRIFT-C1 as a separate analysis gate: synthetic truth-known `b`,
cross-fit candidate, contaminated comparator, shuffled/time-displaced control,
embargo and overlap audit. Do not describe a chart velocity as latent Ito drift.

## Results structure

1. Family availability and native grain table for each recording.
2. Fixed-order local-Jacobian center-label summaries in 3D/9D; FTR deferred.
3. Fixed-order diffusion support, validity, and raw covariance summaries.
4. Existing M3 engineering benchmark evidence and a separately specified candidate feasibility experiment, without scientific qualification.

Keep Q/W_Q, committor and FAR outside the initial E1/E2 endpoints. Labels describe centers/queries only; no stage-conditioned estimator or purity claim.

## Discussion limits

Separate reproducibility of computed chart measurements from biological or NDT
interpretation. Explain that global state-space neighborhoods and recording
parent grain prevent stage-intrinsic conclusions. Treat the `m/e` fallback and
mixed feature provenance as visible scope limitations. No population inference
is made from two recordings.
